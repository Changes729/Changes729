# STM32

## 开发框架

- [Arduino](https://github.com/stm32duino/Arduino_Core_STM32)：依赖 STM32Cube
  - [maple](https://github.com/rogerclarkmelbourne/Arduino_STM32)：似乎已经停止维护
- [CMSIS](https://github.com/ARM-software/CMSIS_5)：官方推荐升级到 [CMSIS_6](https://github.com/ARM-software/CMSIS_6)
- [libopencm3](https://github.com/libopencm3/libopencm3)：[libopencm3-examples](https://github.com/libopencm3/libopencm3-examples) 好像也有点要停止维护的意思
- STM32Cube：包含了自家的 HAL、LL 库以及 CMSIS
  - [STM32CubeF1](https://github.com/STMicroelectronics/STM32CubeF1)
