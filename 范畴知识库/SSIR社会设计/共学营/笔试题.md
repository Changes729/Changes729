# Bank You

# Bank You 金融支持项目

BanQu is the world’s first non-cryptocurrency blockchain app to help the world’s poorest people establish a verifiable economic identity.

BanQu 是全球第一个非加密货币型区块链应用，这个应用帮助世界上最贫困的人建立可核实的经济身份。



*By Sarah Murray [Summer 2020](https://ssir.org/issue/summer_2020)* 

*作者： 莎拉·默里 2020年夏*



![image-20250214152438740](/home/asuki/.config/Typora/typora-user-images/image-20250214152438740.png)

*Ugandan barley farmers learn how to use the BanQu blockchain app on mobile phones. (Photo courtesy of Ab Inbev/BanQu)* 

*乌干达大麦种植者学习如何在手机上使用BanQu区块链应用。（图片来源：Ab Inbev/BanQu）*

 

On a trip to Zambia in 2018, Katie Hoard, Anheuser-Busch InBev’s (AB InBev’s) global director of agricultural innovation, remembers watching a smallholder farmer named Agnes receive a series of text messages: a receipt for her cassava sale, confirmation of payment to her mobile money account, and notification that her solar power bill could be paid from the funds now in her mobile money wallet.

在2018年的赞比亚之行中，百威英博（AB InBev）的全球农业创新总监凯蒂·霍德（Katie Hoard）注意到一位名叫艾格尼丝（Agnes）的小农收到的一系列短信：一份她木薯销售的收据、向她移动钱包账户付款的确认信息，以及通知她太阳能电费可以从她的移动钱包中支付的消息。



Hoard was in Zambia for the rollout of the next phase of a technology platform that allows AB InBev, the world’s largest brewer, to trace its purchases, help its farmers gain control over their finances, and ensure that the traders from which it buys commodities are paying farmers the right amount.

Hoard在赞比亚推出技术平台的下一阶段，就是允许 AB InBev （全球最大的啤酒制造商）平台跟踪货物情况，以帮助农民掌控其财务，并确保购买商品的商户向农民支付了正确的金额。



Agnes’s sale was the first it had used to test the new system. With a simple mobile phone, she not only logged proof of her sales via blockchain—technology that makes data immutable, verifiable, and immediately available to anyone in a network—but also started to build the business track record needed to win supply contracts and secure loans.

艾格尼丝的销售是她第一个用来测试新系统的。仅通过一部手机，她不仅通过区块链记录了她的销售证明，还开始构建那些为取得供销合同和有担保贷款所需要的业务记录。区块链这种技术使数据不可变，可核实，并且对网络中的任何人都即时可用。



“It was a really cool thing to see that it’s a broader part of the digital ecosystem we’re trying to build—and in the very first transaction we did, we saw proof of that,” Hoard says.

Hoard说：“它是我们正在努力建立数字生态的一个更广阔的部分，它真是一件很酷的事——在我们完成第一笔交易的时候，就对此坚信不疑。”



AB InBev’s technology partner is [BanQu](https://banqu.co/), a blockchain software company seeking solutions to extreme poverty. It is doing this in two ways: empowering the world’s poorest people by giving them a verifiable economic identity; and selling a software service to global brands looking to cut supply chain costs, reduce post-harvest loss, and improve their social and environmental performance at a time when more consumers want to buy green and ethical products.

百威英博的技术合作伙伴 —— [BanQu](https://banqu.co/)是一家为极端贫困寻求解决方案的区块链软件公司。它通过两种方式做到这一点：通过提供可核实的经济身份来为世上最贫穷的人赋能；以及向全球品牌出售软件服务，以降低供应链成本，减少收获后损失，以至于当越来越多消费者希望购买绿色和符合道德要求的产品的时候改善其社会和环境表现。



Early in its history, BanQu received grant funding from the Rockefeller Foundation. However, BanQu was established as a for-profit company. Its founder and CEO, Ashish Gadnis, argues that to achieve social progress at scale, BanQu can do far more as a for-profit company.

在初期，BanQu 接受了洛克菲勒基金会的资助。然而，BanQu 设立为一家营利性公司。其创始人兼首席执行官 Ashish Gadnis 认为，为了实现大规模的社会进步，BanQu 作为一家营利性公司可以做得更多。



“For me, it’s easier to walk into a CEO’s office and say, ‘I can make 15 percent more on your supply chain and drive your category sales on the consumer side—and by the way, you’ll do some good in the world,’” he says. “It also allows me to not be the bleeding heart in the room. I don’t need anybody’s pity, because that mother in Congo doesn’t need pity. She needs business.”

他说：“对我来说商业沟通会更容易，我可以直接走进CEO的办公室然后对他说， ‘我可以在你的供应链上多赚15%，并在消费者方面推动你的品类销售，顺便说一句，你会在世界上做一些好事。’这也让我不会在房间里独自心碎。我不需要任何人的怜悯，因为那个在刚果的母亲不需要怜悯。她需要生意。”



### **Blockchain’s Advantages**

### **Blockchain的优势**



*It was his experience in the development sector that prompted Gadnis—who grew up in a poor community in Mumbai, India—to create BanQu in December 2015.*

在印度孟买的一个贫困社区长大的Gadnis，他在发展部门的经历促使他在2015年12月创建了 BanQu。



Three years prior, in 2012, he ended a successful career as a technology entrepreneur, sold his last start-up to a global consulting firm, and joined USAID’s volunteer CEO program in the Democratic Republic of Congo, where he worked for two years.

三年前的2012年，他结束了那有所成就的科技企业家生涯，将最后一家初创企业出售给了一家全球咨询公司，并加入了美国国际开发署在刚果民主共和国的志愿者首席执行官计划，并在那里工作了两年。



While there, Gadnis overheard a conversation between a female farmer and a local bank manager. “She wanted to open a bank account, but the local bank refused because she couldn’t prove her harvest—she couldn’t prove her existence in the supply chain,” he explains. The manager then turned to Gadnis. “The guy said, ‘I can’t bank her. But I’ll bank you.’”

在那里，Gadnis无意中听到了一位女农民和当地银行经理之间的对话。他回忆到：“她想开一个银行账户，但当地银行拒绝了，因为她无法证明自己的收成--她无法证明自己在供应链中的存在。” 然后那位经理转向了 Gadnis 并说：“我不能把钱存到她身上，但我可以把钱存到你身上。”



This got him thinking about what prevented so many people from escaping poverty. “There are billions of people working in global supply chains producing coffee, cacao, or maize, or making your jeans and shoes,” he says, “yet they are completely invisible.”

这让 Gadnis 意识到到底是什么阻止了这么多人摆脱贫困，他说：“有数十亿人在全球供应链中工作，生产咖啡，可可或玉米，或制作牛仔裤和鞋子，但他们完全看不见。”



In 2016, Gadnis and his cofounders, Hamse Warfe and Jeff Keiser, started developing technology that would provide smallholder farmers supplying global buyers with transaction records, enabling them to open bank accounts and access credit and other financial services. The phrase “bank you” became BanQu.

2016年，Gadnis 和他的联合创始人 Hamse Warfe 和 Jeff Keiser 开始研发技术——为全球买家提供交易记录，使他们能够开设银行账户，获得信贷和其他金融服务。“Bank You”一词也变成了 BankQu。



Gadnis is not alone in making the link between financial exclusion and poverty. From nonprofits and microfinance institutions to development banks, plenty of time and dollars have been directed toward providing the roughly 1.7 billion unbanked adults in the world with access to financial services.

Gadnis 并不是唯一一个将金融排斥与贫困联系起来的人。从非营利组织和小额信贷机构到开发银行，大量的时间和资金都被用于为世界上大约17亿没有银行账户的成年人提供金融服务。



Nor is BanQu the only company using blockchain to increase the transparency of global supply chains. For example, Germany-based Minespider’s open, interoperable blockchain protocol tracks shipments of raw materials to ensure that they come from mines that are free from child labor, corruption, human rights abuses, and environmental degradation.

BanQu 也不是唯一一家使用区块链来提高全球供应链透明度的公司。例如，总部位于德国的 BieMinder，他们用开放、可互操作的区块链协议跟踪原材料的运输，以确保材料来自免受童工、腐败、侵犯人权以及环境退化的矿山。



“Whatever the claim is—whether it’s fish not produced by slaves or a garment produced in a factory that’s paying a living wage—blockchain use in the supply chain is all about this expanded ability to have traceability and transparency,” says Jim Fruchterman, founder of Tech Matters, which helps social sector organizations use technology to solve problems.

“无论宣传的是什么——无论是那些是否由奴隶生产的鱼，还是由那些支付工资的工厂所生产的服装——在供应链中使用区块链都是增强了这种可追溯性与透明度。” Jim Fruchterman 说（Tech Matters的创始人，Tech Matters 帮助社会部门组织使用技术解决问题）



Other technologies play a role, too. OpenSC, launched by the World Wildlife Fund and BCG Digital Ventures, the investment and incubation arm of Boston Consulting Group, uses technologies such as sensors and machine learning to help companies and consumers to verify whether a product has been sustainably and ethically produced.

其他技术也发挥了作用。OpenSC 由世界自然基金会和波士顿咨询集团的投资和孵化部门合作建立，其使用传感器与机器学习等技术来帮助公司和消费者验证产品是否以道德且可持续的方式生产。



“Technology can also help smallholders gain a bigger share of the profits from their products,” says Ed Marcum, vice president of investments at Humanity United, which has invested in OpenSC through the Working Capital venture fund it launched in 2018.

“技术还可以帮助小农从他们的产品中获得更大份额的利润，” Humanity United 的投资副总裁艾德·马库姆说（Humanity United 在2018年推出的营运资本风险基金投资了 OpenSC）



For Gadnis, the first priority was to solve the lack of a system of identity for poor farmers and other suppliers. “And it wasn’t an identity problem—it was an inability to prove your transaction identity,” he says.

对 Gadnis 来说，首要任务是解决贫困农民和其他供应商缺乏身份识别系统的问题。他说：“这不是身份问题，而是无法证明你的交易身份。”



The power of blockchain lies not only in its ability to create immutable data but also in the fact that—unlike with a database—everyone in a supply chain shares that data.

区块链的强大之处不仅在于它能够创建不可变的数据，而且还在于供应链中的每个人都共享这些数据，这与数据库不同。



“A database is always owned,” says Hoard. “So, if we shift growing regions and, for whatever reason, we’re no longer in that supply chain, the database goes with us. But the blockchain remains, and the farmer will always have ownership of that data.”

“数据库总是需要持有的，”Hoard说。“所以，如果我们无论出于什么原因而转移了种植区域，我们就不再处于供应链中，数据库也会随之消失。但区块链仍然存在，农民将永远拥有这些数据的所有权。“



Providing smallholders with an economic identity is not the only application of the BanQu platform. BanQu’s customers include Japan Tobacco International (JTI), which is using blockchain to track the eradication of child labor in its supply chain.

为小农提供经济身份并不是 BanQu 平台的唯一应用。BanQu的客户包括日本烟草国际（JTI），该公司正在使用区块链跟踪其供应链中消除童工的情况。



The challenge for JTI was to link the impact of its ARISE (Achieving Reduction of Child Labour in Support of Education) program to the farmers from which it sources tobacco leaf. ARISE educates communities about the need to end child labor, helps families acquire the skills needed to find alternative ways of earning money, offers resources to schools and training to teachers, and helps inform governments in the design and enforcement of relevant laws.

JTI面临的挑战是将其实现减少童工以支持教育计划（ARISE）的影响与其烟叶来源的农民联系起来。ARISE教育社区深知终止童工的必要性，帮助家庭获得寻找其他赚钱方式所需的技能，为学校提供资源，为教师提供培训，并帮助政府制定和执行相关法律。



Through the program, JTI has removed about 51,000 children from child labor. But it wanted to know how many of those were from families in its supply chain. With half of its tobacco sourced from countries such as Tanzania, Malawi, and Brazil, obtaining documentation such as birth certificates was a challenge. Blockchain offered a solution.

通过该计划，JTI已使约5.1万名儿童摆脱童工。但他们想知道其中有多少来自其供应链中的家庭。由于一半的烟草来自坦桑尼亚、马拉维和巴西等国家，获得出生证明等文件是一项挑战。区块链提供了一个解决方案。



“With BanQu, we have designed the first fully digitized system that can track where a child is vulnerable to child labor in our tobacco- growing population and when that child is receiving a service from ARISE,” explains Elaine McKay, social programs director at JTI.

“通过BanQu，我们设计了第一个完全数字化的系统，可以跟踪在我们烟草增长的人口中，儿童在哪里容易成为童工，以及该儿童何时接受ARISE的服务，”JTI社会项目总监Elaine McKay解释说。



“We document that in blockchain, and ARISE will tell us what services a child is being offered and if the child has been in school in the past 10 days,” she says. “And the fact that the system is immutable means we have the security of knowing that no supplier is trying to change the reality on the ground.”

她说：“我们在区块链中记录了这一点，ARISE将告诉我们为孩子提供了什么服务，以及孩子在过去10天内是否在学校。“这个系统是不可变的，这意味着我们可以放心地知道，没有供应商试图改变当地的现实。”



### Business for Social Change 

### 企业促进社会变革



While efforts to reduce poverty and address human rights abuses have traditionally been led by foundations, nonprofits, and international development institutions, Gadnis believes that private sector supply chains offer the scale and speed needed to reach millions of poor and disenfranchised people.

虽然减少贫困和解决侵犯人权问题的努力传统上是由基金会、非营利组织和国际发展机构领导的，但 Gadnis 认为，私营部门的供应链提供了覆盖数百万贫困和被剥夺权利的人所需的规模和速度。



“[Nonprofits are] doing amazing work. It’s just that they can’t make social change fast enough,” says Matt Swenson, chief product officer for Chameleon Cold-Brew, a values-led coffee company that recently signed a contract to work with BanQu in Guatemala.

“[非营利组织]正在做惊人的工作。这只是因为他们不能足够快地实现社会变革，”变色龙冷酿咖啡公司的首席产品官 Matt Swenson 说。变色龙冷酿咖啡公司是一家以价值为导向的咖啡公司，最近与危地马拉的班曲公司签订了一份合同。



Moreover, as consumer demand for sustainable products increases, the business case for acting responsibly is becoming more compelling. “You’re seeing some shift in consumers already,” says Swenson. “They’re voting with their dollars.”

此外，随着消费者对可持续产品的需求增加，采取负责任行动的商业理由也越来越有说服力。“你已经看到消费者的一些转变，”斯文森说。“他们在用钱投票。”



Using business to help solve big global problems is an idea to which Gadnis is firmly wedded. And his experience as a tech entrepreneur has informed the way he has developed the BanQu revenue model, which is similar to that of software-as-a-service companies such as Salesforce, SAP, and Oracle.

利用商业来帮助解决重大的全球性问题是Gadnis坚定的想法。他作为科技企业家的经历为他开发BanQu收入模式的方式提供了信息，该模式类似于Salesforce、SAP和Oracle等软件即服务公司的模式。



“We sell an annual license fee for subscription to the brand,” he explains. “And the brand pays us for the connection points in their supply chain, either on the sourcing side or on the distribution side.”

他解释说：“我们向品牌出售订阅年费。品牌为他们供应链中的连接点向我们付费，无论是在采购端还是在分销端。”



The company has two measurement metrics: annual recurring revenue (ARR) and the number of people in the “last mile”—the smallholder farmers, miners, and other workers at the end of global supply chains—that are connected to its technology. Its goal is to reach the 100 million mark for both by 2023.

该公司有两个衡量指标：年度经常性收入（ARR）和与其技术相关的“最后一英里”的人数——小农、矿工和全球供应链末端的其他工人。其目标是到2023年达到1亿大关。



While ambitious, BanQu’s current trajectory suggests it might not be impossible. By the end of 2017, a year after launching, the company had $100,000 in ARR and 3,000 people connected in the last mile. In 2018, this rose to $706,000 with 145,000 people connected. And by 2020, with about 500,000 people connected, Gadnis was predicting revenues of $3 million to $4 million.

虽然目标远大，但BanQu目前的发展轨迹表明，这可能并非不可能。截至2017年底，在推出一年后，该公司在该地区拥有10万的年度经常性收入和3000人数产生链接。到了2018年，这一数字上升到70.6万的年度经常性收入与14.5万人数链接。到2020年，大约有50万人链接，Gadnis预测收入将达到到400万。



Moreover, mainstream investors are attracted to the BanQu model. “We’ve closed three rounds of funding and raised $4.5 million in the past three years,” says Gadnis. “And it’s been pretty much institutional and private investors who believe that return on investment with purpose is the way to go.”

此外，主流投资者被吸引到 BanQu 模式。“在过去的三年里，我们已经完成了三轮融资，筹集了450万美元，”Gadnis说。“几乎所有的机构和私人投资者都认为，有目的的投资回报才是正确的选择。”



In fact, Gadnis is unequivocal in his belief that harnessing the power of the private sector can accelerate social change. “I want to set a new trend, honestly,” he says. “I want to be a $100 million company; that is, a for-profit company that has enabled 100 million people to get out of poverty—that’s never been done before.”

事实上，Gadnis 坚信利用私营部门的力量可以加速社会变革。“老实说，我想树立一种新的潮流，我想成为一家价值1亿美元的公司；也就是说，一家营利性公司，使1亿人摆脱贫困——这是以前从未做过的。”

# 创新五问

### 一、你是谁？有什么样的价值观和信仰？什么是你生活中最重要的事情？你热爱什么东西？

**01.1 我是谁？**

2025年我刚成为一位独立开发者，专业是嵌入式领域，专业方向主攻创意产品，交互设计和数字化。

独立之前的我分别在一家互联网公司做软件开发，一家广告策展公司做嵌入式开发。其间有一段时间去考研了，目标是浙江大学的创新设计，也算是懂技术也懂设计的人。



我以前一直是一位比较神经质的人，因为做技术也不常与人沟通，但是学设计的适合了解到设计成果的输出需要让别人理解，也算一定程度上改善了自己的社交能力。

因为下面很多问题，我觉得要完整回答还是需要解释一些前提，所以这里啰嗦两句。

我觉得「我」已经不单只「自我」了，我可以是其他人。我只是因为细胞而构成的个体，以及因生活汲取了不同的人生经验而有独立特征的个体。我觉得人都是一样的，只是个人的经验，生活环境等因素变化而使我们产生了差异。所以如果能理解人的共同，也能把握人的差异，那么所有人都一样。我也可以是任何人。

不过如果不整抽象，谈具体的话，我就是最上面回答的内容。



**01.2 价值观与信仰**

因为我觉得人都是一样的，所以我相信所有人需要在有限的时间和空间内寻找到自己合适的位置，并注意到合适的位置不是一成不变的，要时刻做好前往下一个位置的准备。就像电子运动，总是会在空穴中找到自己的位置，而又因为晶格变动而需要找到下一个空穴。

我的价值观，用人话说，就是自己活的舒适，也能对他人有所帮助。



**01.3 生活中最重要的事情**

因为我们要时刻准备寻找并适应新的环境，所以我觉得生活中最重要的事情就是认识环境，适应环境。而这个过程其实就是个人成长的过程。

 

**01.4 热爱的东西**

我觉得探究事情的根本很有意思。宇宙很有意思，微观的量子世界很有意思。所以，emmm？我热爱生活？可是也有很多事情我也没那么感兴趣。



### 二、在你看来，什么是社会创新？它对于社会的价值和意义是什么？ 

**02.1 什么是社会创新？**

社会创新的议题，气候问题、动物保护、可持续能源、教育、乡村振兴、代际友好、社会公平、公共健康等等议题。

公益的解释是，在做的事情是对整个社会有好处的，就可以算是社会公益。社会创新是在公益的基础上，通过实践产生实际的社会变革。所以我认为社会创新的本质是社会实践。

因为我认为人与人之前没什么区别。所以设身处地的想一想，如果其他人悲惨的生活是普遍的、结构化的，那么是不是社会本身存在一定的问题？从这个角度上来说，所谓的社会创新就是社会上的人们在意识到社会生活的困境，面向这些困境而采取的一系列行动。



**02.2 社会创新对于社会的价值和意义？**

因为我把社会创新定义为面向社会困境的一种行动，而人的行动首先需要对世界和自我进行认识与理解。对困境的科学认识就已经具有一定的社会价值了。那么在认识的基础上进一步的采取科学的行动，去实践去验证去试错，最终人类社会就可以攻克这个社会难题，从而让全社会得到发展，变得有益于社会中的每一个个体。
