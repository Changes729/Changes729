# The Hidden Dimensions of Scale

# 扩展规模的隐藏维度

Examples from South Asia show there’s more to successful scaling than replicating a good model.

南亚的例子表明，成功的规模化比复制一个好的模式要多。



By [Maria A. May & Amanda J. Misiti](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/the_hidden_dimensions_of_scale#bio-footer) Oct. 2, 2013

作者：玛丽亚·A.·梅和阿曼达·J.·米西提 2013 年 10 月 2 日



When assessing pilots, people often talk about a program or organization’s potential to scale. Certainly there are factors that make some models easier to scale than others. At BRAC, the world’s largest nonprofit organization, there is a relentless focus on making models that are not only effective, but also efficient and scalable. But our current research initiative, called the [Doing While Learning: Collaborative Models for Scaling Innovation](http://www.brac.net/content/doing-while-learning-collaborative-models-scaling-innovation#.UknL1L--2so) project, is revealing the importance of factors beyond the model, such as organizational capabilities and social capital in the pilot’s environment. To achieve scale, organizations must do more than just replicate; they need to figure out how to survive both financially and politically. Even if the goal of the organization isn’t to scale a specific program, but to scale through leveraging champions and building partnerships, it must take action early on.

在评估试点项目时，人们经常会谈论一个项目或组织规模化的潜力。当然，有些模式会比较容易规模化。孟加拉乡村发展委员会（BRAC）是世界上最大的非营利组织，它一直致力于打造不仅有效，而且高效、可规模化的模式。但我们目前的研究—— 「边做边学：扩大创新规模的协作模式」项目，揭示了模式以外的因素的重要性，如试点环境中的组织能力和社会资本。要实现规模化，组织必须做的不仅仅是复制，还需要想办法在财政和政治上生存下去。即使组织的目标不是扩大某个具体项目的规模，而是通过利用拥护者和建立合作伙伴关系来扩大规模，也必须尽早采取行动。



Recently we researched four programs that scaled up in South Asia to gain a deeper understanding of what successful support strategies might include. All of the programs shared a vision of large-scale and sustained change; worked with and through a variety of stakeholders; and, interestingly, were motivated by feeling as if they were part of something “bigger than themselves.” Linking local efforts to a broader national or global goal was inspiring and attracted more attention from policymakers and donors. We developed a [case study](http://www.brac.net/content/doing-while-learning-case-studies) for each program to capture the organization’s experience and insight from its leadership.

最近，我们对南亚的四个扩大规模的项目进行了研究，以深入了解成功的支持战略可能包括哪些内容。所有这些项目都有一个共同的愿景，那就是实现大规模、持续性的变革；与各种利益相关者合作，并通过他们开展工作；有趣的是，他们受到激励，感觉自己仿佛是某种“比自身更宏大事物”的一部分。将地方努力与更广泛的国家或全球目标相联系，令人振奋，也吸引了政策制定者和捐赠者的更多关注。我们为每项计划编写了一份案例研究，以记录该组织的经验及其领导层的见解。



One case study examines [BRAC’s Educational Support Program](http://blog.brac.net/2013/08/replicating-bracs-model-via-partnerships/). Instead of focusing on BRAC’s own schools, which have graduated more than [5 million poor children](http://opinionator.blogs.nytimes.com/2013/05/08/where-private-school-is-not-a-privilege/?_r=1) in Bangladesh, we looked at how, via external partnerships, BRAC enables other local partners to implement its effective, non-formal school model in 4,700 schools. Independent organizations that otherwise might lack the know-how or resources for management capacity, monitoring, training, and other types of support benefit from BRAC’s infrastructure, and gain inspiration and visibility from association with a well-known, respected organization.

其中一个案例研究考察了 BRAC 的教育支持计划。 BRAC 自己的学校已使孟加拉国 500 多万贫困儿童毕业，但我们并没有将重点放在这些学校上，而是研究了 BRAC 如何通过外部合作伙伴关系，使其他当地合作伙伴能够在 4,700 所学校中实施其有效的非正规学校模式。一些独立的组织可能缺乏管理能力、监督、培训和其他类型支持方面的知识或资源，但 BRAC 的基础设施使这些组织受益匪浅，同时，与一个知名的、受人尊敬的组织合作，也使这些组织获得了灵感和知名度。



Another describes how BRAC’s [Targeting the Ultra Poor](http://www.brac.net/sites/default/files/Tackling extreme poverty.pdf) model, proven to “graduate” people out of extreme poverty, was replicated in 10 new locations, via a learning consortium. The [Consultative Group to Assist the Poor](http://www.cgap.org/) (CGAP) and the [Ford Foundation](http://www.fordfoundation.org/) connected participating organizations with donors and prestigious researchers such as Esther Duflo. With many organizations sharing a common mission, it was easier to attract the attention of policymakers, media, and academics.

另一篇文章介绍了 BRAC 的 “瞄准赤贫者 ”模式是如何通过一个学习联盟在 10 个新地区进行推广的。援助贫穷者协商小组（CGAP）和福特基金会将参与组织、捐助方以及埃丝特-杜夫洛等著名研究人员联系起来。由于许多组织有共同的使命，因此更容易吸引决策者、媒体和学术界的关注。



The third case study describes Pakistan’s [Rural Support Program Network](http://www.brac.net/sites/default/files/Advocacy in action.pdf) (RSPN), formed to address the need for centralized functions, such as government relations. RSPN was created by geographically dispersed organizations known as Rural Support Programs (RSPs) implementing the same rural social mobilization model, as no one organization had the time or access to mobilize resources to scale.

第三个案例研究介绍了巴基斯坦的农村支持计划网络（RSPN），该网络的建立是为了满足对集中化职能的需求，如政府关系。 RSPN 是由分散在不同地区的组织创建的，这些组织被称为农村支持计划 (RSP)，它们实施相同的农村社会动员模式，因为没有任何一个组织有时间或途径调动资源来扩大规模。



[Urban Partnership for Poverty Reduction Program](http://www.upprbd.org/) (UPPRP), a UN initiative focused on helping slum dwellers in Bangladesh organize for better living conditions, is the focus of our fourth case study. Reeling from the devastation of communities in slums during evictions, UPPRP reformulated its approach with intensive work on national urban policy development and deeper relationships with local governments.

城市扶贫伙伴关系计划（UPPRP）是联合国的一项倡议，致力于帮助孟加拉国的贫民窟居民组织起来，争取更好的生活条件。由于贫民窟社区在拆迁过程中遭受了严重破坏，UPPRP 重新制定了工作方法，在国家城市政策制定方面开展了大量工作，并与地方政府建立了更深层次的关系。



### Emerging themes

### 新出现的主题

Certain elements of program or organization design *can* facilitate potential for scale—for example, maintaining low costs or adopting a network approach. However, our research detected a clear set of “implementation plus” or intermediation activities in these scaling success stories—enacted by a third party or by the implementers themselves.

计划或组织设计的某些要素可以促进规模化的潜力，例如，保持低成本或采用网络方法。然而，我们的研究发现，在这些成功的规模化案例中，存在着一系列明显的 “实施附加 ”或中介活动——由第三方或实施者本身来实施。



While we saw diversity in strategy, we noticed that a few recurring types of activities were not directly related to implementation (see the types of intermediation activities chart below).

虽然我们看到了战略的多样性，但我们也注意到，有几类经常出现的活动与实施并无直接关系（见下文中介活动类型图）。

| Benefit      | Description                                                  | Example from case studies                                    |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Access       | - Developing or leveraging social relationships with powerful people<br />- Connecting with experts, stakeholders, and other relevant contacts | The founder of RSPN had personal relationships with the prime minister and other high-ranking government officials, and leveraged them for funding opportunities |
| Capacity     | - Training or training materials<br />- Providing technology<br />- Supporting activities that make the organization more financial sustainable | UPPRP improved training modules for the community leaders and connected them with elected officials |
| Organization | - Creating platforms for sharing, interacting, and coordinating<br />- Providing documentation and knowledge management<br />- Supporting M&E, financial management, and other back-end operations<br />- Creating infrastructure or structures to support | RSPN coordinated strategic internal working groups for organizations in their network |
| Sponsorship  | - Providing thought leadership or input in strategy development<br />- Endorsing an innovation or organization<br />- Providing financial resources | Local NGOs who participated in BRAC's Educational Support program received financial support, and the informal stamp of approval from BRAC made it easier for donors and other NGO partners to trust them |
| Visibility   | - Raising the profile of an issue, organization, or innovation<br />- Conducting advocacy campaigns | CGAP and Ford Foundation graduation consortium members attracted more attention from policymakers, academics, donors, and the press |

| 效益   | 描述                                                         | 案例研究                                                     |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 准入   | - 与有权势的人发展或利用社会关系<br />- 与专家，利益相关者和其他相关者建立联系 | RSPN 的创始人与总理和其他高级政府官员有私人关系，并利用他们获得融资机会 |
| 能力   | - 培训或培训材料<br />- 提供技术<br />- 支持那些使组织更具经济可持续的活动 | UPPRP 改进了社区领导人的培训模式，并将他们与民选官员联系起来 |
| 组织   | - 创建共享、互动和协调的平台<br />- 提供文档和知识管理<br />- 支持机械与电器服务（M&E）、财务管理和其他基础能力<br />- 创建基础设施或结构以进行支持 | RSPN在 其网络中协调各组织的战略内部工作组                    |
| 赞助   | - 为战略制定提供思想领导或意见<br />- 为创新或组织背书<br />- 提供财政资源 | 参与 BRAC 教育支持计划的当地非政府组织获得了资金支持，而 BRAC 的非正式认可印章使捐助方和其他非政府组织合作伙伴更容易信任它们 |
| 可见性 | - 提高某个问题、组织或创新的知名度<br />- 开展宣传活动       | 援助最贫穷者协商小组和福特基金会毕业联合会成员吸引了决策者、学术界、捐 助者和新闻界更多的关注 |

Capacity, access, and organization benefits occurred in every case, while visibility and sponsorship occurred only in some. Some of the best benefits that intermediaries imparted were not intentional. The value of each benefit clearly depends on context—capacity and organization are more focused on changing the groups that are implementing activities, whereas the other three benefits focus more on the dynamics between the ecosystem and the implementers.

能力、准入和组织效益在每一个案例中都有体现，而知名度和赞助只在一些案例中出现。中间人带来的一些最佳益处并非有意为之。每种效益的价值显然取决于具体情况——能力和组织效益更侧重于改变实施活动的群体，而其他三种效益则更侧重于生态系统与实施者之间的动态关系。



While useful, there are clear limitations to historical analyses of scale-up experiences. To explore these themes more deeply, we are currently working with [six South Asian organizations](http://www.brac.net/content/doing-while-learning-collaborative-models-scaling-innovation#1), including BRAC, to follow individual projects through a year of scale-up efforts. Through this project, we hope to develop frameworks and tools to inform and guide future development efforts.

对扩大规模经验的历史分析虽然有用，但也存在明显的局限性。为了更深入地探讨这些主题，我们目前正在与包括孟加拉国农村重建与发展委员会在内的六个南亚组织合作，通过一年的扩大规模工作，对各个项目进行跟踪。我们希望通过该项目制定框架和工具，为未来的发展工作提供信息和指导。
