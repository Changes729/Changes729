# A Mismatch in Philanthropic Interest and Support

# 慈善价值与支持的错配

Donors say that organizational governance and competence are critical factors in determining where their grants flow. So why aren’t they putting their money where their mouths are?

捐赠者说，组织治理能力和工作能力是决定他们赠款去向的关键因素。那么，为什么不把钱用在那些他们认为重要的因素上呢？



Many a dollar has been spent on books and at business schools in the quest to find the magic ingredients that create high-performing businesses. Corporate boards and leaders spend an inordinate amount of time worrying about how to invest in growing a quality organizational culture, building great strategies and bold visions, supporting their leaders, and mitigating risk. These factors indicate the strength of a company, and investors seek out strong companies because they know their investment is safe in its hands.

为了掌握能使企业提高绩效的神奇要素，人们在书籍和商学院之间花费了大量的资金。公司董事会和领导者花费了大量的时间冥思苦想——如何才能投资于发展优质的组织文化、制定伟大的战略和大胆的愿景、支持他们的领导者并降低风险。这些因素象征了一家公司的实力，投资者寻找实力雄厚的公司，因为他们知道自己的投资在这些公司手中是安全的。



Those same organizational competencies matter in the nonprofit world too. Nonprofits that can demonstrate a clear strategy, capability, and leadership are better equipped to provide social and environmental returns. Now more than ever, social investors are attuned to nonprofits’ capability to deliver on their missions. Organizations that fail to articulate how they use funds to advance their mission face a future where philanthropic dollars are harder to find.

这些组织能力在非营利组织中也同样重要。能够展示明晰的战略、能力和领导力的非营利组织更有能力提供社会和环境回报。现在，社会投资者比以往任何时候都更加关注非营利组织履行使命的能力。那些未能明确说明如何使用资金来推进其使命的组织，在未来将很难找到慈善资金。



This mission focus is perhaps best evidenced in my home country of Australia, where a year ago the federal government released its once-in-a-decade [“Giving Australia 2016” report](http://www.communitybusinesspartnership.gov.au/about/research-projects/giving-australia-2016/). The report examines everything from what drives individuals to volunteer to which charities are receiving corporate support and why. It also provides insight into the factors that motivate individuals and families to establish foundations and what ultimately influences their giving practices.

对使命的聚焦也许在我的祖国澳大利亚得到了最好的证明，一年前，澳大利亚联邦政府发布了十年一次的 [“2016 澳大利亚捐赠”]((http://www.communitybusinesspartnership.gov.au/about/research-projects/giving-australia-2016/)) 报告。该报告全面调查了包括独立志愿者（志愿行动）的驱动因素，和哪些慈善机构正在接受企业支持及其原因。报告还深入分析了促使个人和家庭建立基金会的因素，以及最终影响他们捐赠行为的因素。



### The Value of Governance Capability and Competence

### 治理能力和工作能力的价值

After personal alignment with a cause, philanthropists surveyed for the report were most influenced by governance capability and general competence. [A resounding 92 percent of philanthropists](http://www.communitybusinesspartnership.gov.au/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/giving_australia_2016_fact_sheet_philanthropy_accessible.pdf) who responded indicated that nonprofit governance and competence were critical to deciding where their grants flow.

当个人认同某个慈善事业后，受访慈善家们表示他们最看重治理能力和工作能力。[92%的受访慈善家](http://www.communitybusinesspartnership.gov.au/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/giving_australia_2016_fact_sheet_philanthropy_accessible.pdf) 表示，非营利组织的治理能力和工作能力是决定他们赠款去向的关键因素。



But it’s important to note that when we’re talking about governance and capability, we’re really talking about organizational capacity. In the report, one philanthropist highlighted the themes at play, suggesting that they would not consider giving hundreds of thousands of dollars to an organization that doesn’t have strong organizational capabilities.

但需要注意的是，当我们谈论治理能力和工作能力时，我们真正谈论的是组织能力。在报告中，一位慈善家强调了这个主题，表示他们不会考虑向一个没有强大组织能力的组织捐赠数十万美元。



Concerns around organizational capabilities relate to the ability of an organization to create and deliver on strategy, manage its core assets (human, financial, infrastructure, and data assets), mitigate and deal with risk, and ultimately deliver impact.

对组织能力的关注涉及到一个组织在制定和实施战略、管理其核心资产（人力、财力、基础设施和数据资产）、降低和应对风险以及最终产生影响方面的能力。



Unfortunately, for many philanthropists, assessing organizational capacity is somewhat subjective. Philanthropists surveyed for the report noted that their perception of an organization as an issue area expert was important, but so were their previous interactions with that organization and whether the organization was responsive, reliable, and communicative. These factors are not easily quantifiable.

遗憾的是，对许多慈善家而言，评估组织能力是一件主观的事情。接受报告调查的慈善家们指出，他们认为一个组织是否是相关问题领域的专家是很重要的，同时他们先前与该组织的互动过程，以及该组织是否有积极响应、可靠和善于沟通也很重要。而这些因素不易量化。



My organization, [Perpetual](https://www.perpetual.com.au/), is one of Australia’s oldest trustee companies and philanthropic advisory businesses. With more than 1,000 charitable trusts and endowments under management, we couldn’t possibly employ subject matter experts across all fields. Instead, we advise the philanthropists we work with by assessing the organizational capability of charities of all sizes working in all sectors.

我所在的[永恒公司（Perpetual）](https://www.perpetual.com.au/)是澳大利亚历史最悠久的信托公司和慈善咨询公司之一。我们管理着 1000 多个慈善信托和捐赠基金，不可能聘用所有领域的专家。相反，我们通过评估各行各业各种规模慈善机构的组织能力，为我们合作的慈善家提供建议。



Our assessment process works like this: Charities that seek support from our philanthropist clients complete an application form focused on organizational details—as opposed to information about their specific programs or projects. From these responses we can see how the charity:

我们的评估流程是这样的：向我们的慈善家客户寻求支持的慈善组织填写一份申请表，申请表的重点是组织细节，而不是其具体项目或计划的信息。从这些答复中，我们可以了解慈善机构如何：



- Measures and maps its effectiveness
- Measures and maps its efficiency
- Tracks external factors influencing how it works
- Manages and mitigates risk
- 衡量和规划其有效性
- 衡量和规划其效率
- 跟踪影响其工作方式的外部因素
- 管理和降低风险



The only project-focused section of the application looks at how the organization will track the outcomes of its work.

申请表中唯一以项目为重点的部分考察了该组织会如何跟踪其工作成果。



We give each response a score and then aggregate the totals so that we can better evaluate which organizations are top tier. Organizations that score within our recommendation range receive 95 percent of all funding from the philanthropists we work with.  

我们会给每个回答打分，然后汇总总分，以便更好地评估哪些组织是一流的。得分在我们推荐范围内的组织将从我们合作的慈善家那里获得 95% 的资金。 



This effort has several benefits: It is replicable across issue areas, removes the subjectivity that can come with a narrative-based assessment process, and is highly transparent for the applicant organizations.

这项工作有几个好处： 它可以在不同的问题领域复制，消除了以叙述为基础的评估过程可能带来的主观性，而且对申请组织来说是高度透明的。



We don’t restrict the type of funding organizations can ask for. They tell us that this approach leads them to apply for what they need rather than what they think might appeal most to individual donors. It’s not surprising that when provided with this freedom to ask for anything, nonprofits often request support to increase their capacity through staff training, infrastructure upgrades, leadership development, and consulting.

我们不限制组织申请的资金类型。他们告诉我们，这种方法使他们能够申请他们所需要的东西，而不是他们认为最能吸引个人捐赠者的东西。非营利组织有了这种自由申请资源的权利后，往往会通过员工培训、基础设施升级、领导力培养和咨询等方式来提高自身能力，这一点也不足为奇。



**Funding Organizational Capacity**

**资助组织能力**

The second big takeaway from the report is that while philanthropists may increasingly value organizations with strong governance and capacity, we don’t see them increasing their capacity-building grants to match. If philanthropists genuinely believe that governance and organizational competence are critical to providing good outcomes, then why isn’t giving to training, strategy development, infrastructure support, and board capacity-building more common?

该报告的第二个重大启示是，尽管慈善家们可能越来越重视具有强大治理和能力的组织，但我们并没有看到他们增加与之相匹配的能力建设资助。如果慈善家们真的相信治理和组织能力对于提供良好的成果至关重要，那么为什么对培训、战略制定、基础设施支持和董事会能力建设的捐赠不更为普遍呢？



In their article titled “[The Nonprofit Starvation Cycle](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/the_nonprofit_starvation_cycle),” Ann Goggins Gregory and Dan Howard of Bridgespan describe how many nonprofits find themselves chasing funds for the next “innovative” project rather than for their own capacity to deliver outstanding outcomes. This cycle is perpetuated by funders with stringent limitations on what can or cannot be supported, including caps on administrative costs. Assessment methodologies that evaluate charity programs based on funder priorities rather than on organizations’ capacities to meet the needs of their communities are only feeding into this cycle.

布利吉斯潘集团（Bridgespan）的 安·戈金斯·格雷戈里 （Ann Goggins Gregory）和丹·霍华德 （Dan Howard）在题为“[非营利组织的饥饿循环](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/the_nonprofit_starvation_cycle) ”的文章中描述了许多非营利组织是如何追寻下一个 “创新 ”项目资金，而不是提升自身提供卓越成果的能力。资助者对可以或不可以支持的项目有严格的限制，包括行政费用上限，从而使这样的恶性循环持续下去。根据资助者的优先事项而不是组织满足社区需求的能力来评估慈善项目的评估方法只会助长这一循环。



Identifying and investing in well-governed nonprofit organizations is critical to improving funder effectiveness. To find these organizations, philanthropists need an assessment methodology that addresses questions on strategy, leadership, capability, and mission. Philanthropists should feel confident investing in nonprofits that have a culture centered on tracking effectiveness, managing resources well, and mitigating risks.

发现并投资于管理良好的非营利组织对于提高资助者的效率至关重要。为了找到这些组织，慈善家们需要一种评估方法来解决战略、领导力、能力和使命方面的问题。慈善家应该有信心投资于那些有着追踪成效、妥善管理资源和降低风险为中心的文化的非营利组织。



If philanthropists genuinely believe that the best-performing nonprofits have outstanding governance capabilities, then we must see greater investment in helping all nonprofits improve in that area. That means committing resources to build additional governance support, investing in strategy development, leadership capacity, financial management, and even board fundraising proficiency. A rising tide of improved governance capability has the potential to lift the performance of the entire sector and ultimately lead to better outcomes for communities.

如果慈善家们真的相信表现最好的非营利组织拥有出色的管理能力，那么我们就必须加大投资力度，帮助所有非营利组织在这一领域取得进步。这意味着要投入更多的资源来建立更多的管理支持，投资于战略发展、领导能力、财务管理，甚至董事会的筹资能力。提高管理能力的浪潮有可能提升整个行业的绩效，并最终为社区带来更好的成效。
