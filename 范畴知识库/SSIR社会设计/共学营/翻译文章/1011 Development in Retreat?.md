# Development in Retreat?

# 发展在衰退？

Finding a way forward in the global pullback from solidarity.

在全球衰退之中寻找团结前进之路。



***This is the first article in an SSIR series authored by T. Alexander Puutio and other global development experts and leaders on how the sector can chart a path forward in the face of government pullbacks.***

**这是亚历山大·普蒂奥和其他全球发展专家及领导者为《斯坦福社会创新评论》撰写的系列文章中的第一篇，探讨在政府缩减投入（government pullbacks）的情况下，该领域应如何规划未来的发展道路。**



Since the establishment of the first modern development agencies in the mid-20th century, the very concept of global development has never been tested like it’s being tested now. Even during recessions and upheavals, global development always followed a consistently forward trajectory, with an understood commitment to equity, dignity, and shared prosperity. Bolstered by landmark global agreements such as the Millennium Development Goals (MDGs) and later the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), there was both an aspirational consensus on what multilateral global cooperation could achieve, and development was seen as a critical investment for global stability.

自 20 世纪中叶，第一批现代发展机构（development agencies）成立以来，全球发展从未像现在这样面临着考验。即使在经济衰退和动荡的时期，全球发展也始终保持着一贯的轨迹前进，在公平、尊严和共同繁荣等方面有着心照不宣的承诺。在千年发展目标（MDGs）以及后来的可持续发展目标（SDGs）等具有里程碑意义的全球协议（global agreements）的推动下，人们对多边全球合作（multilateral global cooperation）所期望实现的目标有了人人向往的共识，而发展也被视为对全球稳定的关键投资。



Today, we find ourselves confronting a starkly different reality, a nightmare scenario long in the making but one whose arrival nevertheless feels as abrupt as it is unsettling. The optimism of earlier decades has been replaced by caution, doubt, and even open hostility towards global engagement and international cooperation, and the global development industry finds itself in retreat.

当下，我们发现我们面对的世界已经截然不同，噩梦般的舞台早早开始酝酿，而它的到来让人感到既突然又不安。全球发展行业（global development industry）发现自己正在衰退，几十年前的乐观已经被如今的谨慎、怀疑，甚至对全球参与和国际合作的公开反对态度所取代。



## A Slow Turning Point

## 一个缓慢的转折点

While there is nothing gradual about the United States’ withdrawal from the development industry, the slow-boil transformation beneath it has gone unnoticed by many for years. Rising discontent with globalization initially manifested as pushback against trade agreements and global economic integration, but development agencies became symbolic proxies for the broader “global elite,” seen as disconnected from the very communities they sought to help. William Easterly’s and Dambisa Moyo’s warnings went unheeded, while the development industry did little to justify itself to those who did not instinctively validate its value proposition.

虽然美国在发展产业的退出并非一蹴而就，但多年来，很多人还是没有注意到其背后的缓慢转型。对全球化不满的情绪，最初表现为对贸易协定和全球经济一体化的抵制，而发展机构逐渐成为广大 “全球精英” 的象征，（这种“精英象征”）意味着与他们试图帮助的社区脱节。威廉·伊斯特里（William Easterly）和丹比萨·莫约（Dambisa Moyo）的警告也无人理会，同时发展行业几乎没有向那些本就不认同它价值的人加以解释或证明自己的合理性。

> 1. 这里的全球精英应该是至一些高大上的发展论坛，但是一方面这些论坛由西方世界主导，而且往往高谈论阔且不切实际。
>
> 2. 威廉·伊斯特里（William Easterly）和丹比萨·莫约（Dambisa Moyo）的警告在《白人的负担》和《死亡援助（Dead Aid）》两本书中有所体现。其中
>
>    - 《白人的负担》
>      - 反对“规划者”（planners）模式，即由西方专家在纸上制定发展计划。
>      - 倡导“探路者”（searchers）模式——注重本地参与、需求导向、试错反馈。
>      - 指出援助项目缺乏责任机制，**失败了没人负责，成功也与当地人无关**。
>      - 揭露发展机构的“精英主义”倾向，忽视普通人的声音。
>    - 《死亡援助》
>      - 援助会**扭曲非洲的政治经济结构**，造成依赖、腐败和懒惰政府。
>      - 援助没有帮助国家实现可持续发展，反而阻碍了经济自主。
>      - 建议逐步减少援助，转向投资、市场和贸易为基础的合作。
>
>    其实都要求一种 “在地性” 的发展。
>
> 3. **发展行业把自己的正当性当作理所当然，却未向怀疑者努力证明自己有何价值，因此在公共舆论中逐渐失去了信任与影响力。**
>
>    这是对该行业**话语闭环、精英化、脱离公众沟通**的一种深刻批评。



The seeds of discontent were planted in the growing income inequalities within developed nations, which many citizens attributed to globalization and unchecked immigration policies. Politicians, often from the right, skillfully capitalized on these sentiments, amplifying fears and shifting blame towards international agencies and foreign aid programs, portraying them as diverting essential resources from domestic priorities (while the public consistently overestimated how much was being spent to aid foreign nations and how little was gained). The stage was set for the expanding circles of empathy to rapidly contract, an inward turn which has gleefully dismantled decades of consensus around global solidarity. Countries once seen as standard-bearers of international cooperation began to question their roles as global benefactors, ushering in policies focused on national sovereignty and self-interest.

发达国家内部收入不平等的加剧是（对全球化产生）不满的潜在原因，然而许多公民却将其归咎于全球化和不受约束的移民政策。政治家们（通常是右翼人士）巧妙地利用了这些情绪，散播恐惧并将责任推给国际机构和外国援助项目，将它们描绘为从国内优先事项中转出了重要的资源（尽管公众一直高估了对外援助所需的资金，也低估了其收益）。原本正在扩大的同理心圈子，如今却正处于一个迅速收缩的阶段，这种（行政策略）内向的转变，谈笑间（gleefully）就瓦解了数十年来全球团结的共识。曾经被视为国际合作标杆的国家开始质疑自己作为国际援助者（global benefactors）的角色，并开始推行注重国家主权和自身利益的政策。



The pendulum has swung most dramatically in the United States, where after decades of an increasingly uneasy relationship with multilateralism, the United States has embarked on a course that now threatens global development as *a concept*. Much of what we are seeing now was heralded by Donald Trump’s first term. But the withdrawal from the Paris Agreement was only the beginning: Now, the US international development apparatus itself faces dismantling, while the administration disavows the SDGs as neither aspirational nor appropriate. Multilateral commitments hang precariously in the balance, threatening the stability of international institutions relied upon globally.

政策转向在美国表现的最为明显，几十年来，美国与多边主义的关系愈发紧张，如今，美国已经走上了一条将“全球发展”的观念都视作威胁的道路。我们现在看到的大部分情况都在唐纳德·特朗普的第一任期所预示的。然而退出《巴黎协定》仅仅是个开始： 如今，即使美国（为推动国际发展而设立）的国际发展机构本身也面临着解散风险，而美国政府却还认为可持续发展目标既不理想也不合适。多边承诺岌岌可危，威胁到全球所依赖的国际机构的稳定。



This shift represents more than mere defunding. It is a decoupling of values, a fundamental debasing of the very concept of international development. The administration has portrayed development work as antithetical to national interests and foreign assistance budgets as wasteful luxuries. In targeting what many conservative politicians deride as "wokeness," this ideological cleansing disregard the complex blend of soft and hard power benefits that development work has conferred to the country. By disregarding the national security threats posed by unchecked natural disasters, pandemics, famines, and water shortages, this radical departure from previous US foreign policy is creating profound uncertainties about future global cooperation and stability.

这种转变不仅仅是资金的缩减。它是一种价值观的脱钩，在根本上贬低了国际发展的理念。政府将发展工作描述为与国家利益背道而驰，将对外援助预算描述为浪费的奢侈品。面对那些许多保守派政客所讥讽的觉醒意识（wokeness） ，这种意识形态的清洗忽视了发展工作中软实力和硬实力的化学反应能为国家带来诸多益处。由于无视不受控制的自然灾害、大流行病、饥荒和水资源短缺等对国家安全构成的威胁，这种彻底背离美国以往外交政策的做法正在给未来的全球合作与稳定带来深刻的不确定性。

> “Woke” 最初源自非裔美国人的俚语，意为“保持警醒”，特别指对种族歧视和社会不公正的觉察和关注。它本来的含义是呼吁对社会不平等和压迫保持清醒的认识。
>
> 近年来，“wokeness”演变成一种更广泛的社会文化现象，强调反对各种形式的歧视和压迫，提倡多元化、包容和平等。它涵盖了支持 #MeToo 运动、LGBTQ+ 权利、反对系统性种族主义以及推动环境正义等议题。



The immediate consequences of the United States’ retreat from global development are stark. Key projects funded by USAID and contributions to critical UN agencies like [UNFPA have halted](https://www.unfpa.org/press/statement-unfpa-executive-director-united-states-government-funding-cuts), leaving significant gaps in humanitarian and developmental support. Tens of thousands of development professionals face [uncertain futures](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/spotlights/2025/usaid-staffers-describe-colleagues-abandoned-in-violence-in-congo-as-doge-ends-assistance-programs/), and tens of millions of beneficiaries have lost essential lifelines. Future generations may find it difficult to understand why we didn’t do more to stop the crisis.

美国退出全球发展的直接后果是显而易见的。美国国际开发署（USAID）资助的关键项目以及对[联合国人口基金（UNFPA）等联合国关键机构的捐助已经停止](https://www.unfpa.org/press/statement-unfpa-executive-director-united-states-government-funding-cuts)，从而在人道主义和发展支持方面留下了巨大缺口。数以万计的发展专业人员面临着[不确定的未来](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/spotlights/2025/usaid-staffers-describe-colleagues-abandoned-in-violence-in-congo-as-doge-ends-assistance-programs/)，数以千万计的受益者们失去了重要的生命线。子孙后代可能难以理解，为什么我们没有采取更多措施来阻止这场危机。



The withdrawal of American leadership has emboldened others to roll back their own aid, creating a cascade effect further undermining the global development infrastructure. According to the European Network on Debt and Development, 2024 saw the first drop in ODA since 2017 with total aid budgets [falling by 7.1 percent](https://www.eurodad.org/preliminary_aid_2024). Countries like France, Germany, Sweden and Finland are undertaking[ significant cuts to their foreign aid budgets](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/02/19/europe-following-us-in-cutting-foreign-aid-says-caritas-radio-schuman), and parties on the right in many other countries have newfound vigor for calls for an inward turn of their own. These moves amount to an echo effect: once Washington stepped back, the political cost of cutting aid elsewhere fell, accelerating what one analyst called a “burden-shedding cascade” across the entire donor club.

美国领导层的退出使其他国家更加大胆地削减自己的援助，从而产生连带效应，进一步破坏全球发展基础设施。根据欧洲债务与发展网络（European Network on Debt and Development）的数据，2024 年的官方发展援助将出现自 2017 年以来的首次下降，援助预算总额将[下降 7.1%](https://www.eurodad.org/preliminary_aid_2024)。法国、德国、瑞典和芬兰等国正在[大幅削减对外援助预算](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/02/19/europe-following-us-in-cutting-foreign-aid-says-caritas-radio-schuman)，而其他许多国家的右翼政党也在呼吁本国转向内向型发展。这些举动产生了一种连锁反应（Echo effect）：一旦美国开始撤退，其他国家削减援助的政治成本就会下降，从而加速整个援助者联盟（donor club）的 “减负连环效应（burden-shedding cascade）”。



## Taking Accountability

## 承担责任

There is no uncertainty about the immediate catalyst for all of this. Yet alongside justified outrage at the dismantling of a system we had come to take for granted, the global development industry must confront the role it has played in its own unraveling. For decades, aid agencies have struggled to effectively communicate and quantify their impact. Last year OECD-DAC members [rightfully listed ](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/07/development-co-operation-report-2024_b9cb300f/357b63f7-en.pdf?)inadequate data, difficulties in designing results frameworks, and impact evaluation as the three most urgent reasons they could not accomplish more to reduce poverty. The very same problems have undermined the entire industry’s legitimacy in the eyes of others. Past successes became future assumptions while accountability was too often sacrificed on the altar of good intentions.

虽然造成这种衰退的直接原因并不清楚。然而，我们对一个理应存在的系统的解体感到义愤填膺之外，全球发展行业还必须正视自己在其自身的这场解体中所扮演的角色。几十年来，援助机构一直在努力有效地宣传和量化其影响。去年，经合组织发展援助委员会（OECD-DAC）成员[直言不讳地将](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/07/development-co-operation-report-2024_b9cb300f/357b63f7-en.pdf?)数据不足、成果框架设计困难和影响评估列为他们无法在减贫方面取得更大成就的三个最紧迫原因。正是这些问题损害了整个行业在他人眼中的合法性。过去的成功变成了未来的期待，好意成为借口，却牺牲了真正的效果和责任。



Standing on the right side of history and with a moral high ground, the sector grew complacent, failing to recognize that global solidarity requires continuous justification, especially in politically volatile contexts. The development industry’s tendency to focus more on signals of value than [actionable transparency](https://www.publishwhatyoufund.org/the-index/2024/) on the value they bring has made it vulnerable to criticisms: whether valid or opportunistic, proving just as lethal. Internally, too, persistent criticism for inefficiency, opacity, and excessive bureaucracy—as well as high overheads, unclear impact metrics, and an inability to clearly communicate tangible outcomes to donors and the public—have fostered skepticism and eroded trust among those whose support is now desperately needed.

站在历史的正确一边，站在道德的制高点上，该行业变得自满，没有认识到全球团结需要持续的理由，尤其是在政治动荡的背景下。发展行业倾向于更多地关注价值信号，而不是[可操作的透明度（actionable transparency）](https://www.publishwhatyoufund.org/the-index/2024/)所带来的价值，这使发展行业容易受到批评：无论是合理的还是投机取巧的批评，都证明是危害极大的。就内部而言，对低效率、不透明和过度官僚主义的长久批评，以及高昂的管理费用、不明确的影响衡量标准、无法向捐赠者和公众清晰传达实际成果等问题，都引发了怀疑，并削弱了那些目前急需支持的人们对其的信任。



If the development industry's shortcomings were hidden from sight, for decades, they are laid bare today. The price for the failure is now evident, as is the need for introspection and meaningful reform. In this sense, the path forward, however painful, presents a moment for transformative reflection: Organizations must embrace rigorous accountability and effectiveness standards. Efficiency is imperative, and every dollar must justify itself through clear, measurable impacts.

如果说几十年来，发展行业的弊端一直隐藏在人们的视线之外，那么今天，这些弊端已经暴露无遗。失败的代价是显而易见的，反省和有意义的改革的必要性也是显而易见的。从这个意义上说，前进的道路无论多么痛苦，当下都是进行变革性反思的时刻： 各组织必须拥抱严格的问责制和有效性标准。提高效率势在必行，每一分钱都必须通过清晰、可衡量的影响来证明自己的合理性。



## Navigating a Way Forward

## 前进的道路

We aren’t looking for silver linings amidst a raging thunderstorm. But whatever good comes from this process will be through adjustments made to ensure survival, innovations of necessity, and acts of desperation during our industry’s great extinction event (from which only the fittest, luckiest, and best connected will see themselves through). Good results will come at a cost no one wanted to pay. And yet we already see the forces of adaptation re-shaping the industry. NGOs, multilateral organizations, and development experts themselves are finding more than shelter from the storm: Many are finding entirely new strategies to thrive to whatever extent there is thriving left to be had.

我们并不是要在肆虐的暴风雨中寻找一线生机。但无论这一过程中产生了什么有益的结果，都需要经过调整生存策略，必要的创新，以及令人绝望的行业的大灭绝（只有最适合、最幸运、关系最好的团队才能从中幸存下来）。有益的结果将从巨大的，没有人能承受的代价中产生。然而，我们已经看到一些适应性的力量正在重塑这个行业。非政府组织、多边组织和发展专家们自己找到的不仅仅是躲避风暴的避难所： 许多组织正在寻找全新的策略，以求在尚存的“沃土”中茁壮成长。

- **Efficiency and technology**: Perhaps the most immediate impact of the great retreat has been a surge of interest in efficiency, process optimization, and technology-driven solutions. The rate of AI adoption in our industry has greatly increased, and organizations around the world are embracing new technologies to extend their reach and reduce operational costs, making programs more efficient and scalable. For example, Mercy Corps has strengthened [its commitment to AI](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/mercy-corp-uses-data-and-ai-for-preemptive-humanitarian-aid) and climate analytics, while Accountability Lab, Development Gateway, and Digital Public have launched [a partnership matching service](https://accountabilitylab.org/launching-a-partnership-matching-service-for-nonprofits/) to support others through the storm.
- **效率与技术**： 也许大撤退最直接的影响就是人们对效率、流程优化和技术驱动型解决方案的兴趣激增。我们行业采用人工智能的速度大大加快，世界各地的组织都在采用新技术来扩大影响范围、降低运营成本，从而使项目更加高效、更具可扩展性。例如，国际美慈组织（Mercy Corps）加强了[对人工智能的承诺](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/mercy-corp-uses-data-and-ai-for-preemptive-humanitarian-aid)和气候分析，而问责实验室（Accountability Lab）、发展网关（Development Gateway）和数字公共（Digital Public）则推出了[合作伙伴匹配服务](https://accountabilitylab.org/launching-a-partnership-matching-service-for-nonprofits/)，以支持他人渡过难关。
- **Localization**: Another tangible pivot is a resurgence of [localized approaches](https://www.hks.harvard.edu/faculty-research/policy-topics/development-economic-growth/move-freeze-foreign-aid-international). As global pillars of support fall one by one, many are finding ways to lean on locally centered designs that shift power and resources closer to beneficiary communities. Funding will be tighter and scopes will be narrower, of course, but closer connections with the local community will pay dividends of its own, opening new paths to genuine sustainability, strengthening local governance, and reducing future dependency on external funding. Many are also doubling down on community empowerment, promoting self-reliance by investing directly in local capacities and institutions rather than the middleware of development, giving the grassroots a more direct role in the process. [Some experts](https://www.hks.harvard.edu/faculty-research/policy-topics/development-economic-growth/move-freeze-foreign-aid-international) go so far as to argue that it is *good* that African countries are finally encouraged to stand on their own two feet instead of depending on Western interventions.
- **本地化**： 另一个有形的支点是[本地化方法](https://www.hks.harvard.edu/faculty-research/policy-topics/development-economic-growth/move-freeze-foreign-aid-international)的复苏。随着全球支柱的一个个倒下，许多人正在想方设法依靠以本地为中心的设计，将权力和资源转移到更靠近受益社区的地方。当然，资金将更加紧张，范围将更加狭窄，但与当地社区更紧密的联系将带来自身的红利，为真正的可持续发展开辟新的道路，加强地方治理，并减少未来对外部资金的依赖。许多国家还加倍重视社区赋权，通过直接投资于地方能力和机构而不是发展的中间件来促进自力更生，让基层在这一过程中发挥更直接的作用。[一些专家](https://www.hks.harvard.edu/faculty-research/policy-topics/development-economic-growth/move-freeze-foreign-aid-international) 甚至认为，非洲国家最终被鼓励自力更生，而不是依赖西方干预，这是件好事。
- **New alternatives, new partners**: Many organizations are finding themselves entering into conversations with partners they might not have considered before. Private sector collaborations are on the rise, with organizations seeking to deepen their partnerships with private companies in hopes of leveraging their operational expertise, financial acumen, and innovation capabilities, as well as financial resources. Of course, beneficiary states will recall the specter of historic exploitation, and the possibility of neo-imperialism latent in these emerging corporate relationships. Apart from [corporate giving ](https://benevity.com/state-of-corporate-purpose-2024)and partnering, interest in individual philanthropies is growing rapidly, leading to both potential solutions to financing gaps as well as power imbalances that may prove too tricky for some organizations to navigate through unscathed.
- **新的选择，新的合作伙伴**： 许多组织发现自己正在与以前可能从未考虑过的合作伙伴进行对话。与私营部门的合作正在增加，各组织寻求深化与私营公司的伙伴关系，希望利用它们的业务专长、金融头脑、创新能力以及财政资源。当然，受益国会想起历史上剥削的阴影，以及这些新兴企业关系中潜藏的新帝国主义的可能性。除了[企业捐赠](https://benevity.com/state-of-corporate-purpose-2024)和合作伙伴关系之外，人们对个人慈善事业的兴趣也在迅速增长，这既有可能解决资金缺口问题，也有可能导致权力失衡，而这种权力失衡对于某些组织来说可能过于棘手，难以安然度过。
- **Rethinking the long term**: In addition to the tactical shifts, we’re seeing a renewed discourse on the deeper set of strategies deployed by the industry. Impact transparency is among the leading points of discussion, and the need to invest in clearer metrics and more robust reporting practices that communicate tangible outcomes effectively is clear for all who wish to rebuild trust among donors and skeptical publics. The more introspective among us are already calling out for a culture of accountability and a recalibration of how the industry approaches advocacy. Some are even calling for a “back to basics” approach where disease reduction, WASH, literary, and health care concerns are returned to a position of primacy.
- **重新思考长远**： 除了战术上的转变之外，我们还看到业界对更深层次战略的重新讨论。影响力透明度是讨论的重点之一，对于所有希望在捐赠者和持怀疑态度的公众中重建信任的人来说，投资于更清晰的衡量标准和更有力的报告实践以有效传达实际成果的必要性是显而易见的。我们中比较自省的人已经在呼吁建立一种问责制度文化，并重新调整该行业的宣传方式。有些人甚至呼吁采取 “返璞归真 ”的方法，将减少疾病、WASH（Water, Sanitation and Hygiene，水、卫生设施与卫生习惯）、文学和医疗保健问题将重新放在首要位置。



## A Moment of Truth and Opportunity

## 真理与机遇并存的时刻

The era of assumed inevitability of development work is over. While the discourse is rapidly evolving, it is crystal clear that we will need to return to crafting bipartisan narratives that articulate global development as a shared mission that can appeal to a broader political base.

假定发展是一种必然的时代已经过去。虽然话语正在迅速演变，但很显然，我们需要重新构建两党的共识，阐明全球发展作为一项共同的使命，从而吸引更广泛的政治基础。



The harsh lessons of polarization and retreat compel us to acknowledge development work not as guaranteed but as privileged, vital work demanding constant justification, representation, and adaptation. This painful but necessary metamorphosis may yet yield a more resilient and impactful global development ecosystem. If the industry successfully navigates these political headwinds, embedding itself deeper in societal and political foundations, it may emerge stronger, wiser, and more sustainable. In adversity lies opportunity, and perhaps through this crucible, global development will find new legitimacy, broader support, and ultimately, greater effectiveness.

两极分化和行业退缩的惨痛教训迫使我们认识到，发展工作不是有保障的工作，而是有特权的、至关重要的工作，需要不断地证明其合理性、代表性和适应性。这种痛苦但必要的蜕变可能会产生一个更具韧性和影响力的全球发展生态系统。如果该行业能成功驾驭这些政治逆风，更深入地融入社会和政治基础，那么它可能会变得更强大、更明智、更可持续。逆境中蕴藏着机遇，全球发展也许会在这一考验中找到新的合法性，获得更广泛的支持，并最终取得更大的成效。
