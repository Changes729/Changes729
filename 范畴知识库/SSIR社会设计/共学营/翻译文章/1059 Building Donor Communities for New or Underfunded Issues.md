# Building Donor Communities for New or Underfunded Issues

# 为新出现和缺乏资金的问题建立捐赠者社区

Five ways to engage philanthropists and advocates in early-stage or overlooked causes, such as intensive animal agriculture, that have outsized potential for impact.

让慈善家和倡导者参与早期或被忽视的事业（如集约化畜牧业）的五种方法，这些事业具有巨大的影响潜力。

If you had $100 to alleviate the most suffering and do the most good in the world, what would you do with it? For many philanthropists, the answer to this question lies in newer and less expected places.

如果你有 100 美元，可以缓解一些世界上的痛苦，或者是做最好的事情，你会用它来做什么？对于许多慈善家来说，这个问题的答案就在那些新出现的、和不那么令人期待的地方。



One growing community of philanthropists, for example, sees exponential benefits in tackling the harms of intensive animal agriculture. Long overlooked and underfunded, this cause is increasingly attracting donors eager to address the largest social issues of our time. Many funders have woken up to a problem leading thinkers have called “[a defining moral failing of our age](https://www.nytimes.com/2021/12/16/opinion/factory-farming-animals.html)” and “[perhaps the worst crime in history](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2015/sep/25/industrial-farming-one-worst-crimes-history-ethical-question)”: the horrific suffering inflicted on farm animals. Others have recognized factory farming’s colossal negative impacts on [climate](https://academic.oup.com/af/article/9/1/69/5173494), [rural communities, and farmers](https://clf.jhsph.edu/sites/default/files/2022-03/impact-of-industrial-farm-animal-production-on-rural-communities.pdf)—or its contributions to [pandemic risk](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7399585/), [antibiotic resistance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7766021/), and a catastrophic [loss of biodiversity](https://www.chathamhouse.org/2021/02/food-system-impacts-biodiversity-loss).

例如，一个日益壮大的慈善家群体认为，解决集约化畜牧业（intensive animal agriculture）的危害可以带来指数级的收益。这项事业长期以来被忽视，资金不足，但现在却越来越多地吸引着那些渴望解决当代最严重社会问题的捐赠者。许多捐赠者已经意识到了一个被著名思想家称为 “[我们这个时代决定性的道德缺失](https://www.nytimes.com/2021/12/16/opinion/factory-farming-animals.html) ‘和’[也许是历史上最严重的罪行](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2015/sep/25/industrial-farming-one-worst-crimes-history-ethical-question) ” 的问题：农场动物所遭受的痛苦。还有一些人认识到工厂化养殖对[气候](https://academic.oup.com/af/article/9/1/69/5173494)、[农村社区和农民](https://clf.jhsph.edu/sites/default/files/2022-03/impact-of-industrial-farm-animal-production-on-rural-communities.pdf)的巨大负面影响，或对[流行病风险](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7399585/)、[抗生素耐药性](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7766021/)和灾难性的[生物多样性丧失](https://www.chathamhouse.org/2021/02/food-system-impacts-biodiversity-loss)的贡献。

> 集约化畜牧业（intensive animal agriculture），又称**工业化畜牧业生产**、大型农场，反对车多称为 工厂化养殖。



Uniting these funders, including many from younger generations, is an understanding that giving to create a more ethical and resilient food system is, dollar for dollar, one of the highest-impact opportunities in philanthropy and that collaboration can help maximize every precious cent.

让这些捐赠者（包括许多年轻一代的捐赠者）团结在一起的是这样一种认识——为创建一个更道德、更有韧性的粮食系统而进行的捐赠，无论金额大小，都是慈善事业中最具影响力的机会之一，而合作有助于最大限度地利用每一分宝贵的资金。



To this end, in 2018, a group of these funders established Farmed Animal Funders, now [Senterra Funders](https://www.senterrafunders.org/), with the aim of accelerating progress toward a more humane, sustainable food system. Today, Senterra supports high-net-worth individuals who represent about 75 percent of global giving in this sector. It has deployed millions of dollars through a pooled fund and tens of millions more in independent, collaborative donations. It offers a case study for philanthropists and advocates working on other new, underfunded, complex causes on how to coordinate effectively to create wide-ranging social impact.

为此，2018 年，这些捐赠者成立了 农场动物基金会（Farmed Animal Funders），即现在的 [Senterra 基金会（Senterra Funders）](https://www.senterrafunders.org/)，旨在加快实现更人道、更可持续的食品体系。如今，Senterra 资助的高净值个人约占全球该领域捐赠总额的 75%。它通过一个集合基金（pooled fund）投入了数百万美元，并通过独立、合作捐款投入了数千万美元。它为致力于为慈善家和倡导者们，面对其他新出现的、资金不足的和复杂事业的情况下，提供了一个如何有效协调以创造广泛社会影响的案例研究。

> Senterra 是 “sentience（感觉性，感知觉）” 和  “terra” (地球) 的合成词。terra 也有大地的意思，但此处的 terra 明确为 “地球” 的意向。灵智地球基金会、知觉地球基金会。翻译不好。



## An Overlooked Cause With Outsized Returns

## 被忽视的事业却能带来巨大回报

Science shows that farm animals [are as capable](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/aro2.65) of feeling pain, stress, and loneliness as our beloved pets, and given the chance, they experience joy, seek out play, and form deep bonds with one another and with people. However, the vast majority of the billions of land mammals and birds raised for food globally [are confined](https://www.sentienceinstitute.org/global-animal-farming-estimates) in filthy, windowless warehouses—often called factory farms or concentrated animal feeding operations (CAFOs)—where they’re denied fresh air, basic comforts, and the ability to express natural behaviors, and where they’re often subjected to brutal abuse. Billions more sentient aquatic animals endure similar conditions. Shifting how we treat these animals is one of the greatest opportunities to reduce suffering at scale.

科学表明，农场中的动物[能够](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/aro2.65)像我们心爱的宠物一样感受痛苦、压力和孤独，只要有机会，它们就会体验快乐、寻找游戏，并与彼此和人类建立深度的关系。然而，全球饲养的数十亿陆生哺乳动物和鸟类中的绝大多数[被](https://www.sentienceinstitute.org/global-animal-farming-estimates)关在肮脏、没有窗户的笼子里——那些通常被称为工厂化养殖场或集中饲养场（concentrated animal feeding operations，CAFOs）。在那里，它们得不到新鲜空气、基本的舒适感，也无法进行一些自然的行为，还经常受到残酷的虐待。还有数十亿有生命的水生动物忍受着类似的条件。改变我们对待这些动物的方式，是大规模减少痛苦的机会之一。



The very same effort can also save trillions of dollars in [ecological](https://edepot.wur.nl/389545) and [health](https://www.pnas.org/doi/full/10.1073/pnas.1523119113) costs by reforming a system that accelerates climate change and pollution while producing food that is often both [unhealthy](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/cancer-carcinogenicity-of-the-consumption-of-red-meat-and-processed-meat) and [unsafe](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6116329/). Intensive animal agriculture is the [leading cause](https://ourworldindata.org/drivers-of-deforestation) of deforestation and a top global water user, in part because it takes 50 to 100 times more [land](https://ourworldindata.org/land-use-diets) and [water](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/water-withdrawals-per-kg-poore) to produce a single calorie from meat than from plants. Moving toward a more plant-based food system would dramatically ease the strain on the planet and help build a healthier, more livable future. 

同样的努力还可以通过改革一个加速气候变化和污染的系统，节省数万亿美元的[生态](https://edepot.wur.nl/389545)和[健康](https://www.pnas.org/doi/full/10.1073/pnas.1523119113)成本，同时生产的食物往往既[不健康](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/cancer-carcinogenicity-of-the-consumption-of-red-meat-and-processed-meat)又[不安全](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6116329/)。集约化畜牧业是森林砍伐的[主要原因](https://ourworldindata.org/drivers-of-deforestation)，也是全球用水量最大的行业，部分原因是生产一卡路里的肉类所需的[土地](https://ourworldindata.org/land-use-diets)和[水](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/water-withdrawals-per-kg-poore)是植物的50到100倍。向植物性食物系统（plant-based food system）的转变将大大缓解地球的压力，并有助于建设一个更健康、更宜居的未来。



Currently, [95 percent](https://animalcharityevaluators.org/charity-reviews/causes-we-consider/why-farmed-animals/) of animal welfare donations support dog and cat charities, while just 3 percent go to helping farm animals. And at about [$260 million](https://straydoginstitute.org/movement-2024/) annually, giving to this cause represents just [0.04 percent](https://www.citiwarrants.com/home/upload/citi_research/rsch_pdf_30283829.pdf) of global philanthropy and over [100 times less](https://80000hours.org/problem-profiles/factory-farming/#neglectedness) than causes like global development or climate change.

目前，[95%](https://animalcharityevaluators.org/charity-reviews/causes-we-consider/why-farmed-animals/)的动物福利捐款支持猫狗慈善机构，而用于帮助农场动物的捐款仅占 3%。每年用于动物福利事业的捐款约为[2.6亿美元](https://straydoginstitute.org/movement-2024/)，仅占全球慈善事业的[0.04%](https://www.citiwarrants.com/home/upload/citi_research/rsch_pdf_30283829.pdf)，比用于全球发展或气候变化等事业的捐款少[100倍](https://80000hours.org/problem-profiles/factory-farming/#neglectedness)。



But more funding is beginning to flow in its direction, thanks in part to growing [expert](https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/story/10-things-you-should-know-about-industrial-farming) and [popular](https://www.aspca.org/protecting-farm-animals/aspca-surveys) concern. Giving to reform and replace factory farming has surged nearly 500 percent in the last decade, and strategic deployment of these funds has led to remarkable wins.

但是，由于[专家](https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/story/10-things-you-should-know-about-industrial-farming)和[民众](https://www.aspca.org/protecting-farm-animals/aspca-surveys)的日益关注，越来越多的资金开始流向这一领域。在过去十年中，用于改革和取代工厂化养殖的捐赠激增了近 500%，这些资金的战略部署取得了显著的成果。



One example is philanthropy’s role in accelerating the end of the use of battery cages for egg-laying hens, a practice widely condemned for its extreme cruelty and risks to public health. Thanks to donor-funded advocacy, more than [half a billion](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/lewis-bollard) animals no longer endure these conditions. More than [3,000 companies](https://www.openphilanthropy.org/focus/farm-animal-welfare/), including many of the world’s largest food brands, have pledged to stop sourcing battery cage eggs. The [European U](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1999/74/oj/eng)nion and [11 US states](https://cagefreelaws.com/) have banned them, and US cage-free egg production has increased from 13 percent to [45 percent](https://farmanimalwelfare.substack.com/p/crunch-time-for-cage-free) in a decade.

其中一个例子就是慈善事业在加速终止使用电笼饲养蛋鸡方面所发挥的作用，这种做法因其极端残忍和对公众健康的风险而受到广泛谴责。在捐赠者的资助倡导下，超过 [5 亿](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/lewis-bollard)的动物不用再忍受这种条件。超过 [3,000 家公司](https://www.openphilanthropy.org/focus/farm-animal-welfare/)，包括许多全球最大的食品品牌，已经承诺停止采购电笼养鸡蛋。[欧洲](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1999/74/oj/eng)和[美国11个州](https://cagefreelaws.com/)已经禁止使用笼养鸡蛋，美国的无笼养鸡蛋产量在十年间从13%增加到[45%](https://farmanimalwelfare.substack.com/p/crunch-time-for-cage-free)。



Seismic shifts like this are possible, but funding models that help accelerate change are essential. Here are five strategies that have guided Senterra’s approach and progress so far.

这样的巨变是可能发生的，但有助于加速变革的资助模式至关重要。以下是迄今为止有帮助指导 Senterra 的方法与进展的五条策略。



### 1. Build a welcoming front door.

### 1. 构建友好的访客入口。

Many overlooked, complex, or emotionally difficult causes lack funding infrastructure to help donors find or navigate them. Creating a personalized, welcoming on-ramp for them can speed up the process.

许多被忽视的、复杂的或情感上有困难的事业缺少资助基础设施（funding infrastructure）来帮助捐赠者链接到他们。为他们创建一个个性化的、温馨的 “访客入口” 可以帮到他们。



For example, Senterra sets up calls with interested new members to help them understand how their primary motivations and passions align with various giving strategies. It then educates them on the sector, including important intervention areas and strategies (such as improving living conditions for farmed animals, expanding access to healthy plant-based foods via policy and corporate advocacy, and reducing the food system’s climate impact), wins and challenges, and gaps in the field (such as emerging opportunities in the Global South).

例如，Senterra 会与感兴趣的新会员进行电话联系，<u>帮助他们了解自己的主要动机、热情与各种捐赠策略的一致性</u>。然后，Senterra 会对他们进行行业教育，包括重要的干预领域和策略（如改善养殖动物的生活条件、通过政策和企业宣传扩大健康植物性食品的获取途径、减少食品系统对气候的影响）、成功与挑战，以及该领域的差距（如全球南方新出现的机遇）。



Finally, based on what compels them to give, Senterra shares a deeply researched portfolio of promising projects and opportunities to replicate tested interventions. That said, it doesn’t impose strategy or giving requirements, but rather supports donors to refine their own theories of change and understand how they fit into the broader system, in part by connecting them to an active community of values-aligned peers.

最后，Senterra 根据他们的捐赠动机，会分享经过了深入研究的、具有前景的项目组合，以及经过反复测试干预措施的机会。尽管如此，Senterra 并不强加策略或捐赠要求，而是支持捐赠者完善自己的变革理论（theories of change），并了解他们如何融入更广泛的系统，部分方法是将他们与价值观一致的同行们联系起来。



[One for Justice](https://www.oneforjustice.org/)—a coalition of philanthropists, business leaders, and other high-net-worth individuals—offers a comparable front door to criminal justice reform. Its team maps individuals’ interests, goals, and risk appetite then provides ongoing advising and learning through curated briefings, site visits, salons, and giving circles. The result is a clear pathway forward that blends community, education, and advising so that new funders can engage efficiently.

[正义之人（One for Justice）](https://www.oneforjustice.org/)——一个由慈善家、商界领袖和其他高净值人士组成的联盟，为刑事司法改革提供了一个类似的访客入口。该组织的团队将个人的兴趣、目标和风险偏好绘制成图，然后通过精心策划的简报、实地考察、沙龙和捐赠圈，为个人提供持续的建议和学习。这样就为新的捐赠者提供了一条将社区、教育和咨询融为一体的清晰的发展道路，使他们能够高效地参与其中。



### 2. Support effective communication and collaboration.

### 2. 支持有效的沟通与合作。

The combination of shared learning, pooled funds, and independent, coordinated giving (including rapid response efforts) can create a powerful multiplier effect when applied to under-resourced issues, enabling individual funders to collectively punch above their weight.

通过共同学习、集合资金以及独立、协调的捐赠（包括快速反应努力）相结合，可以在资源不足的问题上产生强大的乘数效应（multiplier effect），使单个捐赠者能够通过集体发挥出更大的作用。



Senterra supports shared learning through member calls, working groups, thematic funding circles, retreats, presentations from nonprofit leaders and frontline advocates, and a members-only Slack group. In these forums, members can toss around ideas, examine evidence, and ultimately coordinate donations. They might discuss whether new research on animal sentience should affect which species to prioritize, or debate how to measure impact and progress as a movement.

Senterra 通过会员电话、工作组、专题捐赠圈、静修培训（retreats）、非营利组织领导者和一线倡导者的演讲以及会员专用的 Slack 群组来支持共同学习。在这些论坛上，成员们可以畅所欲言，研究证据，并最终协调捐款。他们可能会讨论关于动物智商的新研究是否会影响优先考虑哪些物种，或者讨论如何衡量运动的影响和进展。



Support also takes the form of pooled funding. For example, [Senterra's PEP Fund](https://www.senterrafunders.org/articles/the-plant-based-eu-policy-pep-fund-3le69) accelerates a shift toward a more plant-based society in Europe by allowing donors to collectively support country-level charities pushing for policy reforms while the time [is ripe for change](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2025/jan/31/more-carrot-less-stick-how-meat-loving-danes-were-sold-a-plant-led-world-first). Many Senterra members don’t have the capacity to vet each group or assess their needs.

支持的形式还包括集合资金。例如，[Senterra 的 PEP 基金](https://www.senterrafunders.org/articles/the-plant-based-eu-policy-pep-fund-3le69) 允许捐赠者在[变革的时机已经成熟](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2025/jan/31/more-carrot-less-stick-how-meat-loving-danes-were-sold-a-plant-led-world-first)时，集体支持国家级慈善机构推动政策改革，从而加速欧洲向植物性食物系统的社会转变。许多 Senterra 成员没有能力去审查每个团体或评估其需求。



Another shared funding model is philanthropic advisory nonprofit [Renaissance Philanthropy](https://www.renaissancephilanthropy.org/)’s “[thesis-driven funds](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/thesis-driven-funds),” which allow donors to back an ambitious, time-bound goal without individually selecting grantees. By relying on the expertise of field leaders, philanthropists can deploy capital quickly and pursue bigger, riskier opportunities than they would on their own.

另一种共享资助模式是非营利性慈善咨询机构[文艺复兴慈善机构（Renaissance Philanthropy）](https://www.renaissancephilanthropy.org/)的“[论文驱动基金](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/thesis-driven-funds)”，它允许捐赠者支持一个雄心勃勃、有时限的目标，而无需单独选择受捐赠者。通过依靠领域领导者的专业知识，慈善家们可以迅速调配资金，追求更大、风险更高的机会。



Finally, Senterra facilitates rapid, decentralized coordination. For example, when a campaign against a global food company that reversed its animal welfare commitments hit a pivotal moment, Senterra hosted a member call to hear directly from a frontline leader and a long-time funder of this work. Based on their insights, members disbursed additional funds to support grassroots advocacy, public awareness campaigns, and shareholder engagement within weeks.

最后，Senterra 推动了一些快速的、分散的配合事项。例如，当一场针对一家全球食品公司违背其动物福利承诺的运动进入关键时刻时，Senterra 举办了一次成员电话会议，直接听取了一线领导者和这项工作的长期捐赠者的意见。根据他们的见解，成员们在几周内就拨付了额外资金，用于支持基层宣传、公众意识宣传活动（public awareness campaigns,）和股东参与。



### 3. Embrace diverse approaches.

### 3. 接受不同的方法。

Often, under-resourced, complex, or early-stage movements have more than one theory of change, and that’s a good thing. We’ve found that embracing varied approaches makes our donor community and overall movement stronger. The different experiences and issue entry points of Senterra’s members underpin a wide range of ideas about what’s effective. This diversity drives thoughtful experimentation and opens avenues for grantees to test different theories themselves.

通常情况下，资源不足、情况复杂或处于早期阶段的运动有不止一种变革理论是好事。我们发现，接受不同的方法会使我们的捐助社区和整个运动更加强大。Senterra 成员的不同经历和问题切入点，形成了关于什么是有效方法的广泛观点。这种多样性推动了深思熟虑的实验，并为受捐赠者自己测试不同的理论开辟了途径。



[The Navigation Fund](https://www.navigation.org/), a grantmaking organization working across multiple issue areas, focuses on building the animal protection movement’s long-term power. It not only funds major efforts like cage-free campaigns but also works to strengthen leadership, organizational capacity, youth engagement, and narrative influence. By doing so, it helps to propel larger and more durable wins for animals over time.

[导航基金（The Navigation Fund）](https://www.navigation.org/) 是一个在多个问题领域开展工作的赠款组织，重点关注建立动物保护运动的长期力量。它不仅资助像无笼养运动这样的重大活动，还致力于加强领导力、组织能力、青年参与度和叙事影响力。通过这样做，它有助于在一段时间内为动物赢得更大、更持久的胜利。



Meanwhile, founding Senterra member[ Open Philanthropy](https://www.openphilanthropy.org/)'s core goal is to phase out the worst factory farm practices. To achieve that, it makes sizable, high-return-on-investment donations to global cage-free campaigns and supports longer-term work like the development of—and advocacy for—[new technologies](https://innovateanimalag.org/egg-sexing) to improve animal welfare. The latter includes an [egg sexing technique](https://www.nytimes.com/2024/03/28/climate/chickens-egg-industry-humane.html) that could spare billions of male chicks from being killed inhumanely.

与此同时，Senterra 的创始成员[开放慈善（Open Philanthropy）](https://www.openphilanthropy.org/) 的核心目标是逐步淘汰最落后的工厂化农场做法。为了实现这一目标，它向全球无笼养运动提供了可观的、高投资回报率的捐款，并支持长期工作，如开发和倡导[新技术](https://innovateanimalag.org/egg-sexing)，以改善动物福利。后者包括一种[鸡蛋性别鉴定技术](https://www.nytimes.com/2024/03/28/climate/chickens-egg-industry-humane.html)，可使数十亿雄性小鸡免遭非人道杀害。



These distinct but overlapping approaches have expanded the larger movement’s understanding of what works, what doesn’t, and where new opportunities lie.

这些截然不同但又相互重叠的方法拓展了更大范围的运动，让我们了解了哪些方法有效，哪些方法无效，以及新的机遇在哪里。



### 4. Work to equalize funder and grantee power.

### 4. 努力实现捐赠者与受捐赠者的权力平等。

Giving effectively in any field, but perhaps especially in new and evolving ones, requires that funders bring curiosity and humility to grantee relationships and often directly collaborate with advocates. Getting closer to the advocates’ work creates both stronger relationships and feedback loops that produce more grounded, effective giving.

在任何领域，尤其是在新出现的和不断发展的领域，有效的捐赠都要求捐赠者带着好奇心和谦逊的态度与受捐赠者建立关系，并经常直接与倡导者合作。走近倡导者的工作，既能建立更牢固的关系，又能形成反馈回路，从而产生更扎实、更有效的捐赠。



Many Senterra members actively participate in advocate conferences, including using downtime between sessions to strategize with movement leaders on emerging opportunities and tools (such as how AI could improve data analysis and campaign coordination) and to gather input from advocates (for example, on what types of support services would most strengthen their work).

许多 Senterra 成员积极参与倡导者的会议，包括利用会议间隙时间与运动领袖就新兴机遇和工具（如人工智能如何改善数据分析和行动协调）制定战略，并收集倡导者的意见（如哪类支持服务最能帮助他们的工作）。



Some Senterra funders even run their own organizations and work shoulder-to-shoulder with advocates in coalitions. Member David Meyer, for instance, leads [Food System Innovations](https://fsi.org/), a nonprofit that offers funding, research, and collaborative thought partnership to advocates advancing plant-based proteins and policy solutions.

一些 Senterra 的捐赠者甚至经营着自己的组织，并在联盟中与倡导者并肩工作。例如，成员戴维-迈耶（David Meyer）领导着[食品系统创新](https://fsi.org/)，这是一家非营利组织，为倡导植物蛋白和政策解决方案的倡导者提供资金、研究和合作思想伙伴关系。



### 5. Finance the conditions for progress.

### 5. 为取得进展创造条件。

Effective giving in an under-resourced field requires more than funding programs; it requires building a whole ecosystem around the movement that helps both funders and grantees experiment, take risks, and honestly evaluate their impact.

在资源不足的领域进行有效的捐赠，需要的不仅仅是资助项目，还需要围绕运动建立一个完整的生态系统，帮助捐赠者和受捐赠者进行试验、承担风险并诚实地评估其影响。



This includes funding research on human behavior to inform advocacy. For example, behavioral science research has shown that small, well-designed “nudges” can produce large, lasting shifts in behavior without restricting choice. Our movement increasingly applies this insight to food systems by, for instance, making healthy plant-based dishes the default at universities and hospitals, and repositioning plant-based meats at eye level in grocery stores. These subtle interventions leverage social norms and convenience to normalize more sustainable and humane eating. The results are astounding: Across nearly a dozen [studies](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://faunalytics.org/tactics-in-practice-the-science-of-plant-based-defaults-and-nudges/__;!!G92We9drHetJ8EofZw!eZebme4azr4LHa9gNw5fbFSXR116sOpIYxtt8smlgOO2KTY2NaBF74kVQA82w089_klkxIQWN8RaSR8bkPo3VfJNBGU$), plant-based “defaults” have, on average, tripled the share of vegetarian meals chosen, often with no drop in satisfaction. Philanthropy-backed research groups like [Faunalytics](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://faunalytics.org/__;!!G92We9drHetJ8EofZw!eZebme4azr4LHa9gNw5fbFSXR116sOpIYxtt8smlgOO2KTY2NaBF74kVQA82w089_klkxIQWN8RaSR8bkPo3QIeqXcE$) help advocates put these learnings into practice.

这包括资助有关人类行为的研究，为宣传提供依据。例如，行为科学研究表明，小规模、精心设计的 “轻度推进（nudges）” 可以在不限制选择的情况下产生巨大而持久的行为转变。我们的运动越来越多地将这一观点应用到食品系统中，例如，在大学和医院中默认健康的植物性菜肴，在杂货店中将植物性肉类重新摆放在与视线水平的位置。这些微妙的干预措施利用了社会规范和便利性，使更可持续、更人道的饮食常态化。结果令人震惊： 在近十项[研究](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://faunalytics.org/tactics-in-practice-the-science-of-plant-based-defaults-and-nudges/__;!!G92We9drHetJ8EofZw！eZebme4azr4LHa9gNw5fbFSXR116sOpIYxtt8smlgOO2KTY2NaBF74kVQA82w089_klkxIQWN8RaSR8bkPo3VfJNBGU$)中，以植物为基础的 “默认值” 平均使选择素食的比例增加了两倍，而且满意度往往没有下降。像 [动物研究（Faunalytics）](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://faunalytics.org/__;!!G92We9drHetJ8EofZw!eZebme4azr4LHa9gNw5fbFSXR116sOpIYxtt8smlgOO2KTY2NaBF74kVQA82w089_klkxIQWN8RaSR8bkPo3QIeqXcE$) 这样由慈善机构支持的研究小组帮助倡导者将这些经验付诸实践。

Other funder-supported groups offering movement-wide services include [Animal Charity Evaluators](https://animalcharityevaluators.org/), which conducts in-depth reviews of organizations and interventions; [Animal Advocacy Careers](https://animaladvocacycareers.org/), which helps the movement attract top talent by offering free career support and mentorship; and [Animal Defense Partnership](https://www.animaldefensepartnership.org/), which provides pro bono legal counsel to nonprofits so that they can focus on mission-critical work. “Meta-organizations” like these help push limited funding further by making smarter use of every dollar and sharing learning.

其他由捐赠者支持、为整个运动提供服务的组织包括：[动物慈善评估者（Animal Charity Evaluators）](https://animalcharityevaluators.org/)，曾对组织和干预措施进行深入审查；[动物倡导职业（Animal Advocacy Careers）](https://animaladvocacycareers.org/)，通过提供免费的职业支持和指导，帮助运动吸引顶尖人才；以及[动物保护合作组织（Animal Defense Partnership）](https://www.animaldefensepartnership.org/)，为非营利组织提供无偿法律咨询，使其能够专注于关键任务。像这样的 “元组织 ”通过更明智地利用每一分钱和分享学习成果，帮助进一步推动有限的资金。



## Write Your Own Playbook

## 构建自己的战术（把握自己的道路）

Building a more humane and sustainable global food system is a massive, complicated challenge with multiple solutions. One new technology or direct service won’t make a substantial difference on its own. Yet in the case of intensive animal agriculture—a root cause of many of today’s global problems—this complexity is forcing interested funders and advocates to be savvy, creative, collaborative, and resourceful, both in how they tackle the problem and how they fund it.

建立一个更人道、更可持续的全球粮食系统是一个巨大而复杂的挑战，需要多种解决方案。一项新技术或直接服务本身不会带来实质性的改变。然而，在集约化畜牧业（当今许多全球性问题的根源）的现状下，这种复杂性迫使感兴趣的捐赠者和倡导者在解决问题的方式和资助方式上都要精明、有创造力、善于合作、足智多谋。



Most new, complicated, niche, or unglamorous causes have the enterprising spirit they need to build a community and infrastructure that allows even modest investments to move whole systems, not just address symptoms. And when donors work together and funding is well placed, every dollar stands to go extraordinarily far.

大多数新出现的、复杂的、小众的或不光彩的事业都需要有进取精神，以建立一个社区和基础设施，即使是微不足道的投资，也能推动整个系统的发展，而不仅仅是治标。如果捐助方齐心协力，资金安排得当，每一分钱都能发挥巨大的作用。
