# Local Community Education, Everywhere

# 社区教育，遍地开花

With global education funding under severe strain, volunteer-based approaches to community-led education offer a cost-effective and sustainable model for progress.

在全球教育资金面临严峻压力之际，以志愿者为基础的社区主导教育的模式，为教育事业的发展提供了经济高效且可持续的解决方案。

![volunteer sits with student in the grass outside a school](https://ssir.org/images/jcogs_img/cache/volunteer-led-community-based-education-592x333_-_abcdef_-_ec76adfb59d23efa05b4d7ecdd5601e6950c9f26.webp)

[A Building Tomorrow Community Education Volunteer works with a learner at St. Kizito Kijaguzo Primary School in Nakaseke District, Central Uganda.](https://ssir.org/images/blog/volunteer-led-community-based-education-592x333.jpg)

一位 “建设明天” 的社区教育志愿者正在乌干达中部纳卡塞克区的圣基齐托·基贾古佐小学指导一名学生。



When 8-year-old Gloria arrived at her first day of a Building Tomorrow “[Roots to Rise](https://www.buildingtomorrow.org/rootstorise/)” literacy camp in Iganga District, Uganda, she was (like [over 90 percent](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/925741559163051034/pdf/Economic-Development-and-Human-Capital-in-Uganda-A-Case-for-Investing-More-in-Education.pdf) of her Ugandan peers) struggling to master the basics of reading, writing, and arithmetic. Over the course of a few weeks, guided by a community volunteer, Gloria began sounding out words, forming sentences, and even comprehending short paragraphs. More than that: She was learning to be joyful again. Gloria found herself not only helping her classmates but dreaming of becoming a teacher herself.

当8岁的格洛丽亚来到乌干达伊干加区参加 “建设明天（Building Tomorrow）” 组织的 “生根发芽（Roots to Rise）” 扫盲营首日活动时，她苦于掌握读写算术一类的基本技能——和[超过90%](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/925741559163051034/ pdf/Economic-Development-and-Human-Capital-in-Uganda-A-Case-for-Investing-More-in-Education.pdf)的乌干达当地同龄人一样。短短数周内，在社区志愿者的引导下，格洛丽亚开始拼读单词、构造句子，甚至能理解简短段落。更重要的是：她重新找回了快乐。格洛丽亚不仅会帮助同学，更还梦想着自己会成为教师。



Today, across sub-Saharan Africa, nearly [nine out of 10](https://uis.unesco.org/en/blog/new-data-reveal-learning-crisis-threatens-development-around-world) children cannot read or write a simple text by age 10, a condition the World Bank terms "[learning poverty](https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/education/brief/what-is-learning-poverty)." The obstacles to reversing this are substantial. The region already faces a shortfall of nearly [15 million teachers](https://news.un.org/en/story/2024/02/1147067#:~:text=Although it's a global issue,teachers are needed by 2030.), declining [education spending](https://www.cgdev.org/blog/why-africa-shouldnt-sell-itself-short-education) as a share of GDP, and deepening post-COVID [learning gaps](https://www.washingtonpost.com/education/2024/07/23/covid-test-scores-learning-loss-absenteeism/), but the crisis has been further intensified by the recent cancellation of [99](https://www.cgdev.org/blog/usaid-cuts-new-estimates-country-level) percent of USAID’s global commitments to basic education, amounting to an estimated $1 billion in cuts. In low- and middle-income countries, these cuts come as ministries are already stretching limited budgets across multiple urgent sectors, often against the backdrop of [double-digit percentage losses](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/06/cuts-in-official-development-assistance_e161f0c5/8c530629-en.pdf) in bilateral aid revenue.

如今，在撒哈拉以南的非洲地区，近[九成]((https://uis.unesco.org/en/blog/new-data-reveal-learning-crisis-threatens-development-around-world) )的儿童在10岁时仍无法阅读和书写简单的文本，世界银行将此称为“学习贫困（Learning Poverty）”。改善这一现状面临巨大的困难。该地区已存在近[1500万的教师缺口](https://news.un.org/en/story/2024/02/1147067#:~:text=Although it's a global issue,teachers are needed by 2030.)， 教育支出占GDP比重持续下降，以及疫情后日益扩大的学习差距。而美国国际开发署近期取消了全球基础教育承诺的[99](https://www.cgdev.org/blog/usaid-cuts-new-estimates-country-level)%，削减资金约达10亿美元，使危机进一步加剧。在中低收入国家，这些削减措施出台之际，各部委正竭力将有限预算分配到多个紧急领域，而双边援助收入（bilateral aid revenue）往往已出现[两位数的百分比损失]((https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/06/cuts-in-official-development-assistance_e161f0c5/8c530629-en.pdf) )。



Gloria represents not only the potential of more than [600 million](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/617-million-children-and-adolescents-not-getting-minimum-reading-and-math) learners worldwide who lack foundational learning skills, but her progress was the result of a promising but underutilized approach for delivering education, a community-led and volunteer-based model that could be the key to solving the global education crisis.

格洛丽亚不仅代表着全球超过 [6亿](https://www.unesco.org/en/ articles/617-million-children-and-adolescents-not-getting-minimum-reading-and-math) 缺乏基础的学习技能的学习者，更体现了一种充满希望却未被充分利用的教育模式——这种由社区主导、志愿者参与的模式，或许正是解决全球教育危机的关键所在。



## The Power of Proximity

## 亲近的力量

To date, more than 900,000 Ugandan children like Gloria have participated in Roots to Rise camps, guided by a grassroots network of over 20,000 Community Education Volunteers. Trained through decentralized models and rooted in local communities, these volunteers demonstrate what other sectors, such as health and livelihoods, have already taught social impact practitioners: proximity and purpose can deliver powerful results. As a scalable, sustainable solution, community-powered education models offer a compelling answer to the intertwined crises of student academic underperformance and the ever-widening teacher shortages in LMICs.

迄今已有超过90万名像格洛丽亚这样的乌干达儿童参与了“生根发芽”夏令营，这些活动由超过2万名社区教育志愿者组成的基层网络指导开展。这些志愿者通过去中心化模式接受培训，扎根于当地社区，向社会影响力实践者展现了医疗和生计等领域早已验证的真理：亲近感与使命感能创造非凡成效。作为可扩展的可持续解决方案，社区驱动的教育模式为低收入和中等收入国家（LMICs）在学生学业表现不佳与教师短缺加剧的双重危机下提供了有力对策。



While community-powered solutions are not groundbreaking, their deployment can be. In Latin America, for example, [TECHO](https://techo.org/) mobilizes tens of thousands of young people to address housing and livelihoods through a three-phase model that centers local agency, volunteer-led advocacy, and partnership to transform informal settlements. Working across 19 Latin American and Caribbean countries, nearly 750,000 volunteers have built over 100,000 homes, not only putting a roof over the heads of families in informal settlements but fostering a deep sense of dignity and civic participation.

社区驱动型解决方案虽非创新之举，其实施方式却可突破常规。以拉丁美洲为例，[TECHO](https://techo.org/) 组织动员数万青年通过三阶段模式解决住房与生计问题：以地方自主权为核心，依靠志愿者倡导推动，通过伙伴关系改造非正规定居点。该组织在19个拉丁美洲及加勒比国家开展工作，近75万名志愿者已建造超过10万套住房，不仅为非正规定居点家庭提供了栖身之所，更培育了深厚的尊严感与公民参与意识。



In western Africa, [Tostan](https://tostan.org/)’s lauded Community Empowerment Program (CEP) flows out of an intensive, three-year deep dive process, in which communities engage in dialogue, craft their collective vision, and design a development agenda of their own. Tostan’s model has resulted in impressive behavior change, including significant reductions in child marriage, female genital cutting, and other health indicators. Each community’s collective declaration is a testament to the power of proximate engagement: when the people closest to a challenge are equipped with the tools to lead a transformation, change endures.

在西非，[托斯坦组织](https://tostan.org/)备受赞誉的社区赋权计划（Community Empowerment Program，CEP）源于为期三年的深度深入过程：社区通过对话凝聚共识，共同制定发展蓝图。该模式促成显著的行为转变，包括大幅减少童婚、女性生殖器切割（FGC）等现象，并改善多项健康指标。每个社区的集体宣言都印证了亲近参与的力量：当那些身处困境之人获得引领变革的工具时，变革才更能持久。



Volunteer-led, community-rooted models are among the social impact sector’s most successful interventions, and the current global state of education requires the sector to harness the same collective imagination to stem the tide.

志愿者主导、社区扎根的模式是社会影响力领域最成功的干预方式之一。当前全球教育现状要求该领域凝聚同样的集体智慧，以遏制危机浪潮。



## CEVs in Education

## 教育领域的社区教育志愿者

At [Building Tomorrow](https://www.buildingtomorrow.org/), an education-focused social impact organization, our Community Education Volunteer model catalyzes education through the formation of locally-identified CEV networks, which boost a community’s available assets toward education. With training and pedagogical support from Building Tomorrow, CEVs enroll out-of-school children, champion inclusion of populations such as girls and children with disabilities in education spaces, build the capacity of school leaders, and facilitate Building Tomorrow’s Teaching at the Right Level-inspired literacy and numeracy camps called Roots to Rise. No two communities will do this in exactly the same way, but a common thread in our experience is that when communities have increased access to training and pedagogical materials, local participants can draw on their existing connections and place-based understanding of students’ environments to impart change and shift mindsets. The results speak for themselves: reducing learning poverty for nearly two-thirds of participants in the over 25,000 literacy and numeracy camps since 2022.

专注教育领域的社会影响力组织 [建设明天](https://www.buildingtomorrow.org/)，在我们的社区教育志愿者模式通过建立本地化的志愿者网络来推动教育发展，这些网络能有效调动社区现有资源投入教育事业。在“建设明天”的培训与教学支持下，社区教育志愿者致力于：招募失学儿童入学；推动女孩及残障儿童等群体的教育包容性；提升校领导管理能力；并协助开展“生根发芽”项目——该项目借鉴“适性教学”理念，通过读写与计算能力训练营提升教育质量。每个社区的实践路径各不相同，但经验表明：当社区获得更多培训资源与教学材料时，当地参与者便能依托既有社会关系及对学生成长环境的本土认知，推动观念转变与变革。成效不言自明——自2022年以来，在超过25,000场读写计算训练营中，近三分之二参与者的学习贫困状况得到改善。



The impact of our CEVs has been validated by a [randomized controlled trial](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31208) (RCT) conducted by Youth Impact, which remarkably showed that less than three hours of individualized instruction by CEVs led to learning gains equivalent to more than one school year of classroom instruction! CEVs accurately targeted instruction to learners’ ability level 81.5 percent of the time, a higher percentage than any other implementers (all of whom were professionally trained) studied in the RCT’s five replication contexts. The recipe for this success is no secret; CEVs were able to leverage the trust they’ve built within the communities they serve to increase learner engagement and parental involvement for vastly improved outcomes.

青年影响组织开展的[随机对照试验（randomized controlled trial, RCT）](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31208)验证了社区教育志愿者的成效——惊人的数据显示：志愿者仅需提供不到三小时的个性化指导，其学习成效就相当于超过一学年课堂教学！志愿者81.5%的教学精准匹配学员能力水平，该比例高于RCT研究中五个复制场景中所有受训实施者（均受过专业培训）。成功的秘诀并非秘密：志愿者依托在服务社区建立的信任关系，有效提升学员参与度和家长参与度，从而实现显著成效。



Across low- and middle-income countries, organizations are already independently deploying volunteer-based education models that show promise. [Educate Girls](https://www.educategirls.ngo/) in India harnesses community volunteers to identify and enroll out-of-school girls; [ARED](https://ared-edu.org/en/) in Senegal leverages community tutors; Kenya’s [Zizi Afrique](https://ziziafrique.org/) and the [Learning Generative Initiative](https://learninggeneration.org/) are both experimenting with community facilitators in foundational skills acquisition. These approaches vary in design, but they share a belief in the value of community ownership, cost-efficiency, and sustainability. Moreover, Educate Girls successfully enrolled over [245,000 learners](https://www.educategirls.ngo/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Annual_Report_2022-23_website.pdf) into school in 2022-2023 (and their goal is to improve access and quality of education for over [15 million children](https://www.educategirls.ngo/who-we-are/) cumulatively by 2025); these numbers should do away with any misconception that community-driven change is confined to small-scale interventions.

在中低收入国家，各类组织正独立推行充满潜力的志愿者教育模式：印度的[培养女孩组织（Educate Girls）](https://www.educategirls.ngo/)借助社区志愿者识别并招募失学女童；塞内加尔的[ARED](https://ared-edu.org/en/)项目依托社区辅导员； 肯尼亚的[非洲之根（Zizi Afrique）](https://ziziafrique.org/)与[学习世代倡议（Learning Generative Initiative，LGI）](https://learninggeneration.org/)则在基础技能培养领域尝试社区辅导员模式。这些方案设计各异，但都认同社区自主权、成本效益及可持续性的价值。此外，“培养女孩” 组织在2022-2023学年成功让超过[24.5万名学习者](https://www.educategirls.ngo/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/ Annual_Report_2022-23_website.pdf) 入学（其目标是到2025年累计为超过[1500万儿童](https://www.educategirls.ngo/who-we-are/)改善教育机会与质量）； 这些数据足以破除“社区驱动变革仅限于小规模干预”的误解。



While community-led solutions can flourish independently, they often reach their full potential only when brought together in shared purpose, accelerating government policy. No greater example exists than in public health where community health worker coalitions, like those such as the [Community Health Impact Coalition](https://joinchic.org/), have advanced shared standards, secured government buy-in, and scaled best practices on a global scale. Similarly, in environmental justice, the [Amazon Headwaters Sacred Alliance](https://sacredheadwaters.org/) brought diverse local groups together in their fight to protect millions of acres of rainforest, strengthening their leverage.

社区主导的解决方案虽可独立蓬勃发展，但唯有汇聚共同目标时才能充分发挥潜力，从而加速政府政策推进。公共卫生领域便是最佳例证：诸如[社区健康影响联盟（Community Health Impact Coalition）](https://joinchic.org/)等社区卫生工作者联盟，在全球范围内推动了共同标准的制定、获得了政府支持，并实现了最佳实践的规模化推广。同样在环境正义领域，[亚马逊源头神圣联盟（Amazon Headwaters Sacred Alliance）](https://sacredheadwaters.org/)汇聚多元地方团体，共同守护数百万英亩雨林，显著增强了行动影响力。



Education must follow suit, because [the future is collective](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/collective-action-approach-social-innovation). A coalition approach offers more than alignment; it enables co-learning, representation, and systems-level coordination. It allows for shared principles, open methods, and interoperable tools. It provides the structure for community-based models to shape—not just supplement—education systems.

教育领域必须效仿此举，因为[未来属于集体](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/collective-action-approach-social-innovation)。联盟模式不仅实现目标协同，更促进共同学习、多元代表与系统级协调。它建立共享原则、开放方法和交互式操作工具，为社区主导模式提供基础框架——使之成为教育体系的塑造者而非补充者。



To build such a coalition, we propose grounding our efforts in a common vision: that every child, regardless of geography or wealth, deserves to master basic literacy and numeracy. Ample proof exists that we can get there. CEVs have delivered impressive learning gains through Roots to Rise with approximately 80 percent of learners reaching minimum proficiency levels in literacy and numeracy. And while CEVs now reach a new Ugandan learner every 90 seconds, far more work lies ahead. To register such gains at scale and with quality, a community education volunteer approach should:

为构建此类联盟，我们倡议以共同愿景为基石：无论地域或财富差异，每个孩子都应掌握基础读写与计算能力。大量证据表明我们能够实现这一目标。社区教育志愿者通过“生根发芽”项目取得了显著的学习成效，约80%的学习者达到了读写与计算的最低能力水平。尽管社区教育志愿者如今每90秒就能为乌干达新增一名学习者，但仍有大量工作亟待完成。为实现大规模、高质量的教育成果，社区教育志愿者模式应：

- Emerge organically from within communities with local buy-in and leadership;
- 在社区内部自然形成，获得当地支持与领导力；
- Leverage existing skill sets among teachers, youth, retirees, and civic leaders;
- 充分利用教师、青年、退休人员及社区领袖的现有技能；
- Complement and strengthen formal systems, not compete with them;
- 补充并强化正规教育体系，而非与其竞争；
- Be accountable to both communities and learners through data and feedback;
- 通过数据与反馈机制向社区和学习者负责；
- Be designed for cost-efficiency, adaptability, and replication.
- 注重成本效益、适应性和可复制性。



The moment is urgent. The opportunity is real. As organizations, funders, policy makers, and advocates, we must resist the instinct to go it alone. The education crisis will not be solved by isolated pilots or siloed innovations, but it might be met by a committed, collective, community-powered movement.

时不我待，机遇真实存在。作为组织、资助方、政策制定者和倡导者，我们必须摒弃单打独斗的本能。孤立的试点项目或割裂的创新无法解决教育危机，唯有凝聚社区力量的集体行动方能应对挑战。



We welcome the opportunity to co-create this movement with like-minded peers—toward a future where every child learns, and every community leads.

我们期待与志同道合的伙伴共同开创这场变革——迈向每个孩子都能学习、每个社区都能引领的未来。
