# No Straight Lines: Making Sense of Our Non-linear World

# 没有直线： 认识我们的非线性世界

The following is an excerpt from the book. 

以下内容摘自本书。

By [Alan Moore](https://ssir.org/books/excerpts/entry/no_straight_lines_making_sense_of_our_non-linear_world#bio-footer) Sep. 11, 2012

作者 艾伦-摩尔 2012年9月11日

### The challenge: be realistic, imagine the impossible

### 挑战：脚踏实地，想象不可能

What do these have in common? A car company built around a global community as an organisation, enabled by combining flex manufacturing techniques, open source platforms, open legal frameworks and social communication technologies premised upon cooperation, fuelled by the desire to be a great company and green; that can build cars 5 times faster at 100 times less the capital costs. A crisis management platform and organisation born out of the Kenyan post-election crisis of 2008 that can record critical information of events unfolding on the ground via a blend of location-based data, eyewitness accounts and mobile telephony, from often hard to reach places which visualises those unfolding events so that others can act and direct action at internet speeds. And now utilised for free in many parts of the world. Or, the largest organic diary farm in Britain, that have evolved a methodology that allows them to remain autonomous, profitable and sustainable in a market that is acutely volatile, because large-scale agricultural farming is mostly run on an oil-based economy, plus diary farmers are at the calculating mercy of the marketing needs and whimsys of large chain supermarkets.

以下案例的共同点是什么？一家以全球社区为组织核心的汽车公司，通过结合柔性制造技术、开源平台、开放式法律框架和基于合作的社交通信技术，以成为一家伟大且环保的公司为愿景，它能以低于 100 倍的资本成本将造车速度提高 5 倍。在 2008 年肯尼亚大选后危机中诞生的危机管理平台和组织，可以通过基于位置的数据、目击者的描述及移动电话，从通常难以到达的地方记录展开事件的关键信息，并将这些正在发生的事件可视化，以便其他人能够借助互联网及时采取行动并指导行动。现在，世界上许多地方都在免费使用这种技术。再者，英国最大的有机奶制品农场，他们已经形成了一种方法，使他们能够在一个剧烈波动的市场中保持自主、盈利和可持续发展，因为大规模的农业耕作主要是以石油为基础的经济，以及奶制品农场主受大型连锁超市的营销需求和运营想法的支配。



They are collectively representative of a new reality of living, working and organising. All organisations or companies have quested to find a means to serve humanity better, to search for meaning in the work that they and others do, and offer up new viable alternatives for the ways that, in the past, these things were done. They seek an outcome that is more distributive of wealth, ideas and resources. In fact, one might argue an outcome that is more humane and community centric. An ungrading to what I call the Human-OS (operating system) This OS wants greater opportunity, greater freedom, greater empowerment, a revitalized sense of justice, a world where mutualism and participatory cultures are the default setting, where openness is seen as resilience and diversity is understood as a good thing, where we have greater autonomy and that seeks a greater aesthetic in everything we do: beautiful buildings, civic spaces, organizational design, it is as easy to make something beautiful as it is to make it ugly. So why choose the latter over the former? That question has always baffled me other than knowing ugly thoughts realize ugly realities.

它们共同代表了一种生活、工作和组织的新现实。所有组织或公司都在寻求更好的方式为人类服务，在自己和他人所做的工作中寻找意义，并为过去的工作方式提供新的、可行的替代方案。他们寻求一种更好分配财富、思想和资源的结果。事实上，人们可能会说，这样的结果更加人性化，更加以社区为中心。这种操作系统希望有更多的机会、更广泛的自由、更强的能力、焕然一新的正义感、一个以互助主义和参与式文化为默认设置的世界、一个开放被视为有韧性、多样性被理解为好事的世界、一个我们拥有更大自主权的世界，以及一个在我们所做的一切中寻求更美好的世界：美丽的建筑、公民空间、组织设计，让事物变得美丽和变得丑陋一样容易。那么，为什么要选择后者而不是前者呢？这个问题一直困惑着我，我只知道丑陋的思想会带来丑陋的现实。



This OS is the key driver to the systems change we are witnessing. I see this Human-OS in the transformational change of all the examples cited in No Straight Lines: from agriculture, hospital design, and healthcare service design, educational programmes, the response to complex civic challenges, manufacturing, NGO’s, the nature of finance, innovation and commerce itself. This OS is the story of why our networked world with its new Human OS is directing the shape of our post industrial future.

这种操作系统是我们正在目睹的系统变革的关键驱动力。我在《没有直线》一书中列举的所有例子的转型变革中都看到了这种人类操作系统：从农业、热情好客的医院设计、医疗保健服务设计、教育计划、应对复杂的公民挑战、制造业、非政府组织、金融的本质、创新和商业本身。这个操作系统讲述了为什么我们的网络世界及其新的人类操作系统正在引导我们后工业时代的未来。



Rather than premised upon the extraction of wealth, and resources, whether they be physical, mineral or otherwise, these very different initiatives represent both moral courage and a collective purpose, if you will. And why is that important? Because it does not matter if you are an employer, a worker, VC fund, an NGO, an organisation, a local council or a government, you will miss out on the energies and capabilities of your people who will increasingly seek those new realities to discover a better way of living, working and being, when better and viable alternatives are on offer. And the fact is we now have the possibility to truly transform our world, to be more lightweight, sustainable and humane, through the tools, capabilities, language and processes at our fingertips. As Tony Judt argued, ‘why do we experience such difficulty even imaging a different sort of society? Why is it beyond us to conceive a different set of arrangements to our common advantage?’

这些迥然不同的倡议并不是以攫取财富和资源（无论是实物、矿产还是其他资源）为前提，而是代表着道德勇气和集体目标。为什么这很重要？因为不管你是雇主、工人、风险投资基金、非政府组织、机构、地方议会还是政府，你都会错过人民的能量和能力，当有更好、更可行的选择时，他们会越来越多地寻求这些新的现实，以发现更好的生活、工作和生存方式。而事实是，我们现在有可能通过唾手可得的工具、能力、语言和程序，真正改变我们的世界，使之更加轻盈、可持续和人性化。正如托尼-朱特（Tony Judt）所言，"为什么我们连想象一个与众不同的社会都如此困难？为什么我们无法想象出一套对我们共同有利的不同安排？



Which brings me on to the title and the challenge of this project. Be realistic, imagine the impossible is taken from a poster from the 1968 Paris riots. In making sense of its meaning for our time, I would argue that what we face at the tail end of our industrial society is a design problem. The reason is that we are witness to a systemic failure of many of the institutions that have brought us so much prosperity and it is this convergence of failures that requires us to understand the challenge from a whole systems approach.

这就引出了本项目的标题和挑战。现实一点，想象不可能》取自 1968 年巴黎骚乱的一张海报。在理解它对我们这个时代的意义时，我认为我们在工业社会末期所面临的是一个设计问题。原因在于，我们目睹了许多曾为我们带来繁荣的机构的系统性失败，而正是这种失败的汇聚要求我们从整体系统的角度来理解这一挑战。



Many of the institutions, organisations and systems that we still use were designed and built for a less complex world, the increase in the complexity of our world is placing an unsustainable load upon those institutions, organisations and systems. One could argue our industrial world has reached the edge of its adaptive range. Consequently, faultlines are running through our society which present a trilemma based around interlocking social, economic and organisational tensions and questions. The design challenge involved in resolving these questions comes because the non-linearity is causing a comprehensive restructuring of society at large, breaking old models of organisation, and the trilemma heralds the coming of the age of uncertainty. All three tensions are in flux, and cannot be addressed without considering the other two. So each and every part of this story reflects upon and relates to this trilemma: the relationship of the individual to companies and other organisations and forms of power, economically, socially, politically.

我们仍在使用的许多机构、组织和系统都是为一个不那么复杂的世界设计和建造的，我们世界复杂性的增加给这些机构、组织和系统带来了不可持续的负担。可以说，我们的工业世界已经达到了其适应范围的边缘。因此，在我们的社会中出现了一些断层，这些断层围绕着相互交织的社会、经济和组织紧张关系和问题，形成了一个三难困境。解决这些问题所涉及的设计挑战，是因为非线性正在引起整个社会的全面重组，打破旧的组织模式，而三难困境则预示着不确定时代的到来。所有这三种矛盾都在变化之中，如果不考虑其他两种矛盾，就无法解决。因此，本故事的每一部分都反映了这一三难问题，并与之相关：个人与公司及其他组织和权力形式之间在经济、社会和政治方面的关系。

![img](https://ssir.org/images/jcogs_img/cache/chart_no_straight_lines_-_abcdef_-_ec76adfb59d23efa05b4d7ecdd5601e6950c9f26.webp)



Now is the time when we need a way of evaluating of what comes next, when we face a world that has gone in a very short period of time from seemingly linear (simple) to complex and non-linear (chaotic). When we move into a world that is inherently more complex, the result is concussive, its disorientating effects surround us, and our responses either individually or at an organisational level result in reflexes and perspectives that can be dangerously corrosive or inappropriate. And yet, this chaos seems to be, if anything, accelerating. At this very moment, great debates are raging. The spanners are in the works, defined by 9/11 (we now talk about asymmetrical warfare) and the near collapse of the world banking system (and its asymmetrical impact on every single one of us). And, as the global centre of economic gravity moves east, this has set off a series of events that are having significant asymmetric economic effects on societies around the world. These are but three examples of faultlines creating battles, ideological or otherwise, that are exploding and imploding at the same time. They all surfaced in a single decade. Though it is important to add that their gestation period has been much longer and are indeed multidimensional. These challenges are highly interlinked and interdependent, so a one-size-fits-all response just won’t do. There are no longer simple problems; what we face is the trilemma of a complex world. This book does its best to face them, because we are in more than just an economic crisis; it is equally political, educational, spiritual and moral.

现在，当我们面对一个在很短的时间内从看似线性（简单）变得复杂和非线性（混乱）的世界时，我们需要一种评估接下来会发生什么的方法。当我们进入一个本质上更加复杂的世界时，结果是令人震惊的，其迷失方向的影响包围着我们，而我们个人或组织层面的反应导致了可能具有危险腐蚀性或不恰当的反射和观点。然而，这种混乱似乎正在加速。此时此刻，大辩论正在激烈进行。9-11事件（我们现在谈论的是不对称战争）和世界银行体系的濒临崩溃（及其对我们每个人的不对称影响）都在影响着我们。而且，随着全球经济重心东移，引发了一系列事件，对世界各地的社会产生了重大的非对称经济影响。以上只是三个例子，说明意识形态或其他方面的断层正在同时爆发和内爆。它们都是在一个十年内出现的。但必须补充的是，它们的酝酿期更长，而且确实是多层面的。这些挑战高度关联、相互依存，因此，“一刀切 ”的对策是行不通的。我们面临的不再是简单的问题，而是复杂世界的三难问题。本书将尽力面对这些问题，因为我们面临的不仅仅是经济危机，同样还有政治、教育、精神和道德危机。



The biggest challenge we face is cultural. How we contextualise (make sense of) the world around us determines how we engage and what action we take. Those actions then determine the outcomes we must live with and this requires a change from our industrial mindset and behaviour to one that is more cognisant of what is now seen as a non-linear world. This is where I want to return to the idea that what we face is a design problem, where answers exist not at an unattainable theoretical level but on the floors of our factories, in the streets of our towns and cities, the classes of our schools, the waiting rooms of our hospitals. These answers will manifest themselves as true acts of creation, originating new ways of getting stuff done, informed by the decisions we collectively take. So in re-designing the world, we need human creativity in the sense of the capacity to ‘make’, we need visionary leadership in the sense of making a difference. And we seek the craftsman’s critical eye, steady hand and creative mind. It is this process of seeing – realising new pathways to success, by bringing two ‘unlikes’ (new information, tools, processes etc.) together in close adjacency – that we create, and make new things. Then we can meaningfully apply that capability.

我们面临的最大挑战是文化挑战。我们如何看待（理解）周围的世界，决定了我们如何参与和采取何种行动。然后，这些行动决定了我们必须接受的结果，这就要求我们改变工业思维和行为，转而更加认识到现在被视为非线性的世界。在这里，我想回到我们所面临的是一个设计问题的观点，答案并不存在于高不可攀的理论层面，而是存在于我们工厂的车间、城镇的街道、学校的课堂、医院的候诊室。这些答案将体现为真正的创造行为，通过我们集体做出的决定，创造出完成工作的新方法。因此，在重新设计世界的过程中，我们需要 “创造 ”能力意义上的人类创造力，我们需要有所作为意义上的高瞻远瞩的领导力。我们需要工匠的批判性眼光、稳健的双手和创造性思维。正是这种 “洞察 ”的过程--通过将两个 “不喜欢”（新信息、工具、流程等）紧密结合在一起，实现通往成功的新途径--让我们创造和制造新事物。然后，我们才能有意义地应用这种能力。



Why is the idea of craftsmanship significant at this epochal moment in time? Because it is about shaping our future and the ‘engaged’ craftsman brings the full power of humanity to bear upon his work. His hand is guided by his eye, informed by his creative mind; his productivity the act of unique creation. Indeed, the master craftsman is adept in using a philosophical framework, as well as tools and materials, to deliver useful things to the world. But more than that, the craftsman must be open constantly to new ideas; he is essentially always in beta. Therefore, we cannot engage with our uncertain non-linear world with the linear and inflexible orthodoxy of logic alone. The craftsman’s critical eye and creative mind is vital to evaluating new possibilities; he must be open to new ideas, information, tools and materials to make things that enable humanity to flourish. This approach is inherently more creative in that it synthesises all aspects of what make us truly human. But the 21st century craftsman does not only exist in the dusty workshop of a forgotten age; a games designer is a craftsman, a Linux programmer is a craftsman, innovative organisations like Local Motors and Ushahidi, which are discussed in more detail in Chapters 3 and 8, embed craftsmanship into everything they do. These are well designed responses to what real life previously perceived as intractable as the plot line in Catch22.

为什么工匠精神的理念在这个时代具有重要意义？因为这关系到我们未来的塑造，而 “参与其中 ”的工匠会将人类的全部力量都投入到他的工作中。他的手由他的眼睛引导，由他的创造性思维提供信息；他的生产力是独特的创造行为。事实上，工匠大师善于利用哲学框架以及工具和材料，为世界带来有用的东西。但不仅如此，工匠还必须不断接受新思想；从本质上讲，他始终处于测试阶段。因此，我们不能只用线性和僵化的正统逻辑来应对我们这个不确定的非线性世界。工匠的批判性眼光和创造性思维对于评估新的可能性至关重要；他必须对新的想法、信息、工具和材料持开放态度，才能制造出让人类繁荣昌盛的东西。这种方法从本质上讲更具创造性，因为它综合了使我们真正成为人类的各个方面。游戏设计师是工匠，Linux 程序员是工匠，第 3 章和第 8 章将详细讨论的 Local Motors 和 Ushahidi 等创新组织将工匠精神融入他们所做的一切。这些都是精心设计的应对措施，解决了现实生活中以前被认为像《Catch22》中的情节线一样难以解决的问题。



And so I come to this project with a strongly held belief, that there is an opportunity to bring a way of thinking to many of the seemingly intractable problems that confront us today. But this requires us to think and act as craftsmen and women and apply our critical thinking to understanding our non-linear world, which is in part shaped by participatory cultures, open, complex and seemingly ambiguous systems that are highly interdependent of each other. We need to be inspired to be epic, to seek epic wins, to make informed choices and co-author innovative new possibilities that can enable humanity to lead a life not constrained by the crushing reality of industrial-age thinking but one designed around the primary needs of humanity. We need to explore our non-linear world, not exploit it.

因此，我怀着一个坚定的信念来参与这个项目，那就是我们有机会为当今许多看似棘手的问题带来一种思维方式。但是，这需要我们像工匠一样思考和行动，运用我们的批判性思维来理解我们的非线性世界，这个世界在一定程度上是由参与性文化、开放、复杂和看似模棱两可的系统所塑造的，而这些系统又是高度相互依存的。我们需要被激发出史诗般的热情，寻求史诗般的胜利，做出明智的选择，共同创造创新的新可能，从而使人类的生活不再受制于工业时代思维的压迫性现实，而是围绕人类的主要需求而设计。我们需要探索我们的非线性世界，而不是利用它。



I believe there is much evidence demonstrating the possibility of this society. It exists in philosophical frameworks, language and literacy, legal frameworks, tools and technologies, and real stories of how others have been motivated by a real desire to create new and better answers to what others would call unsolvable problems. And it has been my mission to bring together these separate component parts to offer to you a vision of the world which is both realistic and eminently possible. But to create this regenerative society requires us to take a voyage of discovery and to look upon the world as Proust would say with fresh eyes. This is the world of no straight lines and this project is how we make sense of this non-linear world, and then act in it.

我相信有很多证据证明了这种社会的可能性。它存在于哲学框架、语言和读写能力、法律框架、工具和技术中，也存在于他人如何被真正的愿望所激励，去创造新的、更好的答案来解决他人称之为无法解决的问题的真实故事中。我的使命就是将这些独立的组成部分整合在一起，为你们提供一个既现实又极有可能实现的世界愿景。但是，要创建这个再生社会，我们就必须像普鲁斯特所说的那样，踏上探索之旅，用全新的眼光来审视这个世界。这是一个没有直线的世界，而本项目就是我们如何理解这个非线性世界，并在其中采取行动。



*Alan Moore, Cambridge 2011*
