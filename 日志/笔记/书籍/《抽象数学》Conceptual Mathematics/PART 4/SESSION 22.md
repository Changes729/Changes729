**Basic figure-types, singular figures, and incidence, in the category of graphs**

> 基本类型：
>
> $D = \bullet$
>
> $A = \bullet \to \bullet$
>
> 有一个很有用的图：
> $$
> M = \xymatrix @dr{
> \bullet \ar[r] & \bullet \\
> & \bullet \ar[u]
> }
> $$
> 这张图意味着：有两个不同的映射在 A 到 M 中

> $$
> \xymatrix{
> D \ar[r]^t \ar[d]_t & A \ar[d]^{m_1} \\
> A \ar[r]_{m_2} & M 
> }
> $$
>
> which means that mxt = m2t or has the same target as m2.'
>
> 这意味着 mxt = m2t 或与 m2 具有相同的目标。
> $$
> \xymatrix{
> D \ar[r]^t \ar[d]_t & A \ar[d]^{m_1} \ar@/^/[ddr]^{x_1} \\
> A \ar[r]_{m_2} \ar@/_/[rrd]_{x_x} & M \ar[dr]^x \\
> && X
> }
> $$
> there is exactly one figure of shape M in X whose arrow m{  matches with x, and whose arrow m2 matches with x2.
>
> X 中恰好有一个形状为 M 的图形，其箭头 m{ 与 x 匹配，并且其箭头 m2 与 x2 匹配。
