**02 紧迫感和耐心**

> “This work takes patient urgency,” Fuller argues. “If you aren’t patient, you only get illusory change. Lasting change is not possible without community. You may be gone in 5 or 10 years, but the community will still be there. You need a sense of urgency to push the process forward and maintain momentum.” The tension between urgency and patience is a productive tension. Navigating that tension allows leaders and community members to achieve the right level of engagement.
>
> “这项工作需要耐心的紧迫感，”富勒认为。“如果你没有耐心，你只会得到虚幻的变化。没有社区，持久的变革是不可能的。你可能会在5年或10年后离开，但社区仍然会在那里。你需要一种紧迫感来推动这一进程并保持势头。”紧迫感和耐心之间的张力是一种富有成效的张力。驾驭这种紧张关系可以让领导者和社区成员实现适当的参与度。



**03 技术性挑战和适应性挑战**

> Leaders who shift to a new data-driven framework need to manage how various constituencies react to that change. A good way to start is by distinguishing between technical challenges and adaptive challenges. In [The Practice of Adaptive Leadership](https://www.amazon.com/Practice-Adaptive-Leadership-Changing-Organization/dp/1422105768/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1454453504&sr=8-1&keywords=practice+of+adaptive+leadership), Ronald A. Heifetz, Alexander Grashow, and Marty Linsky explain that distinction: “Technical problems … can be resolved through the application of authoritative expertise and through the organization’s current structures, procedures, and ways of doing things. Adaptive challenges can only be addressed through changes in people’s priorities, beliefs, habits, and loyalties.”16 For leaders, it’s tempting to focus on straightforward technical challenges (such as developing criteria for funding a data-driven intervention) and to neglect pressing adaptive challenges (such as dealing with changes in relationships and behaviors that staff members, partners, and service recipients will experience with the rollout of that intervention).
>
> 转向新的数据驱动框架的领袖们需要管理不同群体对这一变革的反应。一个好的开始方式是区分技术性挑战和适应性挑战。在《适应性领导力实践》中，罗纳德・A・海菲茨、亚历山大・格拉肖和马蒂・林斯基解释了这种区别：“技术性问题…… 可以通过应用权威专业知识以及通过组织当前的结构、程序和做事方式来解决。适应性挑战只能通过改变人们的优先事项、信念、习惯和忠诚度来解决。” 对于领袖们来说，专注简单的技术性挑战很容易（例如制定资助数据驱动干预措施的标准），进而忽视紧迫的适应性挑战（例如应对工作人员、合作伙伴和服务接受者在推出该干预措施时将经历的关系和行为变化）。
