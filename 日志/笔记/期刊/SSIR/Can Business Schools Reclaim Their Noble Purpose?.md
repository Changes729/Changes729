# Can Business Schools Reclaim Their Noble Purpose?

## 关键词

- Business School（商学院）

  - 新古典经济学（neoclassical economics）：共同的主张是支持自由市场经济、个人理性选择，并且反对政府过度干预。代表人物如<u>凯恩斯、马歇尔</u>

- [联合国负责任管理教育原则（PRME，Principles for Responsible Management Education）](https://www.unprme.org/)

  于 2007 年在日内瓦全球契约领导人峰会上正式成立，旨在激发和支持全球责任管理教育、研究和实践。

- [国际商管学院促进协会（AACSB）](https://www.aacsb.edu/)

  - 第9项标准：参与度与社会影响力（Engagement and Societal Impact）

- 佛蒙特大学格罗斯曼商学院院长桑杰·夏尔马（Sanjay Sharma）& 安德鲁·霍夫曼（Andrew Hoffman）

  - 鞍囊式可持续发展（saddlebag）

## 摘记

**01 旧有商学院的固有问题**

> their **curricula（课程）** have <u>been dominated（主导） by</u> **neoclassical economics（新自由主义经济学）** and its **fixation（固定）** on efficiency, profit maximization, and **shareholder value（股东价值）**. The **lure（诱惑）** of short-termism and the simplicity of **quarterly earnings growth（季度收益增长）** and stock price as the prime objectives of business have drowned out other critical and more nuanced views that should be taught in the classroom: 
>
> - the legal perspective, which clarifies that shareholders are not **principals（负责人）** and that business leaders are not legally required to maximize profit; 
> - the organizational perspective, which holds that companies are human-centered organizations where shared purpose, culture, and values matter; 
> - and the environmental perspective, which **emphasizes（强调）** that economies are dependent on and subsidiary to the environment, not the other way around. The **predominant curriculum（主要课程）** also views government as an intrusion into the free market, **downplaying（淡化）** the government’s essential role as rule setter and **regulator（监管者）**.

> business school curricula “are not preparing future leaders with the skills, knowledge, and wisdom they will need to deal with planetary challenges such as climate change and social challenges at home such as widening income inequality.”

> However, most such efforts have resulted in what University of Vermont Grossman School of Business **Dean（院长，系主任）** Sanjay Sharma and I have called “saddlebag” sustainability—centers, institutes, workshops, and elective courses dedicated to these issues that “hang off the side,” with little change to the underlying core curriculum of business education.

原文 saddle-bag 出自于作者 斯图尔特·L·哈特 的文献——Beyond “Saddle Bag” Sustainability for Business Education，其中解释到：“实际上所有这些倡议，中心，或研究所继续只是挂在现有商学院大楼的一侧。就像谚语所说的马的“鞍袋”一样，问题被放在单独的隔间里，从外面很容易看到，但是对动物本身的行为影响不大。”

文中我翻译为“面子工程”。



> Yet, Hoffman contends, they largely ignore the reality that “we are talking about an existential threat to life on Earth or the moral reality that it is the people of the rich countries that are primarily responsible for climate change and the people of the poor countries who will feel its effects first and hardest.” He concludes that “making the business case [alone] … is not just absurd, it’s sociopathic.”





**02 商学院新的思想**

> Hoffman’s book rightly recognizes the *systemic* nature of the challenge—of transforming the core curriculum to prepare students for careers with purpose—and the urgent need for ***pedagogical（教育）*** innovation to teach the “why” of business as well as the “how”.

> Hoffman’s **prescriptions（处方）** for changing business school education begin with questioning the existing wisdom and dominant narratives that have shaped curricula for the past 40 years. He points to the **inadequacy（不足）** and **unintended consequences（意外后果）** associated with established business precepts such as **discount rate（贴现率）**, growth and consumption, profit maximization, and zero-sum competition.

>  Today’s business students are “**demanding（要求）** an education that equips them to solve society’s challenges through commerce,” rather than simply make more money. 

> Hoffman stresses the idea that management must once again become a vocation, like medicine or law, driven by a set of ethical commitments to benefit society. The current student obsession with “**résumé virtues**” to land the highest-paying job must give way to more of a focus on “**eulogy virtues**” aimed at making lasting positive contributions to the world.

文章——[Eulogy Versus Resume Virtues](https://www.awakin.org/v2/read/view.php?tid=1083) 人生美德和履历美德。前者是一个人去世后所世人所给予的评价，而后者是一个人工作时所需要的对自身的评价。



**03 时代契机**

> As a professor who has spent the past 30-plus years trying to realize such a transformation, I believe our current times could not be “thinner,” thus heightening the **urgency（紧迫感）** for business and business leaders to step up.
>
> Examples of such programs exist, such as the University of Vermont Grossman School of Business’ Sustainable Innovation MBA Program (where I served as cofounder and director). At Vermont, we created a new business model for MBA education by **reinventing（重塑）** not only the curriculum but also the marketing and **recruitment（招聘）** process, as well as the career-development approach (focused on **corporate transformation（企业转型）**, purpose-driven companies, and impact investing—not Wall Street and mainstream consulting). In my view, the current MBA model shaped by shareholder primacy is outdated—most **prospective（未来的，可遇期的）** Gen Z business students aren’t even sure capitalism can be **salvaged（被抢救）**. The time is thus **ripe（成熟）** for disruption and innovation.



**04 理想未来**

> **Faculty（教师）** must stop the **obsession（痴迷）** with **exclusively（专门）** transmitting technical expertise—filling business students’ minds with more quantitative tools, methods, and **metrics（度量，绩效指标）**—and pay more attention to the ends that business seeks. 
>
> Faculty should **attune（使协调）** their **pedagogy（教育法）** so as to function more as “*guides* rather than simply experts” to help their students **cultivate（培育）** their own best selves. 
>
> For Hoffman, this guidance involves teaching to the entire person, developing the virtues of wisdom, character, and purpose—going “back to the future” to when business schools were first created with a strong focus on ethics and serving “the common good.”

> As Hoffman notes, “When political processes are ‘thin,’ the opportunity to distort capitalism is strong and business leaders have responsibility to correct the system. They should sacrifice easy profits from exploiting market flaws and support healthy institutions that are beneficial in the long run because they reinforce capitalism’s legitimacy and the existence of a market society.”



**05 现存问题**

> Despite his **passionate entreaties（充满激情的恳求）** for **transformative change（变革性的改变）** to business education, Hoffman stops short of offering a comprehensive set of practical strategies for achieving such systemic innovation.

> Today’s shareholder-first business education is **bolstered（被支撑着）** by a larger ecosystem, including an academic reward system based on scholarly publishing (rather than teaching innovation), **corporate recruiting（企业招聘）** and career development focused on Wall Street and **consulting（咨询业）** (rather than sustainable innovation by companies), and business school rankings focused on starting salaries for graduates (rather than how well programs prepare students for the future). **The current business school business model makes it difficult for a single professor, dean, or school to realize the necessary change alone.**



## 思路

本文围绕商学院教育改革为核心展开，围绕旧有商学院教育理念存在的固有问题，新思想的切入，时代契机和理想未来几个方面展开，并在最后依旧提出了反思。
