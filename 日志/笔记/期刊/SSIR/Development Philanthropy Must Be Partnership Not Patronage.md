# Development Philanthropy Must Be Partnership Not Patronage

## 关键词





## 摘记

**01 科学公益**

> In the future we must build towards, philanthropic capital should behave like purpose-driven risk equity: money invested to surface what works, prove it quickly, and pave the way for larger, longer-term financing. For receivers, this requires a muscular stance that treats data not as a bureaucratic burden but as the passport to serious funding. **Unit costs, counterfactuals, time-bound targets, and independent validations must become standard for every grant report.**
>
> 在未来，我们必须建立，慈善资本应该像目的驱动的风险资产一样行事：投资的钱是为了展示什么是有效的，迅速证明它，并为更大，更长期的融资铺平道路。对于接收者来说，这需要一种强硬的立场，即不把数据视为官僚负担，而是将其视为获得重大资金的通行证。**单位成本、反事实、有时限的目标和独立验证必须成为每份赠款报告的标准。**



**02 慈善事业的三个规范**

> 1. Donors must commit to "first-loss" positions, using their capital to de-risk pilot projects so that governments or commercial lenders can scale what works.
>    捐助者必须承诺采取“第一损失”立场，利用其资本降低试点项目的风险，以便政府或商业贷款人能够扩大有效的规模。
> 2. Grantees must treat learning as deliverable number one, publishing negative results alongside successes so that the whole field advances.
>    受赠者必须将学习视为第一号交付成果，在成功的同时公布负面结果，以便整个领域取得进步。
> 3. Both parties must embrace time-bound partnerships, understanding that sunset clauses are not failures but milestones that signal when catalytic capital has completed its job.
>    双方都必须接受有时限的伙伴关系，理解日落条款不是失败，而是标志着催化资本何时完成其工作的里程碑。



**02.1 案例**

> This playbook already shows promise in women-led funds channeling resources to girls’ education, in climate-tech ventures blending foundation grants with concessionary loans, and in community foundations where local residents vote on allocation. Each example pushes philanthropy away from noblesse oblige and toward co-creation.
> 这本剧本已经在以下方面显示出了希望：妇女领导的基金将资源引导到女孩的教育中;将基金会赠款与优惠贷款相结合的气候技术企业;以及当地居民投票决定分配的社区基金会。每一个例子都将慈善事业从“义务”推向了“共同创造”。

它展示了慈善如何从传统的“我帮你”转向“我们一起决定资源如何使用”，强调了真正的 **共创（co-creation）与社区赋权（community empowerment）**。
