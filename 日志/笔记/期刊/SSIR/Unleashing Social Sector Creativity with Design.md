## 摘记

> They require that organizations reorient their work so that it focuses on their overall impact goal, not just the individual beneficiary at the center of the innovation project. 



> **Handing over the authorship of micro-trials to the community
> 将微试验的作者权交给社区**
>
> The need to get everyone involved doesn’t stop at the prototyping or “making” phase of design thinking. Prototyping comes out of the idea of experiments, where a researcher or designer goes in and tests something, then interprets the results. However, that process doesn’t work as well in the social sector, given the sector’s complex ecosystems, wide range of stakeholders and variables, and sensitivity to both perceived and actual “do no harm” requirements. Doing a single-variable probe (such as you would attempt in a randomized control trial) as a test to see what happens is too simplistic. The idea—the prototype—needs to live as part of the community, and requires that innovators and designers hand over control and authorship of the prototype to the community—something many of them find hard to do.
> 让每个人都参与进来的需要并不止于设计思维的原型或“制作”阶段。原型设计来自实验的想法，研究人员或设计师进入并测试某些东西，然后解释结果。然而，这一进程在社会部门并不奏效，因为该部门的生态系统复杂，利益攸关方和变量范围广泛，对感知和实际的“不伤害”要求都很敏感。做一个单变量的探测（比如你在随机对照试验中尝试的）作为一个测试，看看会发生什么，这太简单了。这个想法原型需要作为社区的一部分存在，并且要求创新者和设计师将原型的控制权和作者权交给社区，这是他们中的许多人很难做到的。



> **Bringing civic designers inside将城市设计师带入室内**
>
> In the for-profit world, some companies, mostly product-based companies, expect all their employees to innovate. But that’s unrealistic for service-based organizations where time and resources are scarce. Large nonprofits might decide to hire outside help and bring the innovations inside once developed, but that approach is more difficult for small and mid-sized organizations.
> 在营利性世界中，一些公司，主要是以产品为基础的公司，希望所有员工都能创新。但这对于时间和资源稀缺的基于服务的组织来说是不现实的。大型非营利组织可能会决定聘请外部帮助，并在开发后将创新带入内部，但这种方法对中小型组织来说更困难。
>
> For most nonprofits, the solution may be in the middle. For example, [Bloomberg Philanthropies](https://www.bloomberg.org/), which focuses on government innovation, funds innovation teams of three to five people who work on horizontal issues at mayor’s offices in more than 20 cities. The teams are comprised of city employees, designers, and outside researchers, and effectively work as internal incubators and accelerators for ideas on transforming city functions. Importantly, these teams never fully own the projects they tackle; they identify who owns a problem internally, and help bring those people along during the process of discovery and design. When the project goes into the pilot or trial phase, the teams make a clean handoff to the owners. Rather than train everybody in design thinking, these teams serve as catalysts and integrators, similar to Hollywood-style movie development teams.
> 对于大多数非营利组织来说，解决方案可能在中间。例如，专注于政府创新的[彭博慈善基金会（Bloomberg Philanthropies）](https://www.bloomberg.org/)为20多个城市的市长办公室的三到五人创新团队提供资金，这些团队致力于横向问题。这些团队由城市员工、设计师和外部研究人员组成，有效地发挥了内部孵化器和加速器的作用，促进了城市职能转变。重要的是，这些团队从未完全拥有他们所处理的项目;他们在内部确定谁拥有问题，并在发现和设计过程中帮助沿着这些人。当项目进入试点或试验阶段时，团队会将项目移交给所有者。这些团队不是训练每个人的设计思维，而是充当催化剂和整合者，类似于好莱坞风格的电影开发团队。
