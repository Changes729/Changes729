## 摘记

> Rising discontent with globalization initially manifested as pushback against trade agreements and global economic integration, but development agencies became symbolic proxies for the broader “global elite,” seen as disconnected from the very communities they sought to help.
>
> 对全球化日益增长的不满最初表现为对贸易协定和全球经济一体化的抵制，但发展机构成为更广泛的“全球精英”的象征性代理人，他们被视为与他们试图帮助的社区脱节。

集权和分权的问题类似，一方面又需要集权以获取最大的影响力，但是另一方面又需要非常在地以实际作用于本地发展。



> Last year OECD-DAC members [rightfully listed ](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/07/development-co-operation-report-2024_b9cb300f/357b63f7-en.pdf?)inadequate data, difficulties in designing results frameworks, and impact evaluation as the three most urgent reasons they could not accomplish more to reduce poverty. 
>
> 去年，经合组织-发援会成员[正确地将](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/07/development-co-operation-report-2024_b9cb300f/357b63f7-en.pdf?)数据不足、成果框架设计困难和影响评估列为它们无法在减贫方面取得更多成就的三个最紧迫原因。

对发展成就的衡量标准：数据、成果框架、影响力评估。

其实有思考过一种动态的协作和组织机制，比较类似与细胞分化。



> Standing on the right side of history and with a moral high ground, the sector grew complacent, failing to recognize that global solidarity requires continuous justification, especially in politically volatile contexts. The development industry’s tendency to focus more on signals of value than [actionable transparency](https://www.publishwhatyoufund.org/the-index/2024/) on the value they bring has made it vulnerable to criticisms: whether valid or opportunistic, proving just as lethal. Internally, too, persistent criticism for inefficiency, opacity, and excessive bureaucracy—as well as high overheads, unclear impact metrics, and an inability to clearly communicate tangible outcomes to donors and the public—have fostered skepticism and eroded trust among those whose support is now desperately needed.
> 该部门站在历史正确的一边，占据道德制高点，变得自满起来，没有认识到全球团结需要不断的理由，特别是在政治动荡的情况下。开发行业倾向于更多地关注价值信号，而不是对它们带来的价值[的可操作的透明度](https://www.publishwhatyoufund.org/the-index/2024/)，这使得它容易受到批评：无论是有效的还是机会主义的，都被证明是致命的。在内部，对效率低下、不透明和过度官僚主义的持续批评，以及高昂的管理费用、不明确的影响指标和无法向捐助者和公众清楚地传达切实成果，也助长了那些现在迫切需要支持的人的怀疑和信任。
>
> If the development industry's shortcomings were hidden from sight, for decades, they are laid bare today. The price for the failure is now evident, as is the need for introspection and meaningful reform. In this sense, the path forward, however painful, presents a moment for transformative reflection: Organizations must embrace rigorous accountability and effectiveness standards. Efficiency is imperative, and every dollar must justify itself through clear, measurable impacts.
> 如果说开发行业的缺点被隐藏了几十年，那么今天它们被暴露出来了。失败的代价现在是显而易见的，需要进行反省和有意义的改革也是如此。从这个意义上说，前进的道路，无论多么痛苦，都是一个变革性反思的时刻：组织必须接受严格的问责制和有效性标准。效率至关重要，每一美元都必须通过明确、可衡量的影响来证明自己的价值。

开发行业是发展机构的意思。

这里一方面是问题的分析，包括了外部的看法和实际的效用；以及内部的管理和制度透明等。

并最终给出了自己的解决方案：严格的问责制和有效性标准。
