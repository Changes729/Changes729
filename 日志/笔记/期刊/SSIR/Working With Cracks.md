## 摘记

**01 什么是 Radical Engagement？**

> By **radical engagement** we mean engagement born of **radical relationships**, relationships that are gritty, real and robust.
> 我们所说的激进参与是指源于激进关系的参与，这种关系是坚韧不拔的、真实的和强大的。
>
> Radical engagement is the **bedrock for buy-in to change.**
> 激进的参与是支持变革的基石。

**01.1 Radical Relationships**

> *rad.i.cal (adjective): very different from the usual or traditional*
>
> rad.i.cal（形容词）：与通常或传统的非常不同

> Five Keys to a Radical Relationship 
>
> 建立一段激进关系的五个关键
>
> 1. **Radical Commitment**：Beyond your marriage vows, you both are absolutely, 100% committed to your marriage, no matter what and you are as committed to your partner’s happiness as your own. You each take 100% responsibility for the relationship, your experience in the relationship, and your outcomes.
>
>    激进的承诺：除了你的婚姻誓言，你们俩都是绝对的，100%致力于你的婚姻，无论如何，你是致力于你的伴侣的幸福作为自己的。你们每个人都对这段关系、你们在这段关系中的经历和你们的结果承担100%的责任。
>
> 2. **Radical Intimacy**：ou are completely transparent to your partner, don’t hold anything back, and share all your thoughts, feelings, wants, needs, fantasies and desires. 
>
>    激进的亲密：你对你的伴侣是完全透明的，不要隐瞒任何事情，分享你所有的想法，感受，想要，需要，幻想和欲望。
>
> 3. **Radical Romance:** You continually express your love, appreciation, attraction and adoration for your partner in words and actions and don’t take your relationship for granted or allow passion to be replaced by routine.
>
>    激进浪漫：你不断地用语言和行动表达你对伴侣的爱、欣赏、吸引和崇拜，不要把你们的关系视为理所当然，也不要让激情被例行公事所取代。
>
> 4. **Radical Sex:** Beyond satisfying physical urges, you consciously seek and experience emotional, physical, and spiritual connection every day, continually exploring new ways to express love and pleasure your partner and allowing yourself to be loved and pleasured.
>
>    激进的性：除了满足身体上的冲动，你每天都有意识地寻求和体验情感、身体和精神上的联系，不断探索新的方式来表达对伴侣的爱和快乐，让自己被爱和快乐。
>
> 5. **Radical Living:** Beyond survival and comfort, you consciously design your lifestyle together, develop your shared vision and goals, devote time and resources to realizing your dreams in each moment together as well as prioritizing your goals and preparing for your future. You are aware of how short your time is on this planet and savor each precious moment of life and being together.
>
>    激进生活：除了生存和舒适，你们有意识地一起设计你们的生活方式，发展你们共同的愿景和目标，投入时间和资源来实现你们在一起的每一刻的梦想，以及优先考虑你们的目标和为你们的未来做准备。你意识到你在这个星球上的时间是多么短暂，享受生活和在一起的每一个宝贵时刻。
