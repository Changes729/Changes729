## 摘记

**01 尼日利亚食物危机的根源**

In the wet season, which runs from April to October, farmers plant and harvest water-dependent crops like maize and yams. In the dry season, which occurs in the intervening months, such crops are scarce; only farmers who can afford irrigation can grow them, which limits supply and makes food more expensive. 

在4月至10月的雨季，农民种植和收获依赖水的作物，如玉米和山药。在干旱季节，即中间的几个月里，这种作物很稀少;只有能负担得起灌溉费用的农民才能种植，这限制了供应，使粮食更加昂贵。

Similar climate-related challenges of extended rainfall and flooding during the wet season damage crops and strip the soil of nutrients needed to grow food.

类似的气候相关挑战，如雨季降雨量增加和洪水泛滥，损害作物，剥夺土壤中种植粮食所需的养分。
