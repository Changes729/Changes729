## 摘记

**01 使命（Mission）**

> The function of mission is to guide and inspire; to energize and give meaning; and to define a nonprofit and what it stands for. 
>
> 使命的功能是引导和激励；激发活力和赋予意义；定义非营利组织和它所代表的意义。



**01.1 核心价值观（core values）**

> While core values reflect unalterable principles, an organization’s reason for being is defined by its purpose. 
>
> 核心价值观反映的是不可改变的原则，而一个组织存在的理由则是由其目标决定的。

> It also has to answer the question of why an organization does what it does.
>
> 它还必须回答一个问题，即一个组织为什么要做它所做的事情。



**01.2 目的（purpose）**

> Purpose is also the closest thing to what most organizations typically refer to as their “mission.” 
>
> 目标也是最接近大多数组织通常所说的 “使命 ”的东西。



**01.3 主要目标（primary goals）**

> primary goal. Not just any old goal, but a soaring, difficult- to-achieve goal
>
> 首要目标。这不是一个普通的目标，而是一个高耸入云、难以实现的目标；

> Attainability is a critical test of a primary goal because its power stems from its capacity to focus, mobilize, and coordinate an organization’s efforts. Goals that bear little relationship to reality lose this source of practical value.
>
> 可实现性是对主要目标的关键考验，因为目标的力量来自于其聚焦、动员和协调组织努力的能力。与现实关系不大的目标就失去了实用价值。

> The issue of attainability also highlights the way goals function as the link between mission and strategy. Especially when goals entail growth or expansion of resources in a competitive environment, strategy provides a basis for evaluating whether these goals are challenging but realistic, as well as a concrete plan for achieving them.
>
> 可实现性问题还凸显了目标作为使命与战略之间纽带的作用。特别是当目标需要在竞争环境中增长或扩大资源时，战略就为评估这些目标是否具有挑战性但又切合实际提供了依据，并为实现这些目标提供了具体计划。



**01.4 愿景（vision）**

> which Collins and Porras defined as the “vibrant, and engaging, and specific description of what it will be like to achieve the [primary goal].” 
>
> 柯林斯和波拉斯将其定义为 “对实现[首要目标]的具体描述，充满活力，引人入胜”。

> The test of a good vision is whether it makes people see, taste, and feel the future.
>
> 检验一个好愿景的标准是，它是否能让人们看到、品味和感受到未来。



**02 战略（strategy）**

> In the corporate world, strategy is recognized as the body of knowledge that provides a basis for explaining and influencing organizational performance – specifically, profitability.
>
> 在企业界，战略被认为是为解释和影响组织绩效（特别是盈利能力）提供依据的知识体系。

> Moreover, strategy involves devising better ways to compete, and many nonprofit leaders dislike the idea of competition, preferring to emphasize their cooperative stance toward other providers in their industry. 
>
> 此外，战略涉及设计更好的竞争方式，而许多非营利组织的领导者不喜欢竞争的概念，他们更愿意强调与本行业其他提供者的合作立场。

> First, no organization will survive if it’s not economically viable.
>
> 首先，如果经济上不可行，任何组织都无法生存。

> Second, competition is a fact of life for nonprofits just as it is for for-profit businesses. It’s a function of the scarcity of resources – especially money. To the extent that a nonprofit doesn’t have all the resources it needs to carry out its mission, it’s in competition with similar organizations for those resources.
>
> 其次，对非营利组织来说，竞争是一个不争的事实，就像对营利性企业一样。这是资源稀缺--尤其是资金稀缺--的结果。如果非营利组织没有履行使命所需的全部资源，它就会与同类组织竞争这些资源。

> Strategy must be based on an accurate understanding of the dynamics of competition within a given sphere of activity. It can’t drive performance until it has been translated into concrete choices and actions. Otherwise, it’s little more than an abstract slogan or wishful thinking.
>
> 战略必须建立在对特定活动领域竞争态势的准确理解之上。只有将战略转化为具体的选择和行动，才能推动绩效。否则，它只不过是一个抽象的口号或一厢情愿的想法。

> The function of strategy is to evaluate and enhance an organization’s ability to secure the financial resources it needs in order to survive and even to thrive.
>
> 战略的功能是评估和提高组织的能力，以确保其获得生存甚至发展所需的财政资源。

> A strategy also would have provided a logic for designing and evaluating a plan of action to achieve the symphony’s goals.
>
> 战略还可以为设计和评估实现交响乐团目标的行动计划提供逻辑。



**02.1 范围（scope）**

> Scope describes the “playing field” on which an organization competes: who its customers are, where it operates geographically, what products or services it offers, and what activities it performs.
>
> 范围描述了一个组织参与竞争的 “竞技场”：它的客户是谁，它在哪里开展业务，它提供什么样的产品或服务，以及它开展什么样的活动。



**02.2 竞争优势（competitive）**

> Competitive advantage specifies the unique sources of value that an organization offers its customers, as defined by its scope. It explains why these customers (who include both clients and donors) will prefer its products or services over those offered by other organizations. 
>
> 竞争优势明确了一个组织根据其业务范围为客户提供的独特价值来源。它解释了为什么这些客户（包括客户和捐赠者）更喜欢其产品或服务，而不是其他组织提供的产品或服务。



**02.3 逻辑（advantage）**

> logic. It answers two key questions: Why will the strategy work? And how will the organization be designed and managed so as to create and sustain its competitive advantage? Logic makes explicit the causal assumptions about the relationships between the organization’s choices and behavior, the consequences of this behavior, and the interaction between these consequences and the environment in which the organization operates.
>
> 逻辑回答了两个关键问题： 为什么战略会奏效？如何设计和管理组织，以创造并保持竞争优势？逻辑明确了组织的选择和行为、这种行为的后果以及这些后果与组织所处环境之间互动关系的因果假设。

> The symphony’s competitive advantage derived from its uniquely adventurous, well-played, original programming, combined with its convenient location for music lovers living in or near Oakland. **The strategy’s logic worked because there was a clear match between the unique value created by the symphony and the needs of its target audiences.**
>
> 交响乐团的竞争优势来自于其独特的冒险精神、精湛的演奏、原创的节目编排，再加上其便利的地理位置，为居住在奥克兰或奥克兰附近的音乐爱好者提供了便利。**该战略的逻辑之所以行之有效，是因为交响乐团创造的独特价值与其目标受众的需求之间存在着明确的匹配关系。**



**03 常见错误**

> But the question remains of why a clear and compelling mission was never articulated, and how a robust strategy was transformed into a disastrous one. The answer is that the symphony made the common error of confusing the psychological and emotional logic of mission with the economic logic of strategy.
>
> 但问题是，为什么从来没有明确而令人信服的使命，一个强有力的战略又是如何转变为一个灾难性的战略的。答案是，交响乐团犯了一个常见的错误，即混淆了**使命的心理和情感逻辑**与**战略的经济逻辑**。
