## 摘记

**01 商业可持续的渊源**

> In its first incarnation, business sustainability represents a market shift. Market pressures bring sustainability to business attention through core management channels and functions. This began with Nixon-era government regulation and grew to include insurance companies, investors, consumers, suppliers, buyers, and others through the 1980s and 1990s. Such market pressures can emerge from numerous sources: coercive drivers—from domestic and international regulations and the courts; resource drivers—from suppliers, buyers, shareholders, investors, banks, and insurance companies; market drivers—from consumers, trade associations, competitors, and consultants; and social drivers—from nonprofit organizations, activist groups, the press, religious institutions, and academia.
>
> 在其最初形态中，商业可持续性体现为一次市场层面的转向。市场压力通过企业的核心管理渠道与职能部门，将可持续性议题带入商业决策视野。这一过程始于尼克松政府时期的环境监管，并在20世纪80—90年代逐步扩展，涵盖保险公司、投资者、消费者、供应商、采购方等多方主体。这些市场压力可能来自多个来源：强制性驱动因素—国内与国际法规以及司法体系；资源性驱动因素—供应商、买方、股东、投资者、银行与保险公司；市场性驱动因素—消费者、行业协会、竞争对手与咨询机构；社会性驱动因素—非营利组织、行动主义团体、媒体、宗教机构以及学术界。



**02 行星边界（planetary boundaries）**

> To measure that influence, they have identified nine “planetary boundaries” that represent “thresholds below which humanity can safely operate and beyond which the stability of planetary-scale systems cannot be relied upon.”6 These are what Lancaster University management professor Gail Whiteman has called the “key performance indicators” (KPIs) of the planet, many of which are not doing so well. While one (ozone depletion) is on the mend, scientists believe we have overshot the boundaries of three: climate change, biodiversity loss, and biogeochemical flows (nitrogen and phosphorus cycles). Further indicators are also blinking red, such as ocean acidification, freshwater use, and deforestation. The remaining two boundaries—chemical pollution and atmospheric particle pollution—require more data to assess. All of these disruptions are the result of system failures created largely by our market institutions. They will have to be remedied by those institutions.
>
> 为了衡量这种影响，科学家提出了九个“行星边界（planetary boundaries）”，它们代表着“人类可以安全活动的阈值；一旦越过这些阈值，行星尺度系统的稳定性将不再可靠”。⁶ 英国兰卡斯特大学管理学教授盖尔·惠特曼（Gail Whiteman）将这些边界称为地球的“关键绩效指标（KPI）”，而其中许多指标的表现并不乐观。目前，仅有一个指标——臭氧层消耗——正在好转；而科学家认为，我们已经突破了三项边界：气候变化、生物多样性丧失，以及生物地球化学循环（氮与磷循环）。其他指标也在亮红灯，包括海洋酸化、淡水使用以及森林砍伐。剩余的两项边界——化学污染与大气颗粒污染——由于数据不足尚无法作出明确评估。所有这些扰动，都是由市场规则主导形成的系统性失灵所导致的，因此，也必须由这些规则来加以修复。



**03 可持续商业**

> At the Harvard Business School, an immensely popular course called “Reexamining Capitalism” explores “the evolution, power, and limitations of our current capitalist systems” and “how the ‘rules of the game’ by which capitalism is structured should change” to address the social and environmental issues of our day.
>
> 在哈佛商学院，一门极受欢迎的课程“重新审视资本主义（Reexamining Capitalism）”探讨了“当前资本主义体系的进化、权力及其局限”，以及“资本主义结构中的‘游戏规则’应如何改变”，以应对当今的社会和环境问题。
