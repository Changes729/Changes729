### Step 1

- [ ] 是否保持**原文的分段**。

- [ ] 译文中是否沿用原文中组织机构的**缩写名称**（比如UN,WHO等）。

- [ ] 是否通读全部译文并确保**语句通顺、无语病、无错别字等低级错误**。

### Step 2

- [ ] 是否严格使用**词汇表**中的译法。
- [ ] 是否从**官方途径**对原文中的**专有名词**进行确认，并在**批注**中**标明来源**或**注明“未找到官方译文”**。（请将**所有专有名词**放入glossary，供后续编辑和校对的同学查阅。）
- [ ] 对于与文章内容密切相关，但**文中没有进行解释的专有名词**，是否使用**脚注**进行介绍（如影响力投资的文章中提到了“社会责任投资”，但没有对这个词进行解释，就需要添加脚注进行介绍）。
- [ ] 对于文中**多次出现**的关键重要词汇是否保持**翻译的统一，**特殊情况是否标黄。（请将此类词汇放入glossary，供后续编辑和校对的同学查阅。）
- [ ] 利用搜索引擎确认词语**符合中文用法**（如financial return在中文里常用“财务回报”而不是“金融回报”）。

### Step 3

- [ ] 一校和二校意见一致的地方是否全部以**无修订痕迹**的模式呈现。
- [ ] 一校和二校是否对意见不统一的地方进行**讨论**，并在最终无法达成一致的地方**保留修订模式**。

### 第四部分：正文

![shop owner and FUNDES consultant in shop in Bogotá](https://ssir.org/images/jcogs_img/cache/spring-2025-case-study-contnuous-transform-clerks-artcl-592x395_-_abcdef_-_ec76adfb59d23efa05b4d7ecdd5601e6950c9f26.webp)

哥伦比亚波哥大的一位店主（左）正在接受 FUNDES 顾问的建议，这是该组织与 SABMiller 合作的 4e 项目的一部分。（图片由 FUNDES 提供）



On the evening of October 8, 2024, guests poured into the posh restaurant of ÇUINA, the Culinary Innovation Academy, to celebrate an anniversary. In the heart of Mexico City’s hip Roma neighborhood, ÇUINA teaches and showcases chef Xano Saguer’s socially conscious vision of the culinary arts. That night his staff would cook a special meal to mark 40 years of the Foundation for Sustainable Development (Fundación para el Desarrollo Sostenible), better known as FUNDES.

2024 年 10 月 8 日晚，烹饪创新学院 ÇUINA 的豪华餐厅里宾客云集，庆祝学院成立一周年。ÇUINA位于墨西哥城时髦的罗马街区中心，教授并展示厨师哈诺-萨盖尔（Xano Saguer）对烹饪艺术的社会意识（socially conscious）。当晚，他的员工将为可持续发展基金会（Fundación para el Desarrollo Sostenible，简称 FUNDES）的 40 周年纪念烹制一顿特别的大餐。



As people entered, they each received a puzzle piece, on which they were asked to write their vision for FUNDES’ future. Since its inception, FUNDES has sought to foster sustainable economic development across Latin America through financing, education, and support for micro- and small enterprises (MSMEs). While the assembled guests enjoyed their welcome cocktails, members of the board shared their own visions of the organization’s next four decades.

入场时，每个人都会收到一块拼图，要求他们在上面写下自己对 FUNDES 未来的愿景。 自成立以来，FUNDES 一直致力于通过为微型和小型企业（micro-and small enterprises, MSMEs）提供融资、教育和支持，促进整个拉丁美洲的可持续经济发展。 在与会嘉宾享用欢迎鸡尾酒的同时，董事会成员分享了各自对本组织未来四十年的愿景。



“In the first 40 years of FUNDES, it had to reinvent itself continuously,” said Andreas Eggenberg, member of FUNDES’ board and chair of MASISA, a long-term client of FUNDES. “I don’t know where we will be in 40 years, but definitely different than now, keeping the motivational spirit and curiosity to reinvent ourselves again.”

“在 FUNDES 成立的头 40 年里，它必须不断得自我革新。” FUNDES 董事会成员兼 MASISA（FUNDES 的长期客户）主席安德烈亚斯-埃根伯格（Andreas Eggenberg）说：”我不知道 40 年后我们会发展成什么样，但肯定会与现在不同，因此请保持再次自我革新的动力和好奇心。“



The puzzle pieces were eventually assembled, coming together to complete a larger-than-life mural depicting typical FUNDES clients: two female bakers wearing white aprons and hairnets while shaping dough into small loaves of bread. The puzzle featured the FUNDES logo and the slogan “You are also part of the future of Latin America.” The exercise created a fitting symbol of FUNDES’ multidecade struggle and enduring mission to maintain close, meaningful interactions with its beneficiaries. FUNDES champions MSMEs, because of their role in driving employment, poverty reduction, and economic inclusion, and in fostering equitable and sustainable growth. Despite that singular vision, FUNDES has undergone numerous reinventions to sustain itself and its clients in its first 40 years and will inevitably face more in the future.

这些拼图最终被拼接在一起，完成了一幅超大型壁画，描绘了 FUNDES 的典型客户：两位身着白色围裙、头戴发网的女面包师，正在将面团捏成小面包。拼图上有 FUNDES 的标志和口号 "你也是拉丁美洲未来的一部分"。这项活动恰如其分地象征了 FUNDES 数十年的奋斗历程和永恒的使命——与受益人保持密切且有意义的互动。FUNDES 支持中小微企业，因为它们在推动就业、减少贫困、经济包容、促进公平和可持续增长方面发挥着重要作用。尽管有这一独特的愿景，FUNDES 在其最初的 40 年中经历了无数次改革，以维持自身及其客户的生存，未来还将不可避免地面临更多的改革。



Today, many leaders of social impact organizations puzzle over how to reconcile a strong social mission with a sustainable financing model and experiment with a variety of different approaches. Imagine, for example, that you head an international philanthropic organization with a strong social mission and suddenly your funding is stopped. What would be a disaster for most nonprofits was just the beginning of the FUNDES story that took it from a philanthropic organization to a shared value consultancy and ultimately to a social-venture incubator. Yet, FUNDES ultimately had to maintain the same mission throughout its history. <u>“The one thing you may not touch is the mission—the sustainable development of MSMEs in Latin America,” says Ulrich Frei, current director on FUNDES’ board.</u>

如今，许多社会影响力组织的领导者都在思考如何协调强强烈的社会使命与可持续的融资模式，并尝试了各种不同的方法。举例来说，想象一下，你领导着一个具有强烈社会使命的国际慈善组织，但突然间你的资金资助被停止了。这对大多数非营利组织来说都是一场灾难，但这只是 FUNDES 故事的开始，它从一个慈善组织发展成为一个共享价值咨询公司，并最终成为一个社会风险孵化器。但是，FUNDES 在其整个发展历程中依旧保持着相同的使命。<u>FUNDES 董事会现任董事乌尔里希-弗雷（Ulrich Frei）说：“你终其一生追求的东西就是使命，对我们而言，那就是拉丁美洲中小微企业的可持续发展。“</u>



The FUNDES case reveals the conditions, opportunities, and challenges associated with taking different approaches. It shows that realizing a long-term sustainable impact for society requires managers who are entrepreneurial, an ability to approach entirely new market domains, the openness to embrace new stakeholder groups, and a willingness to radically change organizations and their cultures.

FUNDES 但案例揭示了采取不同方法的条件、机会和挑战。它表明，要实现对社会的长期可持续影响，管理者必须有企业家精神，有能力涉足全新的市场领域，以开放的态度接纳新的利益相关者群体，并愿意彻底改变组织及其文化。



### A Mission Is Born 

### 一个使命诞生了

The idea for FUNDES was born in 1983, when Archbishop Marcos McGrath of Panama embarked on a fundraising trip through Europe to seek help for Latin America’s social and economic challenges. These included structural poverty, low economic growth, and few formal employment opportunities for a young and growing population. During his journey, he met with a group of prominent Swiss businessmen, including Stephan Schmidheiny, entrepreneur and scion of a wealthy industrialist and philanthropic family.

FUNDES 的想法诞生于1983年，当时巴拿马大主教马科斯·麦格拉思（Marcos McGrath）踏上了欧洲筹款之旅，为拉丁美洲面临的社会和经济挑战寻求帮助。这些挑战包括结构性贫困、经济增长乏力、年轻且不断增长的人口几乎没有正规就业机会。在旅途中，他会见了一批著名的瑞士商人，其中包括企业家、富有的实业家和慈善家家族的后裔斯蒂芬·斯密德亨尼（Stephan Schmidheiny）。



Schmidheiny was captivated by McGrath’s vision of spurring development in Latin America through small-business support. In Latin America, micro- and small enterprises (MSMEs)—such as traditional mom-and-pop stores, small-scale banana farms, and scrap-metal collectors—play a crucial role in the economy. McGrath envisioned transforming these small ventures into growth engines by providing them with financial assistance and essential business knowledge. Intrigued by this vision, Schmidheiny became the primary donor.

麦格拉斯希望通过支持小企业来促进拉丁美洲的发展，这深深吸引了斯密德亨尼。在拉丁美洲，中小微企业（如传统的夫妻店、小型香蕉园和废金属回收商）在经济中发挥着至关重要的作用。麦格拉斯设想通过向这些小型企业提供资金援助和必要的商业知识，将其转变为增长引擎。被这一愿景吸引的斯密德亨尼成为了主要捐赠人。



FUNDES began its operations in 1984 by offering guaranteed loans to MSMEs in Panama and set up a headquarters in Switzerland to work closely with Schmidheiny. In subsequent years, FUNDES expanded into a loosely organized network across nine more countries: Costa Rica (1986), Guatemala (1988), Colombia (1989), Bolivia (1990), Chile (1992), Argentina (1993), Mexico (1993), Venezuela (1994), and El Salvador (2000). These local offices were “small kingdoms,” according to Ulrich Frei, a Swiss businessman with extensive experience in Latin America who became FUNDES’ CEO in June 2008 and served in that role for more than four years. “Every country office decided what to do as long as it was for the good of MSMEs.” Each country office independently shaped strategies for providing support directly to beneficiaries, such as farmers or small-shop owners. During the first decade, the philanthropic model allowed FUNDES to focus entirely on its purpose and tailor its approach to local contexts.

FUNDES 于 1984 年开始运作，为巴拿马的中小微企业提供担保贷款，并在瑞士设立了总部，与斯密德亨尼密切合作。在随后的几年中，FUNDES 扩展成为一个组织松散的网络，遍布另外九个国家： 哥斯达黎加（1986 年）、危地马拉（1988 年）、哥伦比亚（1989 年）、玻利维亚（1990 年）、智利（1992 年）、阿根廷（1993 年）、墨西哥（1993 年）、委内瑞拉（1994 年）和萨尔瓦多（2000 年）。乌尔里希-弗雷是一位在拉丁美洲拥有丰富经验的瑞士商人，他于 2008 年 6 月成为 FUNDES 的首席执行官，并在此岗位上工作了四年多。“每个国家办事处都可以为中小微企业的利益做些什么”。每个国家办事处独立制定战略，直接向农民或小店主等受益人提供支持。在最初的十年里，慈善模式使 FUNDES 能够完全专注于其目标，并根据当地情况调整其方法。



“To invest in MSMEs, people have to go to these companies and understand what they do,” says Urs Jäger, associate professor with INCAE Business School, Costa Rica, about informal markets in Latin America. “Investors have to enter this dark world before investing.” This groundwork is what FUNDES did. However, over time, its decentralized structure increasingly created operational inefficiencies without paying off in greater impact or learning across countries. Furthermore, its reliance on Schmidheiny, a single donor, made it financially dependent and vulnerable to changes in his philanthropic strategy. FUNDES would need to change.

"要投资中小微企业，人们必须到这些公司去，了解它们的业务“，哥斯达黎加 INCAE 商学院副教授乌尔斯-耶格尔（Urs Jäger）谈到拉丁美洲的非正规市场时说：“投资者在投资之前必须进入这个未知的世界”。这正是 FUNDES 所做的基础工作。然而，随着时间的推移，分散的结构使得其运营效率低下，即没有产生更大的影响，也没有在跨国间得到学习。此外，FUNDES 对斯密德亨尼这位单一捐赠者的依赖，使不仅其在财务上依赖于施密德亨尼，慈善战略还容易受其影响。FUNDES 需要改变。



## The Importance of Knowledge 

## 知识的重要性

In 1996 FUNDES headquarters conducted an internal review. The investigation flagged the limitations of its credit-guarantee model—loan repayment rates were low, and while FUNDES guaranteed the repayment of the loan to the bank, the fundamental challenges for small businesses often remained unaddressed. The study further found that the poor organizational skills of small-business owners and entrepreneurs impeded their success and overall contribution to the regional economy.

1996 年，FUNDES 总部进行了一次内部审查。调查指出了其信贷担保模式的局限性——贷款偿还率低，虽然 FUNDES 保证向银行偿还贷款，但小企业面临的根本挑战往往仍未得到解决。调查还发现，小企业主和企业家的组织能力差，阻碍了他们的成功和对地区经济的整体贡献。



![shop mexico city cash register](https://ssir.org/images/jcogs_img/cache/spring-2025-case-study-contnuous-transform-cash-reg-artcl-592x395_-_abcdef_-_ec76adfb59d23efa05b4d7ecdd5601e6950c9f26.webp)

[In 2016 FUNDES began to experiment with digital strategies to help small shops like this one in Mexico City earn more money and improve business efficiency. (Photo courtesy of FUNDES) 
2016年，FUNDES开始尝试数字化策略，帮助像墨西哥城这样的小商店赚更多的钱，提高经营效率。（图片来源：FUNDES)](https://ssir.org/images/articles/spring-2025-case-study-contnuous-transform-cash-reg-artcl-592x395.jpg)

“After 10 years, we realized that giving MSMEs access to capital without also giving them access to knowledge was a really risky business,” Frei says. Instead of seeing this as a setback, Schmidheiny closely worked with FUNDES management to develop an entirely new model to address their mission.

弗雷说：“10年后，我们意识到，在向中小微企业提供资金的同时，却不向他们提供知识，这确实是一项风险很大的业务”。斯密德亨尼没有有将此视为挫折，而是与 FUNDES 管理层密切合作，开发了一种全新的模式来完成他们的使命。



They reaffirmed their commitment to empowering MSMEs as essential players in Latin America’s economic and social development but shifted their focus toward education. FUNDES began offering workshops and classes for MSME owners across various sectors, from retail to agriculture. These programs improved the organizational and financial skills of MSME owners and, by extension, the management practices of their businesses. By equipping entrepreneurs with practical tools, FUNDES helped them increase their efficiency, expand market reach, navigate regulatory challenges, and grow their businesses sustainably. By 2002, its educational program had reached more than 140,000 participants and covered 40 different subjects, including administration and finance, production, marketing, and human resources.

他们重申致力于增强中小微企业的能力，使其成为拉丁美洲经济和社会发展的重要参与者，并将重点转向教育。FUNDES 开始为从零售到农业等各行各业的中小微企业主开办讲习班和课程。这些课程提高了中小微企业主的组织能力和财务技能，进而改善了他们的企业管理实践。通过为企业家提供实用工具，FUNDES 帮助他们提高效率，扩大市场覆盖面，应对监管挑战，实现企业的可持续发展。到 2002 年，其教育计划的参与者已超过 14 万人，涵盖 40 个不同的科目，包括行政和财务、生产、营销和人力资源。



At the time, MSMEs struggled primarily with “access to credit, tax procedures, unfair competition, and the need to adapt to globalization and technology,” said the owner of a textile SME in Guatemala who wished to remain nameless. The volatile economy, with its unstable financial institutions, waves of inflation, and high levels of corruption, provided harsh conditions for small-business owners across the region. They were often forced to focus on bare survival. Through access to business know-how and a peer group of like-minded entrepreneurs, they were better able to navigate the challenges they faced.

危地马拉一家不愿透露姓名的纺织品中小型企业老板说，当时，中小微企业主要在 “信贷获取、税务程序、不公平竞争以及需要适应全球化和技术 ”等方面挣扎。动荡的经济、不稳定的金融机构、一波波的通货膨胀和严重的腐败，为整个地区的小企业主带来了苛刻的条件。他们往往被迫专注于生存。通过学习商业知识并与志同道合的企业家组成同行团体，他们才能够更好地应对所面临的挑战。



FUNDES expanded its training model over the subsequent years, becoming a significant provider of business education to MSMEs in the region. The model also helped FUNDES learn more about their real pain points in Latin America’s economy. They discovered that MSMEs were critical links in the value chains for big multinationals, yet these relationships were usually not well integrated or managed. Unlocking ways to professionalize MSMEs and enabling them to join forces with larger companies was seen as essential for unlocking economic growth.

随后几年，FUNDES 扩展了培训模式，成为该地区中小微企业商业教育的重要提供者。该模式还帮助 FUNDES 进一步了解了拉丁美洲经济中的真正痛点。他们发现，中小微企业是大型跨国公司价值链中的关键环节，但这些关系通常没有得到很好的整合与管理。如何使中小微企业实现专业化，并使其能够与大公司联手，被视为实现经济增长的关键。



> Frei believed that large corporations would be willing to provide much-needed financial support for FUNDES’ activities, “not necessarily because they are better people, but because they wanted to stay competitive ... because the MSMEs are the backbone of their value chains.”
>
> 弗雷认为，大公司愿意为 FUNDES 的活动提供急需的财政支持，“不一定是因为他们是更优秀的人，而是因为他们希望保持竞争力......因为中小微企业是其价值链的支柱”。



In 2004, FUNDES officially changed its goal from just “delivering knowledge” to “implementing knowledge” in Latin America by offering retail consulting to small-shop owners. That year, FUNDES participated in a study of the United Nations Economic Commission for Latin America and the Caribbean (ECLAC). It found that educating people was important, but that FUNDES’ focus should shift toward the actual impact for their businesses. João Carlos Ferraz, UN-ECLAC’s director, saw the collaboration with FUNDES as “an important opportunity to deepen and refine applied research techniques and methodologies for the evaluation of education programs.”

2004 年，FUNDES 正式将其目标从 “提供知识 ”转变为在拉丁美洲 “落实知识”，为小店业主提供零售咨询。这一年，FUNDES 参加了联合国拉丁美洲和加勒比经济委员会（拉加经委会）的一项研究。研究发现，教育人民固然重要，但 FUNDES 的工作重点应转向对他们的企业产生实际影响。联合国拉丁美洲和加勒比经济委员会主任乔-卡洛斯-费拉兹认为，与 FUNDES 的合作是 “深化和完善教育方案评价应用研究技术和方法的重要机会”。



FUNDES thus adapted its service portfolio to consult with MSMEs on how to improve their operations. Schmidheiny subsidized the new remit, because the MSMEs did not have the resources to pay for the service. While the first consulting projects targeted inventory management, accounting, and other small-scale operational improvements at individual MSMEs, one project was different. In 2006, COPROCA, a Peruvian company storing and exporting llama and alpaca yarn, collaborated with FUNDES on a larger scale to support and professionalize 500 small farmers and turn them into more reliable suppliers. While this was an unusual project for FUNDES at the time, it turned out to be a glimpse into the future.

因此，FUNDES 调整了其服务组合，就如何改善中小微企业的经营状况向其提供咨询。由于中小微企业没有资源支付服务费用，施密德亨尼为新的服务项目提供了补贴。虽然首批咨询项目针对的是库存管理、会计和其他中小微企业的小规模运营改进，但有一个项目与众不同。2006 年，秘鲁一家储存和出口骆驼及羊驼纱线的公司 COPROCA 与 FUNDES 开展了更大规模的合作，为 500 个小农场主提供支持并使其专业化，使其成为更可靠的供应商。对当时的 FUNDES 而言，正是这一个不同寻常的项目，让我们看到了未来。



## Combining Purpose and Profit 

## 结合目的和利润

In 2008 the global financial crisis dried up credit, led to mass unemployment, and hit Latin America’s MSMEs hard. The emergency also limited the ability and willingness of philanthropic funders to continue supporting their causes at the same levels. This crunch created another pivotal moment for FUNDES. In need of refocusing its portfolio of beneficiaries, Schmidheiny’s team identified FUNDES as a project that could potentially become self-sustaining and decided to phase out its annual donations. Frei remembers it as their “biggest wake-up call.”

2008 年，全球金融危机使信贷枯竭和大规模失业，重创了拉丁美洲的中小微企业。紧急状况也限制了慈善资助者继续以相同水平支持其事业的能力和意愿。这场危机为 FUNDES 带来了另一个关键时刻。施密德亨尼的团队需要重新调整受益人组合，他们认为 FUNDES 是一个有自负盈亏潜力的项目，并决定逐步取消其年度捐赠。弗莱认为这是 他们“最大的警钟”。



“We were told that within five years, we must become a financially sustainable organization,” Frei says. <u>“But one thing you may not touch is the mission—the sustainable development of small- and medium-sized enterprises in Latin America.”</u>
“我们被告知，在五年内，我们必须成为一个财务上可持续的组织，”弗雷说。<u>“但有一件事我们不能轻举妄动，那就是使命——拉丁美洲中小企业的可持续发展。”</u>



How could FUNDES continue to fulfill its social mission while generating enough revenue to sustain its operations? The management team knew that MSMEs in Latin America were integral to the value chains of large corporations, operating as suppliers or distributors. Banana farmers for agriculture firms or mom-and-pop stores for beverage producers—there was a lot of interdependence, as well as a lot of potential for efficiency gains and business development if the MSMEs could be supported to operate more successfully and professionally. Frei believed that large corporations would be willing to provide much-needed financial support for FUNDES’ activities, “not necessarily because they are better people, but because they wanted to stay competitive … because the MSMEs are the backbone of their value chains.”

FUNDES 如何既能继续履行其社会使命，又能创造足够的收入来维持运营？管理团队知道，拉丁美洲的中小微企业作为供应商或分销商，是大公司价值链中不可或缺的一部分。香蕉种植者为农业公司服务，夫妻店为饮料生产商服务——如果中小微企业能够得到支持，以更成功、更专业的方式运营，那么以它们之间相互依存关系，在提高效率和发展业务的潜力都是巨大的。弗雷认为，大公司愿意为 FUNDES 的活动提供急需的财政支持，“不一定是因为他们是更优秀的人，而是因为他们希望保持竞争力......因为中小微企业是其价值链的支柱”。

He was proven right, as FUNDES worked with major companies such as Walmart. “Working on shared value allows us to have a direct impact on our business and on the different stakeholders with whom we work, such as MSMEs in agricultural products and mass consumption,” explained Viena Ochoa, corporate social responsibility manager at Walmart Central America, in 2015. “The challenge for them is to grow with us sustainably over time.” This need for sustainable growth was where FUNDES was actively supporting the MSMEs to match their corporate partners’ expectations.

事实证明他是对的，因为 FUNDES 与沃尔玛等大公司开展了合作。"在共享价值上的努力使我们能够对我们的业务以及与我们合作的不同利益攸关方（如农产品和大众消费领域的中小微企业）产生直接影响“。沃尔玛中美洲公司企业社会责任经理维埃纳-奥乔亚（Viena Ochoa）在 2015 年解释说：“他们面临的挑战是与我们一起长期可持续地发展“。FUNDES 积极得支持中小微企业实现可持续增长，以满足企业合作伙伴的期望。



FUNDES decided to go beyond simple knowledge delivery to knowledge implementation through retail consulting services. “The idea of our founder was great in its time, to directly help the MSMEs,” FUNDES’ current CEO, Elfid Torres, says. “There was an inflection point, where the team had to show courage, and if we would not have pivoted then, the organization would probably have ceased existing.”

FUNDES 决定，不仅提供单一的知识，还要通过零售咨询服务来落实知识。"我们创始人当时的想法非常好，就是直接帮助中小微企业“。FUNDES 现任首席执行官埃尔菲德-托雷斯（Elfid Torres）说：“当时出现了一个新的方向，团队需要具有一定的勇气转型。如果我们当时不做出转变，这个组织很可能就不复存在了。“



FUNDES’ new dual consulting model—offering support for both MSMEs and large multinational corporations—coincided with a growing interest in combining purpose with profit, particularly through the lens of “creating shared value,” a concept developed by Harvard Business School professors Michael Porter and Mark Kramer in 2011. They defined shared value as policies and practices that “enhance the competitiveness of a company while simultaneously advancing the economic and social conditions in the communities in which it operates.” Porter and Kramer saw FUNDES as a practical embodiment of this idea. They visited the organization multiple times and referenced it in high-profile discussions with executives.

人们对将目的与利润相结合的兴趣日益浓厚，尤其是通过 “创造共享价值 ”这一视角，“创造共享价值 ”是哈佛商学院教授迈克尔-波特（Michael Porter）和马克-克莱默（Mark Kramer）于 2011 年提出的概念。他们将共享价值定义为 “在提高公司竞争力的同时，改善公司所在社区的经济和社会状况 ”的政策和实践。在这样的考虑下，FUNDES 产生了新的双重咨询模式——既支持中小微企业，也支持大型跨国公司。波特和克拉默将 FUNDES 视为这一理念的具体体现。他们多次访问该组织，并在与高管的高调讨论中提及该组织。



> While SABMiller praised FUNDES’ activities for their impact, it wanted to reach far more small retailers selling their products than the numbers FUNDES provided, and at a lower cost per engagement. But FUNDES’ ability to scale had reached its limits.
>
> 尽管 SABMiller 对 FUNDES 活动的影响力大加赞赏，但它希望能接触到比 FUNDES 提供的数量更多的销售其产品的小零售商，而且每次接触的成本更低。但 FUNDES 的扩展能力已达到极限。

While the shared value model was appealing in theory, implementing it was challenging. FUNDES needed a team with a commercial mindset to pitch projects to multinationals and other for-profit firms—unfamiliar territory for many of its employees, most of whom had backgrounds in philanthropy. To align with this new model, CEO Frei and Torres, who at that time was in charge of the change management office, saw the need to establish a more commercially focused culture at FUNDES. “We had nonprofit professionals in our organization, and all of a sudden, we needed business-minded people,” Frei recalls. In 2008 and 2009, FUNDES let go of 150 out of 200 employees due to their lack of business experience and hired about 60 new employees to support its new consulting model.

虽然共享价值模式在理论上很有吸引力，但实施起来却充满挑战。FUNDES 需要一个具有商业思维的团队来向跨国公司和其他营利性公司推介项目——这对许多员工来说是个陌生的领域，因为他们中的大多数人都是慈善背景。为了与这种新模式保持一致，首席执行官弗雷和当时负责变革管理办公室的托雷斯认为有必要在 FUNDES 建立一种更加注重商业的文化。弗莱回忆说："我们的组织里有非营利组织的专业人士，突然之间，我们需要有商业头脑的人“。2008 年和 2009 年，由于缺乏商业经验，FUNDES 解雇了 200 名员工中的 150 人，并雇佣了约 60 名新员工来支持新的咨询模式。



The new FUNDES had to revise its strategy and mindset as well as overhaul its organization. While FUNDES remained dedicated to supporting MSMEs, it now needed a regional strategy to work effectively with multinational clients and benefit in a more coordinated way from its regional presence. More than 50 multinational enterprises, including Walmart, PepsiCo, CEMEX, Gas Natural, and MASISA, began to work with FUNDES to develop their value chains with MSMEs.

新的 FUNDES 必须修订其战略和思维方式，并对其组织进行全面改革。虽然 FUNDES 仍致力于支持中小微企业，但它现在需要一个区域战略，以便与跨国客户有效合作，并以更加协调的方式从其区域存在中获益。包括沃尔玛、百事可乐、CEMEX、天然气公司和 MASISA 在内的 50 多家跨国企业开始与 FUNDES 合作，与中小微企业共同开发价值链。



To better serve these multinational clients, who wanted integrated solutions in several countries, FUNDES consolidated its decentralized structure into regional or country offices that could coordinate more seamlessly with headquarters. FUNDES’ centralization marked a major cultural shift. <u>“The country kings had to take off their crown,”</u> Frei says. The organization’s headquarters in Costa Rica assumed a dual role, overseeing operations and supporting subsidiaries while directly coordinating a few multinational projects. The core team there, led by Torres, was ambitious, but initiatives often faced bottlenecks. Subsidiary managers sometimes hindered the flow of strategies from headquarters to local teams, making it challenging to fully implement new ideas.

为了更好地服务这些希望在多个国家获得综合解决方案的跨国客户，FUNDES 将其分散的结构整合为地区或国家办事处，以便与总部进行更无缝的协调。FUNDES 的集中化标志着一个重大的文化转变。弗雷说：<u>"国王必须摘下王冠"</u>。该组织位于哥斯达黎加的总部承担着双重角色，既要监督运营，为子公司提供支持，又要直接协调一些跨国项目。由托雷斯领导的核心团队雄心勃勃，但他们的工作经常遇到瓶颈。子公司经理有时会阻碍战略从总部向当地团队的流动，使其难以全面实施新想法。



“I don’t want to be a consulting company,” said a vocal subsidiary manager, who wished to remain nameless. “I want to do business with the government. I want to close deals with foundations. And those partners will not be working with us, if they think we are a consultancy.”

“我不想成为一家咨询公司，"一位不愿透露姓名的子公司经理大声说道。“我想与政府做生意。我想与基金会达成交易。如果这些合作伙伴认为我们是一家咨询公司，他们就不会与我们合作。“



What’s more, many years into the new strategy, some employees still saw FUNDES as an NGO rather than a consulting firm with a social mission. “I believe that our value proposition is to support and develop micro-, small-, and medium-sized companies; it is not to charge them or to generate profit,” said one project coordinator from Mexico who was also guaranteed anonymity. “As our director Ulrich Frei said, if you help develop these companies and help a little to generate economic benefit, you will reduce poverty. That is what I believe FUNDES should do.”

更重要的是，在新战略实施多年后，一些员工仍然认为 FUNDES 是一个非政府组织，而不是一家肩负社会使命的咨询公司。“一位不愿透露姓名的墨西哥项目协调员说："我认为，我们的价值主张是支持和发展中小微企业，而不是向它们收费或产生利润。正如我们的负责人乌尔里希-弗雷所说，如果你能帮助这些公司发展，帮助它们创造一点经济效益，你就能减少贫困。这就是我认为 FUNDES 应该做的事情。"



Despite such challenges, FUNDES’ shared value model gathered momentum, with every successful consulting project serving as an additional proof of concept. One exemplary success was a project with Brazilian steel producer Gerdau, which involved training and organizing thousands of scrap-metal collectors in Mexico, Venezuela, and Colombia to strengthen Gerdau’s supply chain. Gerdau sought two goals from the project: first, to work with its established suppliers on quality, cost reductions, and community development to achieve a relationship of mutual trust, respect, and sustainability; and second, to identify novel suppliers and to develop and integrate them into Gerdau’s supply chain. This large-scale project, supervised by managers from both FUNDES International and in-country heads, was carried out over 80 weeks in 2014 and 2015, improving the work of 58 Gerdau suppliers (13 in Mexico, 25 in Venezuela, and 20 in Colombia).

尽管面临这些挑战，FUNDES 的共享价值模式仍在不断发展壮大，每一个成功的咨询项目都是对概念的进一步证明。其中一个成功范例是与巴西钢铁生产商 Gerdau 合作的项目，该项目涉及培训和组织墨西哥、委内瑞拉和哥伦比亚的数千名废金属收集者，以加强格尔道公司的供应链。格尔道公司希望通过该项目实现两个目标：第一，与现有供应商在质量保证、降低成本和社区发展方面开展合作，以实现相互信任、尊重和可持续发展的关系；第二，发现新的供应商，并将其发展和整合到格尔道公司的供应链中。这一大型项目由 FUNDES 国际总部的管理人员和国内负责人共同监督，于 2014 年和 2015 年开展，历时 80 周，改善了 58 家格尔道公司供应商（墨西哥 13 家、委内瑞拉 25 家、哥伦比亚 20 家）的工作。

FUNDES expanded its client and beneficiary base, working increasingly with more corporate giants such as Coca-Cola, AB InBev, and Bimbo. For AB InBev and its subsidiary SABMiller, FUNDES implemented its first major cross-regional project across six countries—El Salvador, Honduras, Panama, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru—from 2013 to 2018 supporting small retailers not only to improve their overall business performance, but also to foster the quality of life for their employees and to take leadership roles in their communities. The project, called 4e, was first implemented in El Salvador to great success and then expanded across the region. The program had three objectives: first, the development of business capabilities (including group training with personalized technical advice); second, the improvement of quality of life for individuals and their families (including income management, investment, and life planning); and third, the fostering of community and social leadership (including training to develop social enterprises).

FUNDES 扩大了客户和受益群体，与可口可乐、百威英博和宾博等更多企业巨头的合作日益增多。2013 年至 2018 年期间，FUNDES 为百威英博及其子公司 SABMiller 在萨尔瓦多、洪都拉斯、巴拿马、哥伦比亚、厄瓜多尔和秘鲁六个国家实施了首个大型跨区域项目，不仅支持小型零售商提高整体经营业绩，还提高其员工的生活质量，并在社区中发挥领导作用。该项目名为 “4e”，首先在萨尔瓦多实施，取得了巨大成功，随后扩展到整个地区。该项目有三个目标：第一，发展业务能力（包括提供个性化技术咨询的团体培训）；第二，改善个人及其家庭的生活质量（包括收入管理、投资和生活规划）；第三，培养社区和社会领导能力（包括发展社会企业的培训）。



Based on the first successful examples, FUNDES joined forces with Inter-American Development Bank’s Multilateral Investment Fund (MIF) to launch a $20 million project to support small retailers’ business performance. It included a 12-week cycle of recruitment, diagnosis, classroom training, in-store mentoring, monitoring, and awarding of certificates. The first regional iteration of 4e reached 5,631 stores in total (2,780 in Colombia, 622 in Ecuador, 376 in El Salvador, 269 in Honduras, 175 in Panama, and 1,409 in Peru).

在首批成功案例的基础上，FUNDES 与美洲开发银行的多边投资基金（MIF）联手启动了一个 2 000 万美元的项目，以支持小型零售商的经营业绩。该项目包括为期 12 周的招聘、诊断、课堂培训、店内指导、监督和颁发证书。4e 项目的第一个地区迭代共惠及 5 631 家商店（哥伦比亚 2 780 家、厄瓜多尔 622 家、萨尔瓦多 376 家、洪都拉斯 269 家、巴拿马 175 家、秘鲁 1 409 家）。



SABMiller’s ambition was to become more embedded in strong and supportive regional business ecosystems and work with mom-and-pop stores in a way that would ultimately impact the lives of up to 10 million people. “The *tenderos* [‘shopkeepers’] are community references and opinion leaders,” Torres wrote. “Their shops are centers of information and integration for their neighborhood, they build social capital and create a culture of empathy.”

SABMiller 的雄心壮志是进一步融入强大且具有支持性的地区商业生态系统，并和夫妻店合作，最终影响多达 1 千万人生活的方式。“托雷斯写道："店主是社区的参照物和意见领袖。他们的商店是社区的信息中心而邻里融合的中心，他们建立社会资本并创造一种同理心文化"。



While SABMiller praised FUNDES’ activities for their impact, it wanted to reach far more small retailers selling their products than the numbers FUNDES provided, and at a lower cost per engagement. But FUNDES’ ability to scale had reached its limits, as it could rely on only six project managers and roughly 100 consultants for all of Colombia, Ecuador, El Salvador, Honduras, Peru, and Panama.

尽管 SABMiller 公司对 FUNDES 的活动所产生的影响表示赞赏，但它希望接触到比 FUNDES 提供的数量更多的销售其产品的小零售商，而且每次接触的成本更低。但是，FUNDES 的扩展能力已经达到极限，因为它只能依靠六名项目经理和大约 100 名顾问在哥伦比亚、厄瓜多尔、萨尔瓦多、洪都拉斯、秘鲁和巴拿马开展工作。



This lack of scalability was a complaint shared by other corporate clients. Until 2016, FUNDES had worked with 450,000 MSMEs. While that number may sound big, it represents only a fraction of Latin America’s 20 million MSMEs. Furthermore, FUNDES’ big corporate clients concluded that scaling through traditional consulting was financially unsustainable.

其他企业客户也在抱怨缺乏可扩展性。截至 2016 年，FUNDES 已与 45 万家中小微企业开展了合作。虽然这个数字听起来很大，但只占拉丁美洲 2000 万家中小微企业的一小部分。此外，FUNDES 的大企业客户认为，通过传统咨询来扩大规模在财务上是不可持续的。



“Just when we reached break-even, and we were ready to conquer the world, our clients came with new demands,” Frei recalls. “They told us they liked the things we do, but they would not only like us to do this with hundreds or a few thousand MSMEs—but with a hundred thousand MSMEs. At the same time, they said that the cost should be reduced.”

“正当我们达到收支平衡，准备征服世界时，我们的客户提出了新的要求，"弗雷回忆道。“他们告诉我们，他们喜欢我们所做的事情，但他们不仅希望我们与数百家或数千家中小微企业合作，还希望我们与十万家中小微企业合作。同时，他们还说应该降低成本"。



While FUNDES confronted this problem in its work with corporate clients, it cultivated other partnerships with international philanthropic foundations. One of those partners, the elea Foundation for Ethics in Globalization, would eventually lead FUNDES into its next reinvention. The mission of elea, founded by Susanne and Peter Wuffli, is to fight poverty with entrepreneurial means, leveraging the opportunities of globalization. The collaboration between FUNDES and elea began with a joint project to support small mom-and-pop shops in Bolivia in 2009 and developed over the years into a closer relationship.

FUNDES 在与企业客户合作的过程中遇到了这个问题，与此同时，它还与国际慈善基金会建立了其他合作关系。其中一个合作伙伴，即 elea 全球化伦理基金会，最终将带领 FUNDES 进入下一个重塑阶段。elea 基金会由苏珊妮（Susanne）和彼得·威夫利（Peter Wuffli）创立，其使命是利用全球化机遇，以创业手段消除贫困。FUNDES 与 elea 的合作始于 2009 年在玻利维亚开展的一个支持小型夫妻店的联合项目，多年来发展成为一种更加紧密的关系。



“We realized that FUNDES not only had a mission that was closely aligned to ours, but that they were also very entrepreneurial and understood the local value chains really well,” recalls Adrian Ackeret, chief investment officer at elea. “However, the hope that it would be possible to win a growing number of large consumer-goods manufacturers as main clients for this approach within a few years was too ambitious. What was needed was a stronger focus on technology to reduce cost, foster scalability, and effectively use FUNDES’ network.”

“我们意识到，FUNDES 不仅与我们的使命高度一致，而且他们还非常具有创业精神，非常了解当地的价值链”。埃利亚首席投资官阿德里安·阿克雷特回忆说。“然而，希望在几年内赢得越来越多的大型消费品制造商作为这这种方法的主要客户，这种想法过于雄心勃勃。我们需要的是更加注重技术，以降低成本、提高可扩展性并有效利用 FUNDES 的网络"。



## Scalability Through Digitization 

## 通过数字化实现可扩展性

From 2016 to 2021, FUNDES gradually realized that it could not continue its shared value consulting services at the scale demanded by its corporate partners. It faced a choice: Continue the same model on a smaller scale or embark on yet another transformation to continue its mission? FUNDES leadership decided to pivot once again to guarantee financial stability, corporate partner satisfaction, and scaling impact. Over time, it developed a digital model that amended and could eventually replace the old and costly model based on country offices.

从 2016 年到 2021 年，FUNDES 逐渐意识到，它无法按照企业合作伙伴的要求继续提供共享价值咨询服务。它面临着一个选择： 是在更小的规模上继续相同的模式，还是开始另一次转型以继续其使命？FUNDES 领导层决定再次转型，以确保财务稳定、企业合作伙伴满意并扩大影响。随着时间的推移，基金会开发了一种数字化模式，该模式可以修正并最终取代以国家办事处为基础的成本高昂的旧模式。



In 2016, Armando Moguel joined FUNDES as strategy and business development director. A physicist and systems engineer by training and founder of a collaborative supply-chain platform, Moguel brought an outsider’s perspective. He diagnosed that the regional synergies envisioned by Torres and Frei had not materialized. In fact, early international projects, such as the one with SABMiller, exposed FUNDES’ country and regional structures as overly complex and difficult for clients to navigate. Furthermore, the consulting model struggled to meet clients’ demands for scalability and efficiency. “The clients wanted to have twice the work done for half the price,” Frei said.

2016 年，阿曼多-莫盖尔（Armando Moguel）加入 FUNDES，担任战略和业务发展总监。作为一名训练有素物理学家和系统工程师，同时也是协作供应链平台的创始人，莫盖尔带来了局外人的视角。他发现，托雷斯和弗雷所设想的地区协同效应并未实现。事实上，早期的国际项目，如与 SABMiller 合作的项目，暴露出 FUNDES 的国家和地区结构过于复杂，客户难以驾驭。此外，咨询模式也难以满足客户对可扩展性和效率的要求。弗雷说：“客户希望以一半的价格完成两倍的工作。”

The management team decided on two critical responses: First, to reduce costs, FUNDES reorganized its country units into a centralized, hub-based structure. The majority of staff would work in Costa Rica and Mexico and focus on projects with cross-country synergies. Second, to enhance scalability, FUNDES started experimenting with new technologies and business models to reach more MSMEs without sacrificing social impact.

管理团队决定采取两个关键的应对措施： 首先，为了降低成本，FUNDES 将其国家单位重组为一个集中的、以中心为基础的结构。大部分员工将在哥斯达黎加和墨西哥工作，重点关注具有跨国协同效应的项目。其次，为了提高可扩展性，FUNDES 开始尝试新的技术和业务模式，以便在不牺牲社会影响的情况下帮助更多的中小微企业。



During this time, FUNDES’ leaders decided to focus on customer value—determining what clients needed and what they were willing to pay for it. However, Torres also drove FUNDES to make the organization far more efficient. He closed several offices to streamline operations. For Torres, FUNDES’ core competency involved creating project pitches, selling them to clients, and implementing them through local project managers. He described this period as a “search for pain in the market,” seeking out issues FUNDES could help MSMEs address while making a meaningful impact on them. “The truth is that all of this was gradual, the regional reorganization,” Torres says. “Today we only have one managing director for all regions.”

在此期间，FUNDES 的领导人决定将重点放在客户价值上，即确定客户需要什么以及愿意为此支付多少费用。不过，托雷斯也推动 FUNDES 大大提高了组织效率。他关闭了几个办事处，以精简业务。对托雷斯而言，FUNDES 的核心竞争力在于制定项目计划、向客户销售计划并通过当地项目经理实施计划。他将这段时期描述为 “寻找市场痛点”，寻找 FUNDES 可以帮助中小微企业解决的问题，同时对他们产生有意义的影响。“事实是，所有这些都是渐进式的，即区域重组,”托雷斯说。“今天，我们只有一个总经理负责所有地区。”



To further streamline their operations and better serve their clients, FUNDES embraced digital solutions, identifying new opportunities to create impact while maintaining its financial sustainability. FUNDES’ leaders deemed the monetization of its core knowledge—data on MSMEs, which would be crucial for analytics of supply-chain and distribution networks for big corporations—as especially promising. But such a change to FUNDES’ business model would require new agreements and new partner firms. Its leaders engaged in heated discussions on whether the digital future was the right path. But they ultimately agreed that they had little choice: They had to embrace the new digital model if FUNDES wanted to continue to serve its mission.

为了进一步简化业务流程，更好地为客户服务，FUNDES 采用了数字化解决方案，在保持财务可持续性的同时，寻找新的机会来创造影响力。FUNDES 的领导人认为，将其核心知识——中小微企业数据货币化特别有前途，这些数据对于分析大公司的供应链和分销网络至关重要。但是，要改变 FUNDES 的业务模式，就必须签订新的协议，建立新的合作公司。FUNDES 的领导者们就数字化未来是否是一条正确的道路展开了激烈的讨论。但他们最终一致认为，他们别无选择：如果 FUNDES 想要继续履行其使命，他们就必须接受新的数字化模式。



In one of the first new initiatives, FUNDES created a platform in Central America to geo-locate mom-and-pop shops, providing them with consulting and logistics support. The platform also generated market insights for larger clients working with small retailers. At the same time, Elfid Torres led brainstorming sessions on the future of FUNDES. Armando Moguel was added to those sessions to innovate the organization and its operating model. Torres found that adding Moguel was “like a reagent that you add to another static fluid”—he helped catalyze an abundance of new ideas. In 2016 FUNDES launched a unit called the “Innovation Factory,” made up of a small team tasked with experimenting with digital strategies to leverage this new knowledge base. After two years, in 2018, the Innovation Factory evolved into FUNDES Digital, a separate legal entity led by Moguel.

在首批新举措中，FUNDES 在中美洲创建了一个平台，对夫妻店进行地理定位，为其提供咨询和物流支持。该平台还为与小型零售商合作的大客户提供市场洞察力。与此同时，埃尔菲尔德-托雷斯 （Elfid Torres）还主持了关于 FUNDES 未来发展的集思广益会议。阿曼多-莫盖尔（Armando Moguel）也加入了这些会议，以创新组织和运营模式。托雷斯发现，莫盖尔的加入 “就像在另一种静态液体中加入了一种试剂”——他帮助催生了大量新想法。2016 年，FUNDES 成立了一个名为 “创新工厂 ”的部门，由一个小型团队组成，其任务是尝试使用数字战略来利用这一新的知识库。两年后的2018年，“创新工厂 ”发展成为 FUNDES Digital，一个由莫盖尔领导的独立法人实体。

FUNDES quickly proved that digital tools were not only a way to earn money but also instrumental to continuing to serve Latin America’s MSMEs. FUNDES Digital launched Facebook-based communities for mini-store owners in several countries, including Peru, Costa Rica, Colombia, El Salvador, Honduras, and Chile. The groups served as a platform for FUNDES to provide free content and gather insights on MSMEs’ digital skills. Next, FUNDES launched an app called EntreTenderos, which [delivered free business knowledge capsules to small-shop owners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sH309TV51S4). Mini-grocery-shop owners in Colombia appreciated the help and rated the community highly on Facebook. Comments included: “Every video did help me a lot” (Roa Adrigarz), “Good advice on how to manage a store” (Simon Diaz Mora), and “What a good site. We needed it” (Alvaro Jaramillo Moreno).

FUNDES 很快证明，数字工具不仅是一种赚钱方式，也有助于继续为拉丁美洲的中小微企业提供服务。FUNDES Digital 在秘鲁、哥斯达黎加、哥伦比亚、萨尔瓦多、洪都拉斯和智利等多个国家为迷你店主推出了基于 Facebook 的社区。这些社区是 FUNDES 提供免费内容和收集中小微企业数字技能见解的平台。接下来，FUNDES 推出了一款名为 EntreTenderos 的应用程序，向小店主免费提供商业知识囊。哥伦比亚的小型杂货店店主对这一帮助表示感谢，并在 Facebook 上对该社区给予了高度评价。评论包括 “每个视频都对我帮助很大“（罗亚-阿德里加兹的评价）、”关于如何管理店铺的好建议“（西蒙-迪亚斯-莫拉的评价）和 ”多好的网站。我们需要它"（阿尔瓦罗-哈拉米略-莫雷诺的评价）。



FUNDES also began collecting and digitizing data for sale to fast-moving consumer-goods (FMCG) companies interested in better understanding the behavior of FUNDES’ retail network. However, the idea proved to be too challenging to implement—it was not technically feasible to constantly update the data on the MSMEs. By late 2019, FUNDES Digital had initiated a variety of projects. However, as is typical for such early-stage digital initiatives, they did not yield significant cash flow but rather depended on capital to grow. The funding for these initiatives was provided by elea Foundation as an impact investor and by FUNDES, which tapped into its strategic reserve.

FUNDES 还开始收集数据并将其数字化，以便出售给有兴趣更好地了解 FUNDES 零售网络行为的快速消费品（FMCG）公司。然而，事实证明这一想法的实施难度很大——不断更新中小微企业的数据在技术上并不可行。到 2019 年底，FUNDES Digital 启动了多个项目。然而，与此类早期数字计划的典型情况一样，这些计划并未产生大量现金流，而是依赖资本来发展。elea 基金会作为影响力投资者为这些项目提供了资金，而 FUNDES 则动用了其战略储备金。



During this phase of testing its digital bona fides, FUNDES’ partnership with elea evolved into a closer collaboration and joint development. While being a philanthropic organization, elea had always applied an investor logic, striving to establish initiatives that relieve poverty but can be self-sustaining in the long run. This experience was very helpful for informing FUNDES’ digital transformation.

在这一阶段，FUNDES 与 elea 的合作伙伴关系逐渐发展为更紧密的合作和共同发展。作为一家慈善组织，elea 一直秉承投资者的逻辑，致力于建立既能缓解贫困，又能长期自我维持的项目。这一经验对 FUNDES 的数字化转型非常有帮助。



“When FUNDES was building up its digital activities, there was the need to establish an early-stage investors’ logic,” Ackeret says. “That was entirely new to FUNDES and in some sense also to us, as we used to invest in later-stage entrepreneurial initiatives. I was impressed by the entrepreneurial mindset at FUNDES and how the top management was radically questioning their established model. There was a steep learning curve, which we at elea could support.”

“FUNDES 在开展数字业务时，需要建立一个早期投资者逻辑，"阿克里特说。“这对 FUNDES 来说是全新的，从某种意义上说，对我们来说也是全新的，因为我们过去投资的都是处于后期阶段的创业项目。FUNDES 的创业思维给我留下了深刻印象，高层管理者对他们的既定模式提出了彻底的质疑。这是一条陡峭的学习曲线，我们 elea 可以提供支持。



In 2020, as FUNDES explored new business opportunities in the digital world, the COVID-19 pandemic struck. MSMEs in Latin America were hit particularly hard. FUNDES itself was forced to accelerate its transition toward a more integrated, digital-first, and virtual organization. In 2021, FUNDES launched Merkomuna, a digital platform for community-based group buying with small stores serving as distribution points. The new venture quickly showed potential by attracting investors and a sizable user base of shop owners: After two years of operation, it spun off from FUNDES Digital with a dedicated CEO and board of directors, which included seats for FUNDES and elea. Furthermore, Michael Jost, former CFO of Nestlé Mexico, became a board member and investor.

2020 年，正当 FUNDES 探索数字世界的新商机时，COVID-19 大流行病袭来。拉丁美洲的中小微企业受到的冲击尤为严重。FUNDES 本身也被迫加速向一个更加综合、数字优先和虚拟的组织转型。2021 年，FUNDES 推出了 Merkomuna，这是一个以社区为基础的团购数字平台，由小商店作为分销点。这家新企业迅速显示出潜力，吸引了投资者和相当规模的店主用户群： 经过两年的运营，Merkomuna 从 FUNDES Digital 分离出来，并设立了专门的首席执行官和董事会，其中包括 FUNDES 和 elea 的席位。此外，雀巢墨西哥公司前首席财务官 Michael Jost 也成为了董事会成员和投资者。



Jost had experience in FMCG and retail operations, including logistics, last-mile solutions, and the peculiarities of food products and their margins in value chains. He was convinced by FUNDES’ well-organized approach to developing the business. “They looked at 20 different models of how Merkomuna could work, and they did systematic benchmarking, in particular with Pinduoduo, a famous Chinese group buying site,” Jost says. “Based on this benchmarking, we concluded that the group buying model could be applied to small *changarro* stores [mini-supermarkets]. In Mexico alone we had a million changarros. … From my experience in FMCG, I knew that large corporations do not have this direct connection because it is too much of a headache to manage them.”

Jost 拥有快速消费品和零售业的运营经验，包括物流、最后一英里解决方案、食品的特殊性及其在价值链中的利润。他被 FUNDES 井井有条的业务发展方式所折服。“他们研究了 20 种不同的 Merkomuna 运作模式，并进行了系统的基准测试，特别是与中国著名的团购网站拼多多进行了对比，"约斯特说。“基于这一基准，我们得出结论，团购模式可以应用于小型*changarro*商店（小型超市）。仅在墨西哥，我们就有 100 万家 changarro。......根据我在快消品行业的经验，我知道大公司没有这种直接联系，因为管理起来太麻烦了。



The platform raised $2 million in capital within 18 months and achieved annual sales of $1.5 million. All of this was achieved through serving only a part of Toluca, Mexico, where Merkomuna scaled quickly to serve 4,000 small stores. Despite such growth, however, Merkomuna never reached profitability.

该平台在 18 个月内筹集了 200 万美元资金，年销售额达到 150 万美元。所有这些都是在仅为墨西哥托卢卡部分地区提供服务的情况下实现的，Merkomuna 在那里迅速扩展到为 4000 家小商店提供服务。尽管取得了这样的增长，但 Merkomuna 却从未实现盈利。



“In the end we had scaled too fast, moved to a bigger warehouse, invested a lot in the app, integrated the back-office logistics, and hired too many people,” Jost says. The challenges of the capital-intensive model were further magnified by an operational disconnect between Merkomuna management and FUNDES leadership. Ultimately, Merkomuna closed in late 2023. Despite this setback, FUNDES learned a lot from the process and saw an opportunity to build on the insights gained; it formalized its venture-building approach by more systematically exploring, ideating, prototyping, and launching social ventures.

“最后，我们的规模扩大得太快，搬到了一个更大的仓库，在应用程序上投入了大量资金，整合了后台物流，还雇佣了太多员工，"约斯特说。Merkomuna 管理层与 FUNDES 领导层之间的运营脱节进一步加剧了资本密集型模式的挑战。最终，Merkomuna 于 2023 年底关闭。尽管遭遇挫折，但 FUNDES 仍从这一过程中学到了很多，并看到了在所学知识的基础上再接再厉的机会；FUNDES 通过更系统地探索、构思、原型设计和启动社会企业，正式确立了其风险投资建设方法。



## Institutionalizing Social Venturing 

## 社会风险投资制度化

Back in 2020, during the height of the pandemic, as FUNDES’ leadership yet again debated the future of the organization in the face of a global crisis, two young managers, Solutions Manager Mauricio Ramirez and Director of Growth Corentin Larue, weighed in. “Why do we not become the Rocket Internet of solutions for micro and small businesses in Latin America?” they recall asking.

2020 年，在大流行病最严重的时候，FUNDES 的领导层又一次在全球危机面前讨论组织的未来，两位年轻的经理，解决方案经理毛里西奥-拉米雷斯（Mauricio Ramirez）和发展总监科伦廷-拉鲁（Corentin Larue），对此发表了自己的看法。“他们回忆说："我们为什么不成为拉丁美洲小微企业解决方案的 Rocket Internet 呢？

They were inspired by the business model of the German-based venture capital firm Rocket Internet, which specializes in replicating successful internet-based business models in emerging and underserved markets. Just as Rocket Internet launched e-commerce platforms such as Zalando in Europe and Lazada in Southeast Asia, Ramirez and Larue saw potential for a similar model in Latin America. “This came from the realization that we were working with micro and small businesses, and we saw more and more start-ups offering products and services to this audience that we knew very well,” Larue says.

他们的灵感来自德国风险投资公司 Rocket Internet 的商业模式，该公司专门在新兴和服务不足的市场复制成功的互联网商业模式。正如 Rocket Internet 在欧洲推出 Zalando 和在东南亚推出 Lazada 等电子商务平台一样，拉米雷斯和拉鲁也看到了拉丁美洲类似模式的潜力。“拉鲁说："这源于我们与微型和小型企业的合作，我们看到越来越多的初创企业向我们非常熟悉的这部分受众提供产品和服务。



The upshot of this rethinking was [Fundamental](https://www.fundamental.lat/), a social-venture incubator to launch solution-oriented businesses out of the project portfolio that FUNDES had developed. Once again, however, FUNDES had to figure out what this new venture’s trajectory would be. While FUNDES’ early digital initiatives focused on complementing and supporting its consulting model, building new ventures began to turn into an independent avenue for creating impact. Fundamental launched start-ups such as Simón, a software-as-a-service platform that allows large companies to train micro-entrepreneurs in the value chain or workers who serve these micro-enterprises via a TikTok-like video format; Voalá, a micro-franchising social venture supporting beauty salons in Guatemala by offering beauty professionals “a product marketplace to buy and sell, certified training and mentoring, and in-person community events”; Ékole, a healthy-snack enterprise connecting producers to markets; and Trófica, an end-to-end recycling service. Today, Fundamental has developed a robust portfolio of innovative solutions addressing the core business needs of MSMEs, such as last-mile delivery, e-learning, and micro-franchises.

这一反思的结果是[Fundamental](https://www.fundamental.lat/)，这是一个社会企业孵化器，从 FUNDES 开发的项目组合中推出以解决方案为导向的企业。然而，FUNDES 又一次必须弄清这个新企业的发展轨迹。虽然 FUNDES 早期的数字计划主要是对其咨询模式的补充和支持，但建立新的企业开始成为创造影响力的独立途径。Fundamental创办了一些新企业，如Simon，这是一个软件即服务平台，允许大公司通过类似TikTok的视频形式培训价值链上的微型企业家或为这些微型企业服务的工人；Voalá，这是一家微型特许经营社会企业，通过为美容专业人士提供 “产品买卖市场、认证培训和指导以及面对面的社区活动”，为危地马拉的美容院提供支持；Ékole，这是一家连接生产者和市场的健康零食企业；Trófica，这是一家端到端回收服务企业。如今，Fundamental 公司已经开发了一系列强大的创新解决方案，以满足中小微企业的核心业务需求，如最后一英里配送、电子学习和微型特许经营。



Fundamental’s primary focus was on exploration and ideation. Once a venture concept showed promise, Fundamental looked for an entrepreneur to take it forward, offering a 30 percent stake that could grow to 60 or 70 percent over time. Fundamental’s goal was to launch at least one new social venture each year. After 18 months of incubation and venture building, projects would seek independent financing and further development.

Fundamental 公司的主要重点是探索和构思。一旦某个风险企业的理念被看好，Fundamental 就会寻找一位企业家将其发扬光大，并提供 30% 的股份，随着时间的推移，股份可以增加到 60% 或 70%。Fundamental 的目标是每年至少推出一家新的社会风险企业。经过 18 个月的孵化和风险建设后，项目将寻求独立融资和进一步发展。



“This is an ecosystem,” says Torres about the interplay between FUNDES’ consulting model and Fundamental. “FUNDES stays the organization that works with the MSMEs and the corporate world, but that also looks for what could be a social venture [for Fundamental]. Then, the [Fundamental] ventures can bring business to FUNDES and serve as examples of how the corporate world can integrate social value creation in their core and not only entertain CSR [corporate social responsibility].”

“这是一个生态系统，”托雷斯谈到 FUNDES 的咨询模式与 Fundamental 之间的相互作用时说：“FUNDES 是一个与中小微企业和企业界合作的组织，同时也在寻找可以成为社会企业的企业。然后，Fundamental 企业可以为 FUNDES 带来业务，并成为企业界如何将社会价值创造纳入其核心而不仅仅是履行企业社会责任的范例。



> FUNDES is an exciting example of how difficult it can be to pursue a social mission over several decades. While its transformations have all turned out to be effective over time, the journey has been anything but easy.
>
> FUNDES 是一个令人兴奋的例子，它说明了几十年来履行社会使命是多么困难。虽然随着时间的推移，其转型都取得了成效，但这一路走来却并非一帆风顺。



FUNDES’ transformation into a social-venture incubator was facilitated through partnerships with an array of philanthropic investors, including Julius Bär Foundation, Leopold Bachmann Stiftung, and Argidius, as well as impact investors like Fourfold Foundation, GitLab Foundation, and elea. These funders not only contributed financial support but also helped implement the new operating model.

FUNDES 通过与 Julius Bär 基金会、Leopold Bachmann 基金会和 Argidius 等一系列慈善投资者，以及 Fourfold 基金会、GitLab 基金会和 elea 等影响力投资者合作，促进了向社会企业孵化器的转型。这些资助者不仅提供了资金支持，还帮助实施了新的运营模式。



“In the beginning, there was a strong focus on strategy, and we have probably put too much emphasis on it,” Ackeret says. “In hindsight, we should have put extra emphasis on establishing reporting standards and financial transparency for investors. FUNDES has always been very professional at impact reporting. But reporting to investors is more about KPIs [key performance indicators] and proof points.”

阿克雷特说：“一开始，我们非常重视战略，可能也过于强调战略了。事后看来，我们应该更加重视为投资者建立报告标准和财务透明度”。FUNDES 在影响报告方面一直非常专业。但向投资者报告更多的是KPI（关键绩效指标）和证明点。



In FUNDES’ previous transformation, CEO Torres hired business managers to replace philanthropy-focused staff. This time he brought on board individuals with entrepreneurial mindsets who wanted to develop their stakes within ventures and grow alongside them. To further this new model, FUNDES also promoted talent from within. This new generation of staff included leaders like Mauricio Ramírez, who became FUNDES’ new managing director in October 2023; Corentin Larue, who became Fundamental’s managing director in early 2022; and Maxime Braun, an experienced venture builder from Paris, who became Fundamental’s portfolio director in February 2022. Amicably described by Frei as “hipsters,” Fundamental’s teams gathered in Mexico City, a capital for digital nomads, working remotely from the managers on the ground who looked after clients in FUNDES’ core markets across Latin America. Located in its largest market, FUNDES’ Mexico City office has become its strategic hub.

在 FUNDES 的上一次转型中，首席执行官托雷斯聘请了业务经理来取代专注于慈善事业的员工。这一次，他引进了具有创业精神的人才，他们希望在企业中发展自己的股份，并与企业共同成长。为了推进这一新模式，FUNDES 还从内部提拔人才。新一代员工包括毛里西奥-拉米雷斯（Mauricio Ramírez）、科伦坦-拉鲁（Corentin Larue）和马克西姆-布劳恩（Maxime Braun）等领导者，毛里西奥-拉米雷斯于2023年10月成为FUNDES的新任总经理，科伦坦-拉鲁于2022年初成为Fundamental的总经理，马克西姆-布劳恩是一位来自巴黎的经验丰富的风险投资建设者，于2022年2月成为Fundamental的投资组合总监。被弗雷称为 “潮人 ”的Fundamental团队聚集在数字游民之都墨西哥城，与FUNDES在拉丁美洲核心市场的客户经理进行远程办公。FUNDES 的墨西哥城办事处位于其最大的市场，已成为其战略枢纽。



### Lessons From FUNDES

### FUNDES 经验教训

At FUNDES’ 40th anniversary party, different generations of FUNDES management met to celebrate their past achievements and envision a future for the organization. The evening captured the development of FUNDES: While each generation had faced and mastered unique challenges, all of them were united by the enduring mission of helping MSMEs bring prosperity to Latin America. FUNDES’ fidelity to its mission was confirmed by a recent beneficiary, Luis Antonio Tello, the owner of Shucos Estelita, a sandwich store in a low-income neighborhood in Guatemala City. “They helped me realize my dream for my whole family,” Tello said. “Thank you, FUNDES!”

在 FUNDES 40 周年庆典晚会上，FUNDES 各代管理层共聚一堂，庆祝过去的成就，展望组织的未来。晚会记录了 FUNDES 的发展历程： 虽然每一代人都面临和应对了独特的挑战，但他们都被帮助中小微企业为拉丁美洲带来繁荣这一永恒使命团结在一起。最近的一位受益人路易斯-安东尼奥-特洛（Luis Antonio Tello）证实了 FUNDES 对其使命的忠诚，他是 Shucos Estelita（一家位于危地马拉城低收入社区的三明治店）的店主。“特洛说："他们帮助我实现了全家的梦想。“谢谢你们，FUNDES！”



Throughout FUNDES’ transformations, Frei maintained oversight, acting as the steward of the organization’s mission. Although FUNDES remained steadfast in its commitment to Latin American MSMEs, its definition of “impact” has evolved. In FUNDES’ early years, each employee could immediately see the impact he or she was having on MSMEs. In the second, consulting phase, the understanding of impact became broader and more complex, as it also referred to FUNDES’ large corporate partners. This complexity has gained even more momentum for the more recent social-venture initiatives.

在 FUNDES 的整个转型过程中，弗雷始终担任该组织使命的监管人。尽管 FUNDES 仍坚定不移地致力于拉美中小微企业，但其对 “影响 ”的定义已发生了变化。在 FUNDES 成立初期，每位员工都能立即看到自己对中小微企业产生的影响。在第二个咨询阶段，对 “影响 ”的理解变得更加广泛和复杂，因为它也指 FUNDES 的大型企业合作伙伴。这种复杂性在最近的社会风险倡议中得到了更大的发展。



“The more investors want to measure, the more we have to measure,” Frei says. “But those metrics don’t always relate to impact. For example, if 100,000 people use our app, we cannot assume that we lift 100,000 people out of poverty. This was different in the beginning of FUNDES, where we could directly see our impact for MSMEs.”

“投资者想要衡量的越多，我们需要衡量的就越多，"弗雷说。“但这些衡量标准并不总是与影响力相关。例如，如果有 10 万人使用我们的应用程序，我们就不能假定我们让 10 万人摆脱了贫困。这在 FUNDES 成立之初有所不同，我们可以直接看到我们对中小微企业的影响。



The FUNDES story ultimately reveals the conditions, opportunities, and challenges associated with the long-term pursuit of a unifying social mission under changing internal and external circumstances. First, it shows that realizing a long-term sustainable impact for society requires dedicated and very entrepreneurial managers. In the case of FUNDES, the entrepreneurial mindset is rooted in both its mission and the people in charge of driving this mission. FUNDES executives showed a flair for identifying opportunities, took calculated risks, solved problems creatively, persisted in the face of challenges, and exhibited a proactive and innovative approach to business and societal issues. For example, the first transformation into a shared value consultancy required the identification of a radically new business model where there were no role models on the market.

FUNDES 的故事最终揭示了在不断变化的内部和外部环境下长期追求统一的社会使命所面临的条件、机遇和挑战。首先，它表明，要实现对社会的长期可持续影响，就需要尽职尽责、极具企业家精神的管理者。就 FUNDES 而言，企业家思维既植根于其使命，也植根于负责推动这一使命的人员。FUNDES 的管理人员善于发现机会，敢于承担风险，创造性地解决问题，面对挑战坚持不懈，并以积极创新的方式处理业务和社会问题。例如，FUNDES 首次转型为共享价值咨询公司，需要确定一种全新的业务模式，而市场上并没有这样的模式。



Second, FUNDES had to enter entirely new market domains to continue following its mission. Despite pushback from many employees, managers persisted and entered uncharted territory. During the first transformation, they began collaborating with large retail multinationals that differed dramatically from the MSMEs that were FUNDES’ prior customers. Similarly, the second transformation into a social-venture incubator brought again a new market logic and required FUNDES to manage a novel set of key performance indicators, such as user numbers, scalability, or cash flow.

其次，FUNDES 必须进入全新的市场领域，以继续履行其使命。尽管遭到许多员工的反对，但管理人员仍坚持不懈，进入了未知领域。在第一次转型中，他们开始与大型跨国零售企业合作，而这些企业与 FUNDES 之前的客户--中小微企业--大相径庭。同样，在第二次转型中，FUNDES 再次转型为社会风险孵化器，这也带来了新的市场逻辑，要求 FUNDES 管理一系列新的关键绩效指标，如用户数量、可扩展性或现金流。



Third, new market domains came with the need to embrace new stakeholder groups. FUNDES’ early days as a philanthropic organization required it to act very donor-centric and align to the vision and funding purpose of the donor. Foundations remain important today, but in different roles. For example, elea Foundation became more engaged than initial donors. However, complexity was rising with every transformation. As FUNDES is a shared value consulting firm, large corporations and governmental institutions had to be managed carefully without alienating the existing stakeholders. The latest transformation increased stakeholder complexity even further, with many more organizations involved. While before, there was typically one foundation providing financial support, FUNDES management now deals with boards of investors consisting of multiple foundations and funds. Also internally, FUNDES today needs to cater to very distinct demands, such as those of the IT specialists who require remote work opportunities and different incentive mechanisms than those of the frontline consultants working directly with MSMEs.

第三，新的市场领域需要接纳新的利益相关者群体。FUNDES 早期是一家慈善机构，这就要求它以捐赠者为中心，与捐赠者的愿景和资助目的保持一致。如今，基金会依然重要，但扮演着不同的角色。例如，elea 基金会比最初的捐赠者参与度更高。然而，每一次转型的复杂性都在上升。由于 FUNDES 是一家共享价值咨询公司，因此必须在不疏远现有利益相关者的情况下谨慎管理大型企业和政府机构。最近的转型进一步增加了利益相关者的复杂性，涉及到更多的组织。以前，通常只有一个基金会提供资金支持，而现在，FUNDES 管理层要与由多个基金会和基金组成的投资委员会打交道。此外，在内部，FUNDES 如今需要满足截然不同的需求，例如信息技术专家需要远程工作机会，他们与直接为中小微企业服务的一线顾问需要不同的激励机制。



Finally, FUNDES is an exciting example of how difficult it can be to pursue a social mission over several decades. While its transformations have all turned out to be effective over time, the journey has been anything but easy. There were numerous drawbacks, challenges, and conflicts. The good news, however, is that the experiences and capabilities that the organization has accumulated over the last 40 years will likely help it to continue its transformation journey and defend its mission, whatever new challenges await on the horizon.
最后，FUNDES 是一个令人兴奋的例子，它说明了几十年来履行社会使命是多么困难。虽然随着时间的推移，其转型都取得了成效，但这一路走来却并非一帆风顺。其中有无数的弊端、挑战和冲突。不过，好消息是，该组织在过去 40 年中积累的经验和能力可能会帮助它继续其转型之旅，并捍卫其使命，无论新的挑战在地平线上等待着它。
