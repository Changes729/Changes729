# The Sound of No Music

# 无乐之音

Like many nonprofits, the Oakland Symphony failed to understand the distinction between mission and strategy. This mistake helped kill the venerable orchestra

与许多非营利组织一样，奥克兰交响乐团也未能理解使命与战略之间的区别。这个错误扼杀了这个古老的交响乐团



By James A. Phills, Jr. [Fall 2004](https://ssir.org/issue/fall_2004)

作者：小詹姆斯·A·菲尔斯 2004 年秋



Twenty-five years ago, the Oakland Symphony was an exciting cultural phenomenon in the San Francisco Bay Area. Conducted by a charismatic young African- American, Calvin Simmons, the orchestra was surging in popularity among subscribers and music critics alike. Its showcase home was the gorgeously restored Paramount Theatre, an art deco gem listed in the National Register of Historic Places. The symphony had just won its first award for adventurous programming from the American Society of Composers, Authors, and Publishers, and would soon earn a second. The orchestra’s budget was growing, it was playing more concerts than ever, and it had ambitious plans to transform itself into a national-caliber ensemble capable of challenging the vaunted San Francisco Symphony for supremacy in the Bay Area classical music world.

25 年前，奥克兰交响乐团是旧金山湾区一个令人兴奋的文化现象。在魅力十足的年轻非裔美国人卡尔文·西蒙斯（Calvin Simmons）的指挥下，乐团在订阅者和乐评人中人气飙升。交响乐团的演出场所是经过修缮的派拉蒙剧院（Paramount Theatre），这是一座被列入《国家历史名胜古迹名录》的艺术珍品。交响乐团刚刚赢得了美国作曲家、作家和出版商协会颁发的第一个探索者奖项，很快又将赢得第二个奖项。交响乐团的预算不断增加，音乐会的演出场次也比以往任何时候都多，而且它还制定了雄心勃勃的计划，要将自己打造成一个国家级的乐团，能够在湾区古典音乐界向声名显赫的旧金山交响乐团发起挑战。



But the accolades and glitter masked serious financial distress. The Oakland Symphony had posted worsening deficits for years. Subscriptions and single-ticket sales were sagging. The management and board of directors had invaded a supposedly inviolable endowment for operating cash, and the Paramount was proving a costly drain on the orchestra’s treasury. In 1982, the symphony suffered a crushing psychological and emotional blow when its dazzling leader, Simmons, drowned in a canoeing accident at age 32.

但荣誉和光环掩盖了严重的财务困境。奥克兰交响乐团多年来赤字不断恶化。订阅费和单票销售额不断下滑。管理层和董事会挪用了本应不可侵犯的捐赠基金以获取运营现金，而派拉蒙剧院也被证明是对乐团财务的一种昂贵消耗。1982 年，交响乐团耀眼的领军人物西蒙斯在一次独木舟事故中溺水身亡，年仅 32 岁，交响乐团遭受了沉重的心理和精神打击。



Labor troubles soon sent the organization reeling. In 1985, musicians briefly went on strike. Management promptly canceled the upcoming season, and the players union accepted a controversial three-year contract offer by a margin of only one vote. But just eight months later, facing acute money problems, the orchestra’s general manager sought to reopen the contract in order to extract more concessions from players. The musicians immediately complained to the National Labor Relations Board. Worried that they might be personally liable for players’ salaries, the symphony’s directors filed for bankruptcy and, a few weeks later, liquidated the organization. After 53 years of delighting local audiences with classical and contemporary music from Mozart and Brahms to Penderecki and Milhaud, the Oakland Symphony was dead.<sup>[1]</sup>

劳资纠纷很快让交响乐团陷入困境。1985 年，音乐家们举行了短暂的罢工。管理层立即取消了即将到来的赛季，工会仅以一票之差接受了一份有争议的三年合同。但仅仅八个月后，面对严重的资金问题，乐团总经理又试图重新签订合同，以便从乐手那里获得更多的让步。乐手们立即向全国劳资关系委员会投诉。由于担心他们个人可能要为乐手的工资承担责任，交响乐团的董事们申请了破产，几周后，交响乐团进行了清算。奥克兰交响乐团 53 年来为当地观众演奏了从莫扎特、勃拉姆斯到潘德列茨基和米尔豪的古典和现代音乐，如今它已寿终正寝。



The orchestra’s collapse is an almost textbook example of the perils of conflating mission and strategy. Although this error is not usually fatal, it is one that nonprofit leaders and their boards commit with alarming frequency, and it can cause significant hardship. One of the most important lessons to be learned from the symphony’s demise is that mission and strategy are distinct concepts, and understanding and respecting their separate functions is crucial to the long-term success of every nonprofit organization.

奥克兰交响乐团的倒闭几乎是将使命与战略混为一谈的典型案例。虽然这种错误通常并不致命，但非营利组织的领导者及其董事会却经常犯这种错误，而且这种错误会给他们带来巨大的困难。从交响乐团的垮台中汲取的最重要的教训之一就是，使命和战略是截然不同的概念，理解并尊重它们各自的功能对于每一个非营利组织的长期成功都至关重要。



**The Function of Mission**

**使命的作用**



A mission that creates social, cultural, or intellectual value is one of the defining features of nonprofit organizations. Despite the importance of mission, however, there’s little agreement about what it really is, what it’s supposed to do, and how it should be evaluated. The term is often used to describe a nonprofit’s activities, purpose, aspirations, impact, and strategy, to name a few.

创造社会、文化或知识价值的使命是非营利组织的决定性特征之一。然而，尽管使命非常重要，人们对使命的真正含义、使命的作用以及使命的评估方式却知之甚少。这个词经常被用来描述非营利组织的活动、目的、愿望、影响和战略等等。



There are several things that mission is not. It’s not a strategy or strategic plan, although the terms mission and strategy are often used interchangeably. As we’ll see, strategy is concerned with markets and competition. It serves functions that are economic in nature and it must satisfy economic criteria in order to be considered effective. Mission can’t explain, predict, or ensure an organization’s financial viability, nor should it be expected to.

有几件事与使命无关。它不是战略或战略计划，尽管使命和战略这两个词经常被交替使用。我们将看到，战略与市场和竞争有关。它在本质上服务于经济功能，必须满足经济标准才能被认为是有效的。使命无法解释、预测或确保组织的财务可行性，也不应期望它能做到这一点。



So what *is* mission? It’s the expression of the essence of an organization and it provides the psychological and emotional logic that drives it. A compelling mission is especially important for nonprofits. It is the source of the passion and commitment that leads people to forgo higher-paying private-sector jobs and work in the nonprofit sector. It is why donors give their hard-earned dollars (besides the tax write-off, of course). It is often why clients or customers patronize a nonprofit. Mission triggers all this by defining the social value an organization creates. The key feature of social value – be it spiritual, moral, societal, aesthetic, intellectual, or environmental – is that it transcends economic value.

那么*使命* 是什么呢？它表达了一个组织的本质，提供了推动组织发展的心理和情感逻辑。对于非营利组织来说，令人信服的使命尤为重要。它是人们放弃私营部门的高薪工作而投身非营利部门的激情和承诺的源泉。这也是捐赠者捐出血汗钱的原因（当然，除了税务注销）。这通常也是客户或顾客光顾非营利组织的原因。使命通过定义一个组织所创造的社会价值来触发这一切。社会价值——无论是精神价值、道德价值、社会价值、美学价值、智力价值还是环境价值——的主要特征是它超越了经济价值。



Nonprofit leaders give a host of answers to the question of what they expect from their missions: to guide the organization’s activities; to inspire and motivate stakeholders and staff; to appeal to donors; to provide a basis for evaluating the organization; to explain the organization’s reasons for being; to place boundaries on the range of strategies that are acceptable. In order to satisfy all of these demands, we need a more complex and multifaceted notion of mission than is found in common usage.

非营利组织的领导者对他们期望从使命中得到什么这个问题给出了一系列答案：指导组织的活动；激励利益相关者和员工；吸引捐赠者；为评估组织提供依据；解释组织存在的理由；为可接受的战略范围划定界限。为了满足所有这些要求，我们需要一个比常见的使命概念更复杂、更多层面的使命概念。



Just such a notion emerged from a study conducted by Jim Collins and Jerry Porras, who examined the founding, evolution, and long-term performance of 18 “visionary” companies and a quasi control group of comparison firms.<sup>[2]</sup> The results showed that companies with a meaningful and deeply shared mission outperformed matched peers in cumulative stock returns by more than 6-to-1, and the market by 15-to-1, over 64 years. Collins and Porras’ book, “Built to Last: Successful Habits of Visionary Companies,” became a best seller, not only because it demonstrated the economic value of mission in the corporate world, but also because its message tapped into the intrinsic appeal of infusing even seemingly mundane businesses with meaning and significance.

吉姆·柯林斯（Jim Collins）和杰里·波拉斯（Jerry Porras）对 18 家 “有远见 ”的公司和一组准对照组公司的创立、发展和长期业绩进行了研究。研究结果表明，在 64 年的时间里，具有意义深远的共同使命的公司，其累计股票回报率比同行高出 6 倍以上，比市场高出 15 倍。柯林斯和波拉斯的著作《基业长青：有远见公司的成功习惯》成为畅销书，不仅因为它证明了使命在企业界的经济价值，还因为它所传达的信息挖掘出了为看似平凡的企业注入意义和重要性的内在吸引力。



**The Building Blocks of Mission**

**使命的基石**



Unfortunately, there are no comparable studies of the relationship between mission and performance in nonprofits. This is at least partly due to the elusive nature of performance in the nonprofit sector. However, Collins and Porras’ research remains important and relevant to nonprofits because of the clarity and precision it brings to the perennially fuzzy concept of mission. The authors break it down into four elements: core values, purpose, primary goals, and vision.

遗憾的是，目前还没有关于非营利组织使命与绩效之间关系的类似研究。这至少部分是由于非营利部门的绩效难以捉摸。然而，柯林斯和波拉斯的研究对非营利组织仍然具有重要的现实意义，因为它使使命这一长期模糊的概念变得清晰和准确。**作者将其细分为四个要素：核心价值观、目的、主要目标和愿景。**



Core values, Collins and Porras said, are the “essential and enduring tenets” of an organization, its “timeless guiding principles that require no external justification.” They are abstract concepts considered inherently good, meaningful, or valuable, such as freedom, justice, and equality. People care deeply about these values and would sooner shut their organization down than violate them.

柯林斯和波拉斯说，核心价值观是一个组织的 “基本和持久的信条”，是其 “永恒的指导原则，不需要任何外部理由”。它们是一些抽象的概念，被认为是固有的好、有意义或有价值的，如自由、正义和平等。人们非常在乎这些价值观，宁可让组织关门大吉，也不愿违背这些价值观。



While core values reflect unalterable principles, an organization’s reason for being is defined by its purpose. Purpose is also the closest thing to what most organizations typically refer to as their “mission.” Given their role in society, one would expect nonprofits to have little difficulty articulating their purpose, but they often do. For instance, purpose might be framed as “We provide shelter for the homeless” or “We provide scholarships to allow disadvantaged students the chance to go to college.” But to galvanize and inspire, purpose must go beyond such flat statements. It also has to answer the question of why an organization does what it does. It has to convey the significance of the organization’s activities or programs. Why does a nonprofit provide scholarships to disadvantaged students? Because otherwise they might not be able to attend college simply for financial reasons, and that would be unjust. So scholarships are actually a way of advancing social justice, and that’s the real purpose.

核心价值观反映的是不可改变的原则，而一个组织存在的理由则是由其目标决定的。目标也是最接近大多数组织通常所说的 “使命 ”的东西。考虑到非营利组织在社会中的作用，人们会认为非营利组织在阐述其目标时不会有太大困难，但事实往往如此。例如，目的可能被表述为 “我们为无家可归者提供庇护所 ”或 “我们提供奖学金，让贫困学生有机会上大学”。但要激发和鼓舞人心，目标必须超越这些平铺直叙的表述。它还必须回答一个问题，即一个组织为什么要做它所做的事情。它必须传达出组织活动或计划的意义。为什么一个非营利组织要为贫困学生提供奖学金？因为否则他们可能仅仅因为经济原因而上不了大学，这是不公平的。因此，奖学金实际上是一种促进社会公正的方式，这才是真正的目的。



Consider the example of Habitat for Humanity, a nonprofit Christian organization that provides housing to the needy. Habitat for Humanity has a bold, sweeping purpose: “eliminating substandard housing and homelessness worldwide and making adequate, affordable shelter a matter of conscience and action.” Habitat’s core values define how it pursues this purpose and include a deep belief in the “necessity of putting faith into action” and “bridging theological differences” through an open and inclusive approach to partnering. <sup>[3]</sup> The nonprofit Environmental Defense has a similarly ambitious purpose: “protecting the environmental rights of all people, including future generations” by creating “innovative, equitable, and cost-effective solutions to society’s most urgent environmental problems.” Its core values include pursuing this purpose only in ways that are “nonpartisan,” “fair,” and “guided by science.”<sup>[4]</sup>

以 “仁人家园”（Habitat for Humanity）为例，这是一个非营利的基督教组织，专门为有需要的人提供住房。仁人家园有一个大胆而全面的目标：“在全球范围内消除不符合标准的住房和无家可归现象，使提供适当的、可负担得起的住房成为人们的良知和行动”。仁人家园的核心价值观确定了它如何实现这一目标，包括坚信 “必须将信仰付诸行动”，以及通过开放和包容的合作方式 “弥合神学分歧”。非营利组织 “环境保护 ”也有类似的远大目标：通过 “为社会最紧迫的环境问题制定创新、公平和具有成本效益的解决方案”，“保护包括子孙后代在内的所有人的环境权利”。该组织的核心价值观包括：只以 “无党派”、“公平 ”和 “以科学为指导 ”的方式追求这一目标。



Another key component of mission is the organization’s primary goal. Not just any old goal, but a soaring, difficult- to-achieve goal; one that might take years to accomplish, such as President Kennedy’s call to put a man on the moon by the end of the 1960s. Such a far-reaching objective provides focus, attention, and structure to the activities of an organization’s members. Its tangible and challenging nature, like the finish line in a race, is also an immediate source of motivation, and once attained, provides a deep sense of accomplishment.

使命的另一个关键组成部分是组织的首要目标。这不是一个普通的目标，而是一个高耸入云、难以实现的目标；一个可能需要数年才能完成的目标，例如肯尼迪总统呼吁在 20 世纪 60 年代末将人类送上月球。这样一个意义深远的目标为组织成员的活动提供了重点、关注和结构。它的有形性和挑战性，就像赛跑中的终点线一样，也是一种直接的动力源泉，一旦实现，就会给人一种深刻的成就感。



The last constituent of mission is vision, which Collins and Porras defined as the “vibrant, and engaging, and specific description of what it will be like to achieve the [primary goal].” Vision is rich, textured, and vivid; it should be where the poetry and music are heard. The test of a good vision is whether it makes people see, taste, and feel the future.

使命的最后一个组成部分是愿景，柯林斯和波拉斯将其定义为 “对实现[首要目标]的具体描述，充满活力，引人入胜”。愿景是丰富的、有质感的、生动的；它应该是诗歌和音乐的发源地。检验一个好愿景的标准是，它是否能让人们看到、品味和感受到未来。



Remember the classic Apple Computer television ad that introduced the Macintosh in 1984? It pictured an athletic young woman hurling a sledgehammer at a huge screen from which a despotic-looking authority figure preaches obedience to scores of mesmerized, shaven-headed men in prison garb. The hammer shatters the screen, breaking the despot’s hypnotic control over his audience. A voice-over intones: “On January 24, Apple Computer will introduce Macintosh. And you will see why 1984 won’t be like ‘1984.’” The commercial was Apple’s clever way of invoking the dark, fearful imagery of mass conformity from George Orwell’s famous novel to launch its metaphorical assault on archrival IBM. The quintessential icon of corporate power and conformity at the time, IBM had overtaken Apple’s early lead in the personal computer business. But Apple co- founder Steve Jobs battled back with a vision of the future in which everyday people were empowered by PCs rather than enslaved by the efficient but dreary technocracy of IT professionals – a world dominated, Big Brother-style, by IBM. (IBM’s nickname is, perhaps not coincidentally, Big Blue.)

还记得 1984 年苹果电脑公司推出 Macintosh 的经典电视广告吗？广告中，一位身手矫健的年轻女子向一个巨大的屏幕掷出一把大锤，屏幕上是一位面目专横的权威人士，他向数十名身着囚服、剃着光头、神情专注的男子宣扬服从。大锤砸碎了屏幕，打破了专制者对观众的催眠控制。画外音响起 “1 月 24 日，苹果电脑公司将推出 Macintosh。你将看到为什么《1984》不会像《1984》一样"。这则广告是苹果公司巧妙地从乔治·奥威尔（George Orwell）的名著《1984》中唤起黑暗、恐惧的 “大众服从”（mass conformity）意象，并以此为隐喻，向宿敌 IBM 发起攻击。在当时，IBM 是企业权力和墨守成规的典型代表，在个人电脑业务上超越了苹果的早期领先地位。但是，苹果公司的共同创始人史蒂夫·乔布斯（Steve Jobs）奋起反击，他提出了一个未来愿景：在这个世界里，普通人将被个人电脑赋予力量，而不是被高效但沉闷的 IT 专业技术官僚所奴役——在这个世界里，IBM 是老大哥式的霸主。(IBM 的昵称是 “大蓝”，也许这并非巧合）。



**Mission: Impenetrable – the Oakland Symphony**

**任务：坚不可摧——奥克兰交响乐团 坚不可摧——奥克兰交响乐团**



A careful review of the record yields little indication of what the Oakland Symphony regarded as its core values and purpose. Indeed, San Francisco consultant Melanie Beene concluded in a detailed study of the orchestra’s demise that “a major stumbling block for the Oakland Symphony was its inability to define its mission clearly.” Nevertheless, by looking closely at the orchestra’s artistic and operational choices one might infer that its core values included “innovative programming,” “community involvement,” and “diversity.” The symphony’s purpose appears to have been contributing to Oakland’s sense of civic pride and identity by providing access to nationally recognized live symphonic music.

仔细查阅相关记录，几乎找不到奥克兰交响乐团认为自己的核心价值和目标是什么。事实上，旧金山顾问梅兰妮·比尼（Melanie Beene）在对该交响乐团的消亡进行详细研究后得出结论，“奥克兰交响乐团的主要绊脚石是无法明确界定其使命”。尽管如此，通过仔细研究该乐团在艺术和运营方面的选择，我们可以推断出其核心价值包括 “创新的节目编排”、“社区参与 ”和 “多样性”。交响乐团的目标似乎是通过提供国家认可的现场交响音乐，增强奥克兰的公民自豪感和身份认同感。



From 1959 to 1971, the symphony thrived under music director Gerhard Samuel. Its primary goals appear to have been artistic growth and innovation. An award-winning youth orchestra was created, the season expanded from eight to 24 concerts, and the orchestra won major grants from the Ford Foundation. It is important to note that this success occurred during a period when the American Symphony Orchestra League characterized the symphony as a “regional” ensemble, based on its budget size.

1959 年至 1971 年，交响乐团在音乐总监格哈德·塞缪尔（Gerhard Samuel）的领导下蓬勃发展。其主要目标似乎是艺术发展和创新。乐团成立了一个屡获殊荣的青年交响乐团，演出季从 8 场音乐会增加到 24 场，并赢得了福特基金会（Ford Foundation）的重大资助。值得注意的是，这一成功发生在美国交响乐团联盟根据其预算规模将交响乐团定性为 “地区性 ”乐团的时期。



But a new and very explicit primary goal emerged in 1974 when music director Harold Farberman exhorted the organization “to achieve major orchestra classification.” The vision, though not explicit, appeared to be growing to rival or even outshine the San Francisco Symphony, with its larger subscriber base, deeper pockets, and intoxicating social cachet.

但在 1974 年，当音乐总监哈罗德·法伯曼（Harold Farberman）劝说交响乐团 “要达到大型交响乐团的级别 ”时，一个新的、非常明确的首要目标出现了。这个愿景虽然并不明确，但似乎正朝着与旧金山交响乐团相抗衡甚至超越的方向发展，因为旧金山交响乐团拥有更大的订户群体、更雄厚的资金以及令人陶醉的社会声望。



The problem with this goal and vision is twofold. First, they are framed in terms of objectives that are primarily financial rather than artistic. In fact, noted Beene, the justification for the goal was almost entirely financial: to be “eligible to receive higher grant amounts from the National Endowment for the Arts.” This rationale contained a trickle-down logic: Larger grants would allow the symphony to grow its roster of musicians and pay them more for their services. But such an emphasis subjugated the orchestra’s basic artistic purpose. Although symphony managers hoped to attract better musicians, there seemed to be no compelling end to justify its growth. The growth goal became enshrined in subsequent long-range planning documents. In her report, Beene observed: “The goal of becoming a ‘major’ orchestra for the sake of the designation alone overrode other considerations. … The 1980-1984 plan does not present the board/management rationale for becoming a major orchestra … nor make clear how [it] furthers the symphony’s mission.”

这一目标和愿景存在两方面的问题。首先，它们的目标主要是经济目标，而不是艺术目标。事实上，比尼指出，这一目标的理由几乎完全是经济方面的：“有资格从国家艺术基金会获得更高的资助金额”。这一理由包含了一种涓滴逻辑（trickle-down logic）： 更多的资助将使交响乐团能够扩大音乐家名册，并为他们的服务支付更多的报酬。但是，这种强调却削弱了交响乐团的基本艺术目的。尽管交响乐团的管理者希望吸引更优秀的音乐家，但似乎并没有令人信服的目的来证明其发展的合理性。在随后的长期规划文件中，增长目标被写入了文件。比尼在她的报告中指出 “成为'大型'交响乐团的目标仅仅是为了这个称号，它压倒了其他考虑因素。......1980-1984 年的计划没有提出董事会/管理层成为大型交响乐团的理由......也没有明确说明[它]如何推进交响乐团的使命"。



The second problem with becoming a major orchestra was the dubiousness of ever achieving that goal and vision. Attainability is a critical test of a primary goal because its power stems from its capacity to focus, mobilize, and coordinate an organization’s efforts. Goals that bear little relationship to reality lose this source of practical value.

成为大型交响乐团的第二个问题是能否实现这一目标和愿景。可实现性是对主要目标的关键考验，因为目标的力量来自于其聚焦、动员和协调组织努力的能力。与现实关系不大的目标就失去了实用价值。



The issue of attainability also highlights the way goals function as the link between mission and strategy. Especially when goals entail growth or expansion of resources in a competitive environment, strategy provides a basis for evaluating whether these goals are challenging but realistic, as well as a concrete plan for achieving them.

可实现性问题还凸显了目标作为使命与战略之间纽带的作用。特别是当目标需要在竞争环境中增长或扩大资源时，战略就为评估这些目标是否具有挑战性但又切合实际提供了依据，并为实现这些目标提供了具体计划。



Although the preceding is based on an analysis of an inferred mission, the symphony eventually articulated the following mission in its 1980-84 long-range plan: “to furnish the citizens of the greater Bay Area and beyond with performances of the highest artistic quality, to serve expanded audiences through a vigorous outreach program, to offer education-related programs to young people at all school levels, to provide inspiration to young musicians who aspire to a musical career, and to provide programs that will involve the area’s diverse ethnic communities in the activities of the orchestra.”

尽管前文是基于对推断使命的分析，但交响乐团最终还是在其 1980-84 年的长期计划中明确提出了以下使命： “为大湾区及其他地区的市民提供最高艺术质量的演出，通过积极的推广计划为更多的观众服务，为各级学校的青少年提供与教育相关的计划，为有志于音乐事业的年轻音乐家提供灵感，并提供让该地区不同种族社区参与乐团活动的计划"。



But even this belated mission statement provides little guidance and arouses little passion since it’s largely a description of what the symphony does. It doesn’t convey what impact or contribution the orchestra’s activities will make. One can’t help wondering why its audiences must be expanded and what imperative drives its reach beyond the Bay Area.

但即使是这份迟来的使命宣言，也没有提供多少指导，也没有激起多少热情，因为它主要是在描述交响乐团的工作。它并没有传达乐团的活动会产生什么影响或做出什么贡献。人们不禁要问，为什么交响乐团必须扩大听众范围，又是什么在驱使交响乐团向湾区以外的地区拓展。



**The Function of Strategy**

**战略的功能**



Like many nonprofits, the Oakland Symphony was falling into the trap of conflating its mission and a viable economic strategy. In the corporate world, strategy is recognized as the body of knowledge that provides a basis for explaining and influencing organizational performance – specifically, profitability. Thus its relevance to nonprofits is not immediately clear since the main objective of nonprofits is to produce a social good, not a profit. Moreover, strategy involves devising better ways to compete, and many nonprofit leaders dislike the idea of competition, preferring to emphasize their cooperative stance toward other providers in their industry. Nonetheless, strategy matters to nonprofits for two important reasons.

与许多非营利组织一样，奥克兰交响乐团也陷入了将自身使命与可行的经济战略混为一谈的陷阱。在企业界，战略被认为是为解释和影响组织绩效（特别是盈利能力）提供依据的知识体系。因此，它与非营利组织的相关性并不明显，因为非营利组织的主要目标是创造社会效益，而不是利润。此外，战略涉及设计更好的竞争方式，而许多非营利组织的领导者不喜欢竞争的概念，他们更愿意强调与本行业其他提供者的合作立场。尽管如此，战略对于非营利组织来说还是很重要的，原因有两个。



First, no organization will survive if it’s not economically viable. Even nonprofits need money for salaries, rent, utilities, and other costs of running their programs. Ask almost any nonprofit leader what their biggest problem is and they’ll respond: “Securing adequate funding.”

首先，如果经济上不可行，任何组织都无法生存。即使是非营利组织也需要资金来支付工资、房租、水电费和其他项目运营成本。几乎所有非营利组织的领导者都会回答：“确保充足的资金”： “确保充足的资金"。



Second, competition is a fact of life for nonprofits just as it is for for-profit businesses. It’s a function of the scarcity of resources – especially money. To the extent that a nonprofit doesn’t have all the resources it needs to carry out its mission, it’s in competition with similar organizations for those resources.

其次，对非营利组织来说，竞争是一个不争的事实，就像对营利性企业一样。这是资源稀缺——尤其是资金稀缺——的结果。如果非营利组织没有履行使命所需的全部资源，它就会与同类组织竞争这些资源。



It may be more useful (and philosophically more palatable) for nonprofit executives to think in terms of achieving prosperity – having the resources necessary to pursue their mission as freely as possible. Prosperity gives a nonprofit what Stanford Graduate School of Business professor Robert Burgelman refers to as “control over its destiny.”<sup>[5]</sup> Conversely, a nonprofit that’s not prosperous has less control over its destiny because the desperate, never-ending pursuit of money limits its ability to be selective in accepting funding for programs or activities consistent with its mission.

对于非营利组织的管理者来说，从实现繁荣的角度来思考问题可能更有用（在哲学上也更容易接受）——拥有必要的资源来尽可能自由地履行使命。繁荣使非营利组织获得了斯坦福大学商学院教授罗伯特·伯格曼（Robert Burgelman）所说的 “对自己命运的控制权 ” 。相反，一个不繁荣的非营利组织对自己命运的控制权就比较小，因为对金钱的不择手段、永无止境的追求限制了它有选择性地接受与自己使命相一致的项目或活动资金的能力。



Strategy must be based on an accurate understanding of the dynamics of competition within a given sphere of activity. It can’t drive performance until it has been translated into concrete choices and actions. Otherwise, it’s little more than an abstract slogan or wishful thinking.

战略必须建立在对特定活动领域竞争态势的准确理解之上。只有将战略转化为具体的选择和行动，才能推动绩效。否则，它只不过是一个抽象的口号或一厢情愿的想法。



An effective strategy contains three elements: scope, competitive advantage, and logic.6 And as we’ll see, the Oakland Symphony was lacking in all three.

有效的战略包含三个要素：范围、竞争优势和逻辑<sup>[6]</sup> 。



Scope describes the “playing field” on which an organization competes: who its customers are, where it operates geographically, what products or services it offers, and what activities it performs.

范围描述了一个组织参与竞争的 “竞技场”：它的客户是谁，它在哪里开展业务，它提供什么样的产品或服务，以及它开展什么样的活动。



Competitive advantage specifies the unique sources of value that an organization offers its customers, as defined by its scope. It explains why these customers (who include both clients and donors) will prefer its products or services over those offered by other organizations. Competitive advantage can stem from any of a wide range of attributes that customers value. These can be tangible, such as quality (reliability or durability) and performance (speed), or intangible like brand, prestige, or reputation.

竞争优势明确了一个组织根据其业务范围为客户提供的独特价值来源。它解释了为什么这些客户（包括客户和捐赠者）更喜欢其产品或服务，而不是其他组织提供的产品或服务。竞争优势可以来源于客户所看重的各种属性中的任何一种。这些属性可以是有形的，如质量（可靠性或耐用性）和性能（速度），也可以是无形的，如品牌、声望或声誉。



The final element of strategy is logic. It answers two key questions: Why will the strategy work? And how will the organization be designed and managed so as to create and sustain its competitive advantage? Logic makes explicit the causal assumptions about the relationships between the organization’s choices and behavior, the consequences of this behavior, and the interaction between these consequences and the environment in which the organization operates.

战略的最后一个要素是逻辑。它回答了两个关键问题： 为什么战略会奏效？如何设计和管理组织，以创造并保持竞争优势？逻辑明确了组织的选择和行为、这种行为的后果以及这些后果与组织所处环境之间互动关系的因果假设。



**The Symphony Loses Its Way**

**交响乐团迷失方向**



Although it was not explicit, the Oakland Symphony did appear to have had a workable strategy in the 1960s and early ’70s. Its scope clearly included typical fine arts patrons – generally older, affluent, well educated, and white. It operated in the greater Oakland area and offered contemporary music of international stature.

尽管奥克兰交响乐团的战略并不明确，但在 20 世纪 60 年代和 70 年代初，它似乎确实有一套可行的战略。它的听众范围显然包括典型的高雅艺术赞助人——一般都是年长、富裕、受过良好教育的白人。交响乐团在大奥克兰地区运作，提供具有国际地位的现代音乐。



The symphony’s competitive advantage derived from its uniquely adventurous, well-played, original programming, combined with its convenient location for music lovers living in or near Oakland. The strategy’s logic worked because there was a clear match between the unique value created by the symphony and the needs of its target audiences.

交响乐团的竞争优势来自于其独特的冒险精神、精湛的演奏、原创的节目编排，再加上其便利的地理位置，为居住在奥克兰或奥克兰附近的音乐爱好者提供了便利。该战略的逻辑之所以行之有效，是因为交响乐团创造的独特价值与其目标受众的需求之间存在着明确的匹配关系。



Consistent with its strategy, the symphony’s artistic choices and investments also reflected its contemporary focus. In the 1969-70 season, music director Gerhard Samuel staged five West Coast premieres and three world premieres, including one commissioned work. He attracted national attention by introducing local music lovers to artists such as Penderecki, Lutoslawski, Berio, Varese, and Milhaud.

与战略相一致的是，交响乐团的艺术选择和投资也反映了其对当代的关注。在 1969-70 乐季中，音乐总监格哈德·塞缪尔（Gerhard Samuel）上演了五场西海岸首演和三场世界首演，其中包括一部委约作品。他向当地音乐爱好者介绍了潘德列茨基、卢托斯拉夫斯基、贝里奥、瓦雷斯和米尔豪德等艺术家，吸引了全国的关注。



Oakland’s competitive advantage arguably extended into the territory of its western rival. In a 1986 article in San Francisco Focus magazine, the critic Allan Ulrich wrote: “In the 1960s, the [Oakland] orchestra filled a unique niche in Bay Area cultural life. While the San Francisco Symphony suffered through the lackluster regime of Enrique Jorda in the late 1950s, and then the reconstructive surgery of Josef Krips … Gerhard Samuel was igniting the East Bay sky with ruggedly individualistic programming. … The Oakland Symphony was a very special phenomenon.”

可以说，奥克兰的竞争优势已经延伸到了其西部竞争对手的领地。评论家艾伦·乌尔里希（Allan Ulrich） 1986 年在《旧金山焦点》杂志上发表的一篇文章中写道："20 世纪 60 年代，（奥克兰）交响乐团在湾区文化生活中占据了一个独特的位置。20 世纪 50 年代末，旧金山交响乐团经历了恩里克·若尔达（Enrique Jorda）乏善可陈的统治，之后又经历了约瑟夫·克里普斯（Josef Krips）的重建手术......而格哈德·塞缪尔则以粗犷的个性节目点燃了东湾的天空。......奥克兰交响乐团是一个非常特别的现象"。



Given the environment at the time, this strategy worked relatively well. But as the symphony’s desire to grow intensified in the mid-’70s, its scope underwent a dramatic shift. While the target customer remained the same, the orchestra enlarged its geographic market to encompass the entire East Bay region. It also made a crucial decision to return to a more traditional symphonic repertoire. But by eliminating more modern and innovative programming, the switch eroded Oakland’s competitive advantage and put it in more direct competition with the San Francisco Symphony. Oakland’s artistic quality didn’t exceed that of its cross-bay rival. And it wasn’t really that difficult to drive past downtown Oakland and over the Bay Bridge into more elegant San Francisco.

考虑到当时的环境，这一策略相对有效。但到了 70 年代中期，随着交响乐团发展欲望的增强，其业务范围也发生了巨大变化。虽然目标客户没有改变，但交响乐团扩大了地理市场，将整个东湾地区都包括在内。此外，乐团还做出了一个重要决定，即回归更为传统的交响乐曲目。但是，由于取消了更为现代和创新的节目，这一转变削弱了奥克兰的竞争优势，使其与旧金山交响乐团的竞争更为直接。奥克兰交响乐团的艺术质量并没有超过其跨湾竞争对手。而且，驱车经过奥克兰市中心，越过海湾大桥，进入更优雅的旧金山，其实并不困难。



A growing perception that downtown Oakland was unsafe eroded the symphony’s competitive advantage. And although the move to the Paramount Theatre may have provided some novelty in the first year or so, it quickly became a giant albatross, consuming resources so fast that it became a major cost disadvantage.

越来越多的人认为奥克兰市中心不安全，这削弱了交响乐团的竞争优势。虽然搬迁到派拉蒙剧院在第一年左右可能会带来一些新鲜感，但它很快就变成了一个巨大的包袱，迅速消耗了资源，成为成本上的一大劣势。



The orchestra’s ambitious expansion also inflated its costs. During the 1973-74 season, for example, the symphony offered 35 subscription concerts, pops, galas, and other full-orchestra performances. By the 1986-87 season, the projected number of shows had more than doubled to 73. As the volume of performances increased, so did the number of musicians and their compensation. The orchestra’s payroll almost quadrupled in less than a decade, rising from $217,547 in 1976 to $791,250 in 1985.

乐团雄心勃勃的扩张也增加了成本。以 1973-74 乐季为例，交响乐团共举办了 35 场订票音乐会、流行音乐会、联欢晚会和其他全乐团演出。到 1986-87 乐季，预计演出场次增加了一倍多，达到 73 场。随着演出数量的增加，音乐家的人数和报酬也随之增加。在不到十年的时间里，乐团的工资几乎翻了两番，从 1976 年的 217,547 美元增至 1985 年的 791,250 美元。



The symphony’s increase in capacity seems to make sense in light of its desire to grow. But the “strategy” implicit in its choices during this period appears to be the “field of dreams” approach: “If we build it, they will come.” Indeed, one symphony executive director said as much when he declared that “supply creates demand.” Such faulty reasoning made it impossible to articulate a compelling logic about how and why any strategy would work.

从交响乐团发展壮大的愿望来看，乐团增加演出能力似乎是合情合理的。但是，这一时期交响乐团的选择所隐含的 “战略 ”似乎是 “梦想领域 ”的方法： “如果我们建造它，他们就会来"。事实上，一位交响乐团执行总监在宣称 “供应创造需求 ”时也是这么说的。这种错误的推理使得我们无法就任何战略如何以及为什么会奏效阐述出令人信服的逻辑。



Unfortunately, Oakland’s demographics were turning against the symphony as it was laying on more and more mainstream classical music. By 1980, 56 percent of the city’s population was racial minorities, and median income was declining. Moreover, the symphony was losing ground dramatically in its core geographic markets: the city of Oakland and the adjacent but wealthier communities of Piedmont and Alameda. In 1973, 52 percent of symphony subscribers lived in one of those three communities; by 1985, the percentage had plunged to 23. Considering the symphony’s lack of any clear and sustainable competitive advantage, combined with Oakland’s changing social and economic structure, the growth strategy seems hopelessly and tragically naive.

不幸的是，随着交响乐团开始演奏越来越多的主流古典音乐，奥克兰的人口结构也发生了逆转。到 1980 年，该市 56% 的人口为少数民族，收入中位数不断下降。此外，交响乐团在其核心地域市场的地位急剧下降：奥克兰市以及毗邻的皮德蒙特和阿拉米达等富裕社区。1973 年，52% 的交响乐订户居住在这三个社区之一；到 1985 年，这一比例骤降至 23%。考虑到交响乐团缺乏明显的、可持续的竞争优势，再加上奥克兰不断变化的社会和经济结构，交响乐团的发展战略显得天真得令人绝望和悲哀。



**Strategic Planning That Doesn’t Produce a Strategy**

**没有产生战略的战略规划**



Clearly, both a fuzzy mission and a flawed strategy contributed to the failure of the Oakland Symphony. Those looking for the antecedents of these causes will no doubt point to the role of leadership and governance as well. But the question remains of why a clear and compelling mission was never articulated, and how a robust strategy was transformed into a disastrous one. The answer is that the symphony made the common error of confusing the psychological and emotional logic of mission with the economic logic of strategy.

显然，奥克兰交响乐团的失败是使命模糊和战略失误造成的。那些寻找这些原因的人无疑也会指出领导和管理的作用。但问题是，为什么从来没有明确而令人信服的使命，一个强有力的战略又是如何转变为一个灾难性的战略的。答案是，交响乐团犯了一个常见的错误，即混淆了使命的心理和情感逻辑与战略的经济逻辑。



The function of mission is to guide and inspire; to energize and give meaning; and to define a nonprofit and what it stands for. The function of strategy is to evaluate and enhance an organization’s ability to secure the financial resources it needs in order to survive and even to thrive. Mission, no matter how clear, compelling, or poetic, won’t ensure economic vitality. That is the job of strategy. The elements of mission (core values, purpose, primary goals, and vision) can support or reinforce strategy but they can’t substitute for it. Similarly, strategy, no matter how elegant or robust, won’t evoke the passion, devotion, and commitment of a nonprofit’s staff, volunteers, donors, or patrons.

使命的功能是引导和激励；激发活力和赋予意义；定义非营利组织和它所代表的意义。战略的功能是评估和提高组织的能力，以确保其获得生存甚至发展所需的财政资源。使命无论多么明确、引人注目或富有诗意，都无法确保经济活力。这是战略的职责所在。使命的要素（核心价值观、目标、主要目标和愿景）可以支持或加强战略，但不能取代战略。同样，无论战略多么优雅或有力，都无法唤起非营利组织的员工、志愿者、捐赠者或赞助人的热情、奉献和承诺。



The Oakland Symphony engaged in strategic planning exercises and produced a series of long-range plans.<sup>[7]</sup>  None of these, however, contained anything resembling a clear strategy. Although the orchestra’s decisions were guided by a host of strategic assumptions, they were implicit. And since symphony managers lacked a framework for thinking about strategy, it was impossible to subject their economic logic to critical evaluation.

奥克兰交响乐团参与了战略规划工作，并制定了一系列长期计划 。尽管交响乐团的决策以一系列战略假设为指导，但这些假设都是隐含的。由于交响乐团管理者缺乏战略思考框架，因此无法对其经济逻辑进行批判性评估。



While the plans laid out a clear primary goal (that of becoming large enough to attain “major symphony” status), no goal is attainable if the organization lacks a viable strategy sustainable over the long run. Given the competition the Oakland Symphony faced as it tried to secure both earned and contributed income, a strategy would have provided a framework for assessing how attainable the orchestra’s goals were – particularly if they implied the need for additional financial resources. A strategy also would have provided a logic for designing and evaluating a plan of action to achieve the symphony’s goals. But its board and management conflated mission and strategy, acting as if the significance of their goals and vision would generate the revenue these aspirations required. In other words, they expected mission to fulfill the function of strategy. They assumed that because they cared deeply about growth and becoming a “major” orchestra, that goal would be achieved. But it wasn’t, since mission can’t do the work of strategy. And eventually the symphony’s ever-expanding deficits caught up with it and it collapsed.

虽然这些计划制定了明确的主要目标（即成为足以获得 “大型交响乐团 ”地位的大型乐团），但如果乐团缺乏可长期持续发展的可行战略，任何目标都无法实现。考虑到奥克兰交响乐团在努力确保收入和捐助收入时所面临的竞争，战略本可以为评估乐团目标的可实现程度提供一个框架——尤其是当这些目标意味着需要额外的财政资源时。战略还可以为设计和评估实现交响乐团目标的行动计划提供逻辑。但是，交响乐团的董事会和管理层却将使命和战略混为一谈，似乎他们的目标和愿景的重要性就能带来这些愿望所需的收入。换句话说，他们期望使命能够实现战略的功能。他们假定，由于他们非常关心发展和成为 “主要 ”交响乐团，这个目标就会实现。但事实并非如此，因为使命无法完成战略的工作。最终，交响乐团不断扩大的赤字使其不堪重负，轰然倒塌。



**A New Orchestra Is Born**

**新乐团诞生**



Less than two years after the demise of the Oakland Symphony, a new ensemble rose from its ashes: the much leaner – and in many ways more competitive – Oakland East Bay Symphony.

奥克兰交响乐团解散后不到两年，一个新的乐团从废墟中崛起：奥克兰东湾交响乐团（Oakland East Bay Symphony, OEBS）更加精干，在许多方面更具竞争力。



Founded by the defunct orchestra’s musicians and volunteers, OEBS has a clear and compelling mission: “to make classical music accessible, particularly to those individuals in the community who might otherwise never hear live symphonic music. … To serve as a community resource, offering education, performances, and outreach to schools and the community, introducing new audiences of adults and children to symphonic music; to help ensure the future of symphonic music through the commission and performance of works by contemporary American composers; to provide leadership in the artistic community by fostering unity, collaboration, and co-creation among Oakland and East Bay arts organizations.”<sup>[8]</sup>

OEBS 由已解散的交响乐团的音乐家和志愿者创建，其使命明确而令人信服： “让古典音乐走进千家万户，尤其是让社区中那些可能永远也听不到现场交响乐的人。......作为社区资源，向学校和社区提供教育、演出和外联活动，向新的成人和儿童听众介绍交响乐；通过委托和演出当代美国作曲家的作品，帮助确保交响乐的未来；通过促进奥克兰和东湾艺术组织之间的团结、合作和共同创造，在艺术界发挥领导作用"。



This statement, which emphasizes purpose and vision, echoes its predecessor in several ways, including commitments to accessibility, diversity, community involvement, and serving the needs of a multiethnic city. But the OEBS places significantly more emphasis on music education and outreach, which represent 35 percent of its budget. Moreover, OEBS is clearly trying to reach out to new audiences with its adventurous and eclectic programming, including concerts with artists like DJ Spooky and Omar Sosa, an Afro-Cuban jazz pianist and composer.

这份强调目标和愿景的声明在多个方面与其前身相呼应，包括对无障碍环境、多样性、社区参与以及满足多民族城市需求的承诺。但 OEBS 更加重视音乐教育和推广，这占其预算的 35%。此外，OEBS 还通过其冒险和不拘一格的节目编排，包括与 DJ Spooky（保罗·丹尼斯·米勒） 和非洲裔古巴爵士钢琴家兼作曲家奥马尔·索萨（Omar Sosa）等艺术家的音乐会，努力向新观众进行宣传。



Similarly, OEBS has a distinct and robust strategy that also reflects a return to the past – not the ill-fated expansion of the late ’70s and ’80s, but the critically acclaimed period of Gerhard Samuel’s direction of the former Oakland Symphony. The new organization’s scope is clearly centered on contemporary music, innovative programming, and a broad spectrum of potential audiences (in terms of age, income, and ethnicity) in Oakland and the East Bay. Its competitive advantage stems from its extensive involvement and close ties to the community, and the ability that this gives the orchestra to develop and present work that resonates socially, politically, and aesthetically with its target listeners.

同样，OEBS 也制定了独特而有力的战略，体现了对过去的回归——不是 70 年代末和 80 年代命运多舛的扩张，而是格哈德·塞缪尔执掌前奥克兰交响乐团期间广受好评的时期。新交响乐团的业务范围明确以当代音乐、创新节目以及奥克兰和东湾地区广泛的潜在观众（年龄、收入和种族）为中心。其竞争优势源于其广泛的参与和与社区的密切联系，以及这赋予乐团开发和呈现在社会、政治和美学上与目标听众产生共鸣的作品的能力。



This unique ability is central to the logic: to build a reputation for integrating artistic excellence (within a clearly defined niche) with community service and ultimately to establish its legitimacy with East Bay audiences, ensuring their loyal patronage. Many of the OEBS’ artistic choices and resource allocations that support the mission also support this strategy. The new orchestra also has eschewed the notion that being big matters in its own right and has proudly embraced its role as a regional orchestra. It has matched its size to the demand in its niche. Even in 2003, OEBS’ $1.5 million budget was half the size of its predecessor’s projected budget in 1985. Consistent with its strength as a community-oriented organization, OEBS invests in col- laborations with other local arts and civic institutions, such as the Oaktown Jazz Workshop and the Oakland Ballet. In 2001, OEBS presented a special concert tribute to Gordon Parks in collaboration with the Oakland Museum on the eve of the opening of a national touring exhibit of the great African-American photographer’s work. In 2002, the symphony joined with Jack London Square merchants to put on a free Fourth of July pops concert, complete with fireworks.

这种独特的能力是乐团的核心逻辑：在明确界定的利基范围内建立艺术卓越性与社区服务相结合的声誉，并最终在东湾观众中建立其合法性，确保他们的忠实赞助。OEBS 的许多艺术选择和资源分配都支持这一使命，也支持这一战略。新乐团还摒弃了 “规模大 ”本身就很重要的观念，而是自豪地接受了自己作为地区性乐团的角色。该乐团的规模符合其特定领域的需求。即使在 2003 年，OEBS 的 150 万美元预算也只是其前身 1985 年预计预算的一半。作为一个以社区为导向的组织，OEBS 投资于与当地其他艺术和民间机构的合作，如奥克敦爵士乐工作坊（Oaktown Jazz Workshop）和奥克兰芭蕾舞团（Oakland Ballet）。2001 年，在这位伟大的非裔美国摄影师的作品全国巡回展开幕前夕，OEBS 与奥克兰博物馆合作举办了一场向戈登·帕克斯（Gordon Parks ）致敬的特别音乐会。2002 年，交响乐团与杰克伦敦广场（Jack London Square）的商家合作，举办了一场免费的国庆节流行音乐会，并燃放了烟花。



Since the OEBS has a clear, compelling mission (including realistic aspirations) and a well-reasoned strategy, it is not surprising that it enjoys a measure of financial prosperity and has received critical and public acclaim. Though it struggled with deficits during its first several years, the symphony has enjoyed budget surpluses every year since the 1997-98 season.

由于 OEBS 具有明确、引人注目的使命（包括现实的愿望）和合理的战略，因此，它享有一定程度的财务繁荣并获得评论界和公众的赞誉也就不足为奇了。虽然交响乐团在成立的头几年里一直在赤字中挣扎，但自 1997-98 乐季以来，每年都有预算盈余。



Thanks to grants from the James Irvine Foundation, the OEBS has been able to commission a number of original works by local and California composers. The orchestra received critical acclaim for its 2002 opening night performance of “Holy the Firm – Essay for Cello and Orchestra” by composer Jake Heggie, who also composed the well-reviewed opera “Dead Man Walking.” The second concert of the season featured the world premiere of Sosa’s “From Our Mother,” a work for jazz ensemble and orchestra that weaves together jazz, hip-hop, and North African influences.

在詹姆斯欧文基金会（James Irvine Foundation）的资助下，OEBS 能够委托当地和加州作曲家创作许多原创作品。2002 年，乐团在开幕之夜演出了作曲家杰克·海吉 （Jake Heggie）的 “Holy the Firm - Essay for Cello and Orchestra”，获得了好评。音乐季的第二场音乐会是索萨的 “来自我们的母亲 ”的世界首演，这是爵士乐团和管弦乐队的作品，融合了爵士乐、嘻哈音乐和北非音乐的影响。



Player positions remain part time, and many musicians work for one or more other small symphonies in the area, calling themselves members of the “Freeway Philharmonic.” The OEBS has made significant concessions to musicians as well. For one thing, a player representative sits on the OEBS board with full voting rights – something the old symphony resisted strongly. The OEBS has also made a point of letting musicians inspect its books, so they know exactly what its financial condition is. An eleventh-hour standoff with musicians helped sink the old orchestra, but OEBS managing director Jennifer Duston said the new one has generally good relations with the musicians union.

演奏员职位仍然是兼职的，许多音乐家为该地区的一个或多个其他小型交响乐团工作，自称为 “高速公路爱乐乐团 ”的成员。OEBS 对音乐家也做出了很大让步。首先，一名乐手代表进入了 OEBS 董事会，并拥有完全的投票权——这是旧交响乐团强烈抵制的。此外，OEBS 还让音乐家查阅账目，让他们清楚地了解乐团的财务状况。与音乐家在最后一刻的僵持曾让老交响乐团一蹶不振，但 OEBS 总经理珍妮弗·达斯顿（Jennifer Duston）表示，新交响乐团与音乐家工会的关系总体良好。



The OEBS struggled hard to win back the trust of the community following the high-profile failure of the Oakland Symphony and the disappointment and skepticism it engendered. But with imaginative programming, extensive outreach, and disciplined financial management, the OEBS seems to have established itself as a fixture in the artistic landscape of Oakland and the East Bay region.

在奥克兰交响乐团的高调失败以及由此引发的失望和怀疑之后，OEBS 努力争取重新赢得社区的信任。但是，凭借富有想象力的节目编排、广泛的外联活动和严谨的财务管理，OEBS 似乎已成为奥克兰和东湾地区艺术景观中的一个固定组成部分。



But recognizing the dynamic nature of competition and having learned the lesson that strategy is something distinct from – and just as essential as – mission, Duston cautions that the new symphony still faces hurdles. “It’s still a difficult industry,” she said. “We worry about creating an audience for the future. We worry about our musicians. … We’re very worried about the future viability of what we do.”

但达斯顿认识到竞争的动态性，并吸取了战略有别于使命——但与使命同样重要——的教训，他提醒说，新交响乐团仍然面临着障碍。“这仍然是一个困难重重的行业，"她说。“我们担心的是如何为未来创造听众。我们担心我们的音乐家。......我们非常担心未来的生存能力。
