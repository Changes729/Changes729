![farmer harvesting coffee beans](https://ssir.org/images/jcogs_img/cache/enterprise-ownership-global-south-592x333_-_abcdef_-_ec76adfb59d23efa05b4d7ecdd5601e6950c9f26.webp)

> （图片来源：iStock/andresr）

The current global economic system has failed to foster a livable society for all. Today’s capitalist model largely prioritizes the interests of external investors enabling exploitative models that have widened wealth gaps and degraded our natural ecosystem. Even where there is overall economic growth, continued concentration of ownership prevents ordinary working people, and marginalized communities in particular, from reaping the benefits of their contributions, reinforcing power imbalances and social inequalities.

当前的全球经济体系未能为所有人促成一个适宜生活的社会。今天的资本主义模式在很大程度上优先考虑外部投资者的利益，促成了一种贫富差距扩大、 自然生态系统退化的剥削模式。即使在总体经济增长的地方，所有权的持续集中也使普通劳动人民，特别是边缘化的社区无法从他们的贡献中获益，加剧了权力的不平衡和社会的不平等。



Transform Finance’s recent *SSIR* article [“Investing in Enterprises That Work for Everyone”](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/investing_in_enterprises_that_work_for_everyone) and accompanying report threw a much-needed spotlight on how Alternative Ownership Enterprise (AOE) models can help redistribute economic value, looking at the landscape in the United States. These business models rebalance economic (and often governance) rights away from outside investors to other stakeholders, such as workers and producers who drive value creation in the business. By shifting power in this way, AOEs aim to create a more equitable distribution of wealth and foster inclusive economic growth.

名为财务转型（Transform Finance）的团队最近在 SSIR 投稿的文章 [“投资给那些为每个人而运作的企业”](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/investing_in_enterprises_that_work_for_everyone) 及其附属报告，关注了非传统所有制企业模式（Alternative Ownership Enterprise, AOE）在如今的美国社会现状之下如何助力经济价值的重新分配。这些商业模式将那些外部投资者的经济权利（通常是治理权）重新转移到一些利益相关者上，如推动企业价值创造的工人和生产者们。非传统所有制企业（AOE）期望通过以这种方式转移权力，创造更公平的财富分配并促进包容性经济的发展。



#### The Global South

#### 全球南方

We should not assume that the potential of these models is confined to the United States and other advanced economies. In fact, the Global South is where some of the most stunning, large-scale success stories in alternative ownership have played out, particularly in the agri-food sector. Some of these span multiple decades, others are new enterprises that have been set up with alternative ownership at the core. Indeed, AOE models across the Global South have proven successful in shifting entire sectors toward greater inclusion, particularly of smallholder farmers and other groups that have been historically marginalized.

我们不应假定这些模式仅能在美国和其他发达经济体中发挥潜力。事实上，非传统所有制正在全球南方出现了许多大规模、令人惊叹的成功案例，特别是在农业食品部门。有些案例具有十多年的跨度，还有以非传统所有制为核心建立的新型企业。事实上，横跨全球南方的经验已经成功证明非传统所有制企业模式（AOE）可以将整个行业发展得更具包容性，特别是小农和其他历史上被边缘化的群体。



In Kenya, many decades of colonial exclusion of smallholder tea growers were reversed with the creation in the 1960s of what would become the [Kenya Tea Development Agency](https://ktdateas.com/about-us/) (KTDA), the world’s second-largest tea exporter and one of Kenya’s top foreign exchange earners, owned by over 600,000 tea growers. This enabled native Kenyan smallholders to move from being dependent laborers working on plantations owned by large colonial enterprises to becoming masters of their own destiny and reaping the rewards appropriately. Research suggests that the growth and success of KTDA over the decades has provided a strong boost to rural economic and social development in Kenya, complementing the role played by more inclusive banks (such as Equity Bank).

在肯尼亚，几十年来对小茶农的殖民排斥随着1960年代[肯尼亚茶叶发展署](https://ktdateas.com/about-us/)（KTDA）的成立而扭转，该署由60多万茶农拥有，是世界第二大茶叶出口商，也是肯尼亚最大的外汇收入来源之一。这使得原本在大型殖民企业所有的种植园工作的肯尼亚本土小农的身份发生了转变——不仅从受制于人的劳动者转变成为自己命运的主人，而且还能取得适当的回报。研究表明，肯尼亚茶叶发展署 （KTDA）几十年来的发展和成功为肯尼亚的农村经济和社会发展提供了强劲的推动力，补充了普惠银行（例如 Equity Bank）所发挥的作用。



![fields of tea](https://ssir.org/images/jcogs_img/cache/kenya-tea-farm-592x444_-_abcdef_-_ec76adfb59d23efa05b4d7ecdd5601e6950c9f26.webp)

Tea smallholdings that supply KTDA factories in Kenya. (Photo courtesy of Harvey Koh)
为肯尼亚茶叶发展署工厂提供茶叶的肯尼亚小型茶园。（图像来源：Harvey Koh）



Meanwhile, one of India’s top consumer food brands, Amul, belongs to the Gujarat Cooperative Milk Marketing Federation (GCMMF). From humble beginnings as a single cooperative in newly independent India in the 1940s, GCMMF has become [India’s largest dairy processor](https://amul.com/m/about-us) with revenues of over $4 billion in 2016. Unlike in typical agricultural value chains, this has brought substantial rewards in terms of higher and more stable prices, and profit distributions, for the 3.6 million dairy farmers who own the business.
与此同时，印度最大的消费食品品牌之一 Amul，其所属的古吉拉特邦合作牛奶营销联合会（GCMMF） 从20世纪40年代在新独立的印度以一个单独的合作社开始，如今已经成为 [印度最大的乳制品加工商](https://amul.com/m/about-us)，2016年的收入超过40亿美元。与典型的农业价值链不同，更高、更稳定的价格和利润分配为合作社内的360万奶牛养殖户提供了丰厚回报。



In Colombia, Promotora de Cafe Colombia (Procafecol), the company behind the iconic Juan Valdez brand, has turned a highly fragmented coffee sector into an international export success story benefiting the 95 percent of coffee growers that are smallholder farmers. Set up in 2002 by the National Federation of Coffee Growers of Colombia (FNC) to promote Colombian coffee, Procafecol has expanded the scope of Colombia’s coffee industry from commoditized beans to value-added products sold in domestic and international markets. The company is over 90 percent owned by the FNC, which itself is owned by more than 500,000 smallholder farmers. This structure has implications for the company’s governance, which combines a professional management team with a board in which two of five seats are allocated to FNC farmer representatives. <u>It also implements better market practices that reflect those encouraged by the FNC, including fair trade practices, technical assistance to farmers, and an enhanced focus on youth and female farmers.</u>
在哥伦比亚，标志性品牌胡安·巴尔德斯（ Juan Valdez）背后的哥伦比亚咖啡推介所（Promotora de Cafe Colombia，Procafecol），将高度分散的咖啡行业转向国际出口的成功案例使 95% 的咖啡种植者受益。哥伦比亚咖啡推介所（Procafecol）是哥伦比亚全国咖啡种植者联合会 (FNC) 于2002年为推广哥伦比亚咖啡而成立的，它将哥伦比亚咖啡业的范围从商品化咖啡豆扩展到在国内和国际市场销售的增值产品。该公司（Procafecol）90%以上的股份由哥伦比亚全国咖啡种植者联合会 (FNC) 持有，而 FNC 本身由50多万小农户拥有。这种结构影响了公司的治理方式——将专业管理团队与董事会相结合，而董事会的五个席位中有两个席位会分配给 FNC 的农民代表。<u>该组织还推行了更完善的市场规范，这些规范体现出 FNC 所倡导的理念，具体包括：实施公平贸易准则、为农户提供技术援助，并着重加强对青年农民及女性农业从业者的扶持力度。</u>



Similar efforts to advance these ideas continue today driven by individual enterprises, philanthropy, and government. [Babban Gona](https://ssir.org/podcasts/entry/from_plow_to_prosperity) is a Nigerian business that has [more than doubled earnings for some 70,000 smallholders](https://search.issuelab.org/resource/bending-the-arc-how-the-full-spectrum-of-capital-can-enable-inclusive-growth-in-agriculture.html) involved in maize, rice, and soybean production. In 2015, Babban Gona gave those smallholders a 30 percent ownership stake in the company through a Farmer Trust (each Trust Group comprises of three to five farmers with a leader who receives business and agronomy training). A farmer representative now sits permanently on the company’s board with full voting rights, including the ability to veto any changes that take Babban Gona away from its inclusive and equitable agribusiness model.
今天，在个体企业、慈善机构和政府的推动下，推进这些理念的类似努力仍在继续。[Babban Gona](https://ssir.org/podcasts/entry/from_plow_to_prosperity) 是一家尼日利亚企业，[为大约70000名](https://search.issuelab.org/resource/bending-the-arc-how-the-full-spectrum-of-capital-can-enable-inclusive-growth-in-agriculture.html)从事玉米、水稻和大豆生产的小农使收入翻了一翻多。2015年，Babban Gona 通过农民信托基金向这些小农户提供了公司 30% 的所有权股份（每个信托基金由三到五名农民组成，并有一名接受商业和农学培训的领导者）。现在，一位农民代表永久性地成为公司的董事会成员，拥有否决任何使 Babban Gona 偏离其包容性和公平性农业模式的变更在内的全部投票权。



In Rwanda, 5,000 smallholders are now 100 percent owners of the country’s largest tea factory in Mulindi, supported since 2012 by the Wood Foundation and Gatsby Africa who have invested in factory modernization, field expansion with high-yielding clones, upskilling farmers and local management, providing corporate governance support, <u>and diversifying the end-market base into premium markets</u>. Within a decade, this work led to greenleaf production growing by a third and a doubling in farmer gross incomes, including $3.1 million in bonuses paid over and above their greenleaf income.
在卢旺达，该国最大的茶叶厂 Mulindi 的 100% 所有者现在是 5000 名小农，自2012年以来，伍德基金会（Wood Foundation）和 Gatsby Africa 一直在投资工厂的现代化，通过高产无性系作物扩大种植，提高农民和当地管理人员的技能，提供公司治理支持，<u>并将基础的终端市场扩大业务至高端市场</u>。在十年内，这项工作使叶子产量增长了三分之一，农民的总收入增加了一倍，其中包括310万美元的奖金，超过了他们叶子的收入。



However, we must also note recent efforts that failed to get off the ground, such as two (in Southeast Asia and Latin America) that the authors advised on. In Southeast Asia, an “inclusive economic zone” scheme was proposed that would attract private investment into the country’s poorest regions on condition that workers were given substantial ownership stakes. Despite positive early soundings with investors, the initiative foundered when the minister championing this idea abruptly resigned from his post. In the Latin American case, several regional conglomerates championed a model for community ownership of renewable energy assets in off-grid zones. Although a version of the initiative has progressed, the focus shifted toward a more traditional public-private partnership as the donors involved were uncomfortable with deploying grant funding outside of their existing models and with the perceived risks of community groups lacking business experience.
然而，我们也必须提到一些近期未能取得进展的努力，例如两项作者提供的内容——分别在东南亚和拉丁美洲。在东南亚，提出了一项“包容性经济区”计划，该计划将吸引私人投资进入该国最贫困地区，条件是工人们要被给予大量的所有权股份。尽管投资者的初步反应是积极的，但当支持这一想法的部长突然辞职时，这计划就随之失败了。拉丁美洲的案例，几家地区性企业集团倡导一种在离网地区（off-grid zones）由社区拥有可再生能源资产的模式。虽然该倡议的一个版本已经取得了进展，但关注点转向了更传统的公私合作伙伴关系，因为相关的捐赠者对不按照现有模式分发赠款资金感到不安，并且认为社区团体因缺乏商业经验而存在风险。



## Key Lessons Learned 

## 主要经验教训

These stories powerfully demonstrate the potential of AOE models across the Global South, where they can transform entire sectors for greater inclusion and stronger economic success, while also highlighting some challenges. From these we can draw some key lessons:
这些故事有力地展示了非传统所有制企业模式（AOE）在全球南方的潜力，它们可以影响整个行业，以实现更大的包容性和更大的经济成就，同时也突出了一些挑战。从中我们可以得出一些重要的教训：



**1. Strong business acumen and professional management teams are drivers of success.** An alternative ownership enterprise success story is, first and foremost, a business success story. KTDA and GCMMF both have strong track records of R&D, manufacturing, logistics, and marketing innovation that have enabled them to excel in the marketplace.
**1. 敏锐的商业智慧和专业的管理团队是成功的驱动力。**一个非传统所有制企业（AOE）的成功故事，首先是一个成功的商业故事。肯尼亚茶叶发展署（KTDA）和古吉拉特邦合作牛奶营销联合会（GCMMF）在研发、制造、物流和营销创新方面都有着良好的业绩记录，使他们能够在市场上脱颖而出。



At a pivotal point in GCMMF’s formative years, a bright young manager named Verghese Kurien (later hailed as the “milkman of India”) arrived and went on to lead the federation through decades of innovation and growth. At the beginning of the KTDA journey, colonial tea companies were contracted to provide management and—crucially—train native Kenyan talent in the first decade of operations, making up for exclusion from management in the colonial era. This stands in contrast to the experience in Kenyan smallholder coffee and Tanzanian smallholder tea efforts of the same era that failed in large part due to the absence of competent, professional management (though this is not the whole story—see #5 below).
在古吉拉特邦合作牛奶营销联合会（GCMMF）发展前期有一个关键时刻，一位名叫 Verghese Kurien（后来被誉名为“印度乳业之父”）的年轻经理来到了这里，并带领联合会经历了数十年的创新与发展壮大。在肯尼亚茶叶发展署（KTDA）故事的最开始，他们与殖民的茶叶公司达成的协议弥补了殖民时代被排除在管理层之外的情况——不仅殖民公司需要在运营的第一个十年内提供管理，还要培训肯尼亚的本土人才。这与同一时期很大程度上由于缺乏称职且专业的管理而失败的肯尼亚小农咖啡和坦桑尼亚小农茶叶的案例形成鲜明对比（尽管这不是故事的全部-见下文第5条）。



**2. <u>The alternative ownership structure should reinforce the key business drivers of success, as well as tie it back to intended impact**</u>. Giving substantial ownership to smallholder growers entrenches the impact purpose of these inclusive agribusinesses, but what is less obvious is that these mechanisms can also help the business outperform.
**2. <u>非传统所有制结构应强化成功的关键业务驱动因素，并将其与预期影响联系起来。</u>**给予小农种植者实质性的所有权，确立了这些包容性农业综合企业的影响力目标，但不太明显的是，这些机制也可以帮助企业更加出色。



In the tea sector for instance, grower practices in tea husbandry and picking are critical to producing high-quality tea that fetches higher market prices, thus creating a strong linkage and powerful feedback loop for the smallholder grower-owners of KTDA. This is heightened by the fact that growers actually own shares in their local tea factory (which in turn own KTDA as a whole), and that each factory sells its finished product separately at the Mombasa export auctions, with weekly reports on the tea prices achieved by all factories then circulated to all grower-owners. This creates a strong feedback loop between local quality production and local financial rewards.
以茶叶行业为例，种植者在茶叶养殖和采摘方面的做法对于生产高质量茶叶、获得更高的市场价格是至关重要的，因此肯尼亚茶叶发展署（KTDA）的作物所有者们（grower-owners）建立了一个强有力的联系与有效的反馈机制。更重要的是，种植者实际上拥有当地茶厂的股份（该茶厂反过来又拥有整个肯尼亚茶叶发展署），而且每个工厂在蒙巴萨出口拍卖会上单独销售其成品，每周报告所有工厂达到的茶叶价格然后分发给所有作物所有者。这就在本地优质产品和本地经济收益之间形成了一个强有力的反馈循环。



Likewise, GCMMF and Procafecol were able to achieve in their structures a combination of local dynamism in production and scale advantage in marketing. For example, in the case of Procafecol, the winning strategy was to balance a strong focus on enhancing local production quality (by equipping farmers with technology to maximize output) with a simultaneous expansion into international markets. Competition and performance are just as essential to businesses of this kind as they are to all other businesses.
同样，古吉拉特邦合作牛奶营销联合会（GCMMF）和哥伦比亚咖啡推介所（Procafecol）能够在其结构中实现当地生产活力与营销规模优势的结合。例如，哥伦比亚咖啡推介所（Procafecol）公司的战略成功是在大力提高当地生产质量的同时（通过使农民掌握技术以最大限度地提高产量）与拓展国际市场之间取得平衡。竞争和绩效对这类企业与其他所有企业一样重要。



**3. Access to flexible capital at the right points is key to supporting the AOE model.** At Babban Gona, concessional debt from donor-funded lenders had been instrumental in growing their smallholder base fivefold between 2013 and 2015, without excessive pressure for returns. This then set the stage for the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation to provide $4 million grant in 2015 that funded the issue of shares to smallholders, giving them a 30 percent ownership stake in the business.
**3. 在正确的节点获取灵活资本是支持 AOE 模式的关键。**在 Babban Gona，在2013年至2015年期间，来自捐助者资助的放贷方优惠贷款帮助其小农基地扩大了五倍，而且没有过度的回报压力。这为比尔·梅林达·盖茨基金会在2015年提供400万美元的赠款奠定了基础，这些赠款为向小农发行的股票提供了资金，使他们获得了该企业30%的所有权。



<u>At KTDA, patient capital investments from the World Bank and CDC (now British International Investment) were key to building a smallholder-centric steward ownership model from inception, with the transition to grower ownership in 2000 through the issuance of shares to growers as part of an inclusive privatization process.</u>
<u>在 KTDA，世界银行和 CDC（现为英国国际投资公司）的耐心资本投资从一开始就对建立以小农为中心的管家式所有权模式起到了关键作用，2000 年，作为包容性私有化进程的一部分，通过向种植者发行股票过渡到种植者所有权。</u>



Meanwhile, Procafecol was able to leverage ongoing state funding derived from a small export tax on Colombian coffee to establish and maintain the enterprise in its early years. Additionally, an investment from the International Finance Corporation helped the company launch its coffeehouse retail outlets in Colombia.
与此同时，哥伦比亚咖啡推介所（Procafecol）能够在早期利用从哥伦比亚咖啡的小额出口税中获得持续的国家资金来建立和维持企业。此外，国际金融公司的投资帮助该公司在哥伦比亚推出了咖啡零售店。



**4. <u>Historically, game-changing moves have been supported by larger societal shifts and state intervention</u>**. Our emphasis on the business aspects of success should not obscure the reality that these big wins were hard-fought in the face of unfavorable established norms and power structures. Social movements and political leadership often assist with success and effectively reset the rules of the game.
**4. <u>从历史上看，改变游戏规则的举措都得到了更大的社会变革和国家干预的支持</u>。**我们对商业成功的强调不应该掩盖这样一个现实，即这些重大的胜利是在不利的既定规范与权力结构面前艰难争取到的。社会运动和政治领导往往有助于成功，并有效地重新设定游戏规则。



For example, Jomo Kenyatta’s “Africanisation” agenda in post-independence Kenya (in which KTDA begins its journey) was an uphill struggle in a national and global economy under continued colonial domination. In KTDA’s first decade, native Kenyans were brought in to train as new managers, but they endured racist attitudes and vitriol from their superiors even as they did so.
例如，在独立后的肯尼亚（即 KTDA 创立的时候），乔莫·肯雅塔（Jomo Kenyatta）的“非洲化”议程是一场艰难的斗争——尽管国家已独立，但国内与国际经济仍受殖民势力影响。”。在 KTDA 的第一个十年里，当地的肯尼亚人被培训成为新的管理者，即便如此，他们也要忍受上级的种族主义态度和尖酸刻薄的言论。



GCMMF’s success has also clearly been aided by state intervention, such as the ban on butter imports in the 1960s following a currency crisis, effectively a form of infant industry protection that allowed Amul to gain an initial foothold in the butter market in India. And inclusion in the GCMMF model gradually widened to encompass the lower Hindu castes and minority religious groups over time, in line with shifting societal norms.
古吉拉特邦合作牛奶营销联合会（GCMMF）的成功显然也得益于国家干预，例如在20世纪60年代货币危机后禁止黄油进口，这实际上是一种幼稚产业保护政策，使 Amul 在印度黄油市场获得初步立足点。随着时间的推移，古吉拉特邦合作牛奶营销联合会（GCMMF）模式中的包容性逐渐扩大到包含较低的印度教种姓和少数宗教团体，这是与不断变化的社会规范相一致的。



It should be noted, though, that championing by a single political leader without a wider base of support carries the risk of disruption due to leadership reshuffling and may also weaken the overall likelihood of success.
不过，应当指出的是，在没有广泛支持基础的情况下，单一政治领导人的支持有可能因领导层改组而出现动荡，也会在总体上削弱成功的可能性。



**5. While strong political support can help, direct state control and interference have been a recipe for failure.** It is always worth asking: Which efforts failed and why? As mentioned above, the Kenyan smallholder coffee and Tanzanian smallholder tea initiatives failed even as KTDA thrived. These stories were characterised not only by lack of management competence (as described above) but by high levels of unhelpful political interference, and these factors were very much related: Governments are generally not well-placed to directly manage businesses. In Kenyan coffee cooperatives, the government appointed managers for political, not business, reasons. Direct administration by government bodies also meant that farmer payments were delayed and investments stalled whenever there was pressure on public-sector finances. A World Bank report on Tanzanian tea from 1983, when the sector was near its (modest) peak, recounts a litany of problems, including non-replacement of broken machinery, power failures, feeder roads left in disrepair, inadequate use of inputs and new seed varieties, and even likely falsification of payment records.

**5. 虽然强有力的政治支持可能有所帮助，但直接的国家控制和干预通常就是失败的原因。** 我们应该经常思考——有哪些实践努力失败了，并且为什么失败了？如上所述，尽管肯尼亚茶叶发展署（KTDA）蓬勃发展着，但肯尼亚小农户咖啡和坦桑尼亚小农户茶叶倡议失败了。这些案例的特征不仅是缺乏管理能力（如上所述），还有高度无用的政治干预，这些因素是密切相关的：政府通常不适合直接管理企业。在肯尼亚的咖啡合作社，政府任命经理是出于政治原因，而不是商业原因。政府机构的直接管理也意味着，每当公共部门财政面临压力时，农民薪资就会延后，投资就会停滞。世界银行1983年在坦桑尼亚茶叶接近其（适度）峰值时提供了一份报告，叙述了一系列问题，包括不更换损坏的机器，电力故障，支线公路年久失修，投入和新种子品种的使用不足，甚至可能伪造付款记录。

## Recommendations 建议

AOEs have the potential to enable economic development in a more inclusive and equitable way across the Global South. We see a tremendous role for philanthropy, impact investors, international development actors, and governments to drive these potentially transformative changes. Here are a few ideas for what could be done:
非传统所有制企业（AOE）有潜力在全球南方以更具包容性和公平的方式实现经济发展。我们看到慈善事业、影响力投资者、国际发展参与者和政府在推动这些潜在的变革方面发挥着巨大作用。以下是一些关于可以做些什么的想法：



1. **Consider the potential for AOEs (e.g., worker ownership, grower ownership) when embarking on enterprise-led economic inclusion initiatives.** For instance, instead of focusing only on creating jobs for local communities, initiatives could give local workers a stake in the businesses they contribute so much to. As shown in the cases described above, and in line from research evidence in the Global North, AOE structures can actually *strengthen* business resilience and performance, while deepening economic inclusion and equity impacts.
   **在开展以企业为主导的经济包容倡议时考虑非传统所有制企业（AOE）的可能性（例如，工人所有权、种植者所有权）。**例如，与其只关注为当地社区创造就业机会，还不如让当地工人在他们为之做出巨大贡献的企业中占有一席之地。正如上述案例所示，以及全球北方的研究证据表明，非传统所有制企业（AOE）结构实际上可以加强企业的弹性和绩效，同时深化经济包容性和公平影响。
2. **Tailor investment instruments and grant mechanisms to achieve this.** Appropriate investment can help build and grow more AOEs, and flexible and patient [catalytic capital](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/impact-investing-catalytic-capital-myths) could be especially helpful where there are new models, markets, or geographies involved, allowing them to tolerate high risk and ensure that impact aspects are prioritized. There may also be scope to support conversion of mature businesses to AOE models facilitated by specialized investment funds such as is being done in the United States, which could also simultaneously address the problem of limited exit routes for business owners in much of the Global South. Meanwhile, philanthropic grants could directly support the issuance of shares to workers and communities where appropriate, as we see in the example of BMGF and Babban Gona. This requires going beyond traditional grantmaking models to develop creative solutions that can support AOE models while responding to funder requirements. Funders with more experience in these areas should share their learning with others so that the field can advance more broadly.
   **定制化设计投资工具和拨款机制以实现这一目标**。适当的投资可以帮助建立和发展更多的非传统所有制企业（AOE），而灵活和耐心的[催化资本](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/impact-investing-catalytic-capital-myths)在涉及新模式、市场或地理位置时尤其有用，能够使他们承受高风险，并确保优先考虑影响力层面。专业投资基金可能有能力推动成熟企业向非传统所有制企业（AOE）模式的转变，如美国正在做的那样，这也可以同时解决全球南方许多企业家退出路径有限的问题。与此同时，慈善赠款可以在适当情况下直接支持向工人和社区发行股票，正如我们在比尔及梅琳达·盖茨基金会和Babban Gona的例子中看到的那样。这就需要超越传统的资助模式，开发出能够支持 AOE 模式的创造性解决方案，同时满足资助者的要求。在这些领域有更多经验的资助者应该与其他人分享他们的学习，以便该领域能够更广泛地发展。
3. **Support the development of effective management and governance to enable business success, bringing in new people and fostering new capabilities where needed.** While the ownership model itself is crucial, effective business management is essential to enabling the level of scale necessary to ensure long-term success. Experienced professional management with strong business acumen is needed to help AOEs (like any other business) navigate complex business environments, scale operations, and achieve sustainable growth. Where such management talent exists within AOEs, they should be empowered; where they are absent, they need to be brought in so they can lead as well as nurture future cohorts of management. Specific capacity building may also be needed to support needs ranging from management skills at different levels, to effective governance mechanisms and structures. When tailored to the specific needs of an enterprise, this support can be critical in accelerating the success of AOEs, as seen most recently in the case of the Rwandan tea factory supported by the Wood Foundation and Gatsby Africa.
   **支持有效管理和治理的发展，以实现业务成功，在需要的地方引入新人并培养新的能力。**虽然所有权模式本身至关重要，但有效的业务管理对于确保实现长期成功所需的规模水平至关重要。需要经验丰富的专业管理人员和强大的商业头脑来帮助AOE（像任何其他企业一样）驾驭复杂的商业环境，扩大运营规模并实现可持续增长。如果AOE中存在这样的管理人才，就应该赋予他们权力;如果没有，就需要引入外部人才，这样他们就可以领导和培养未来的管理团队。还可能需要进行具体的能力建设，以支持从不同级别的管理技能到有效的治理机制和结构等各种需求。如果根据企业的具体需要加以调整，这种支持对于加速AOE的成功至关重要，最近伍德基金会和盖茨比非洲公司支持的卢旺达茶厂就是一个例子。
4. **Collaborate with governments and other societal stakeholders to embed inclusivity in the “rules of the game” where possible.** Strong political leadership, supportive policies, and a conducive economic environment have been instrumental in the large-scale success stories. Collaboration with governments can help secure favorable regulatory frameworks, access to necessary resources, and protection against external threats. Additionally, engaging with societal stakeholders, including local communities and civil society organizations, can ensure that AOEs are aligned with broader social and economic goals, thereby enhancing their legitimacy and impact. Building a wider base of support for initiatives, rather than relying on an individual champion to push things through, could be very helpful not only in getting efforts started but also in fostering the conditions for this kind of systemic change to succeed.
   **与政府和其他社会利益相关方合作，尽可能将包容性纳入“游戏规则”。**强有力的政治领导、支持性政策和有利的经济环境在取得大规模成功方面发挥了重要作用。与政府合作有助于确保有利的监管框架，获得必要的资源，保护以免受外部威胁。此外，与包括当地社区和民间社会组织在内的社会利益相关方接触，可以确保AOE与更广泛的社会和经济目标保持一致，从而增强其合法性和影响力。为各项举措建立更广泛的支持基础，而不是依靠个人倡导者来推动工作，不仅有助于开始努力，而且有助于为这种系统性变革的成功创造条件。

These stories from the Global South demonstrate that achieving business growth and commercial success while centering inclusion and equity is possible. Alternative ownership enterprises have already proven that they can be successful at scale, and it is not a question of whether but rather where and when we should be enabling the next wave of transformative AOEs, with the right talent, support, and investment. We look forward to working with others to advance this work across the Global South in the months and years to come.

这些来自全球南方的故事表明，在实现业务增长和商业成功的同时，以包容和公平为中心是可能的。非传统所有制企业已经证明，它们可以大规模取得成功，问题不在于我们是否应该去做，而在于我们应该在何时何地，在合适的人才、支持和投资的情况下，促成下一波 AOE 转型的出现。我们期待着与其他人合作，在未来的岁月里在全球南方推进这项工作。
